# Graduate Scheme 2026

Recruit the 2026 intake of the Pellmore graduate scheme (two-year rotations across operations, finance and commercial). [[marek-zielinski]] owned it; [[callum-rourke]] and I ran the coordination: online tests, video interviews, two assessment centres and offers.

## Outcome

Reached on 2026-03-27: 18 offers made from 1,340 applications through two assessment centres; 16 accepted for a 2026-09-07 start.

## Current State

- Completed. 16 graduates started on 2026-09-07, alongside [[carys-whitlock]] as the HR apprentice.
- Lessons are written up in [[graduate-scheme-2026-lessons-learned]]; the 2027 round opens on 2026-10-05 with Callum leading.
- The talent pool of 26 strong unsuccessful candidates is held for 12 months with their consent, then deleted ([[how-long-do-we-keep-unsuccessful-candidate-data]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Attraction was Internal Communications' and Marek's; I started at the application stage. Placement and rotations belong to Learning and Development. No applicant is named anywhere in these notes; the funnel is in counts only.

## Working Context

Funnel ([[graduate-scheme-2026-applications-summary]]): 1,340 applications, 412 passed the online tests, 96 video interviews, 44 invited to assessment centres (22 each), 18 offers, 16 acceptances. One offer was declined for another employer and one for further study.

The first assessment centre (2026-01-28, [[graduate-assessment-centre-2026-01-28]]) was online; the second (2026-02-25, [[graduate-assessment-centre-2026-02-25]]) was in person at Head Office. We ran both formats deliberately to compare them ([[do-assessment-centres-need-to-be-in-person]]). Exercises and scoring followed [[structured-interviewing]]: the same competency questions and anchored rating scales for every candidate, with two assessors per exercise ([[graduate-assessment-centre-pack-2026]]).

# Hybrid Manager Guide

The guide for line managers of hybrid-eligible staff, written by [[priyanka-desai]]. Version 1 published 2026-03-25; version 2 on 2026-08-24 for [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]].

## Summary

Version 2 covers: agreeing the team's two chosen days with the team and recording them in [[talvento]]; checking the 95% per-floor desk cap with Facilities before confirming Head Office days; applying the pro rata rule for part-time and compressed-hours staff; when to involve the HR Business Partner (any request for an exception or an adjustment); and what the quarterly attendance report will and will not show.

## Why It Matters

The FAQ points managers here. Most "my manager says" tickets are resolved by linking the right section.

## Caveats

The attendance section says "aggregated by team" without the minimum group of 10; to be corrected once [[how-should-anchor-day-attendance-be-reported]] is settled.

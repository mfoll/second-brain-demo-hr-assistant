# Talvento Go-Live 2026-06-29

Core HR, organisation structure, absence and self-service went live in [[talvento]] on Monday 2026-06-29, three months after the original date.

- Cutover weekend 2026-06-26 to 2026-06-28: Staffora frozen Friday 17:00, final migration Saturday, reconciliation counts Sunday ([[go-live-weekend-notes-2026-06]]).
- Record counts matched: 4,096 active employees in both systems on the Sunday evening (four leavers processed on the Friday were held back by design and keyed on the Monday).
- From Monday, Talvento is the master; changes are keyed in Talvento and then in Staffora for payroll until cutover.
- Hireloom starts handing new starters to Talvento; anyone starting before 2026-06-29 was entered in Staffora and migrated.
- First week: 214 Deskwell tickets tagged Talvento, mostly password and "where is my leave balance"; down to 41 by the third week.
- Absence mismatch for June rose to 2.4% because of the freeze ([[absence-data-reconciliation]]).

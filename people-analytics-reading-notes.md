# People Analytics Reading Notes

Notes from reading around people analytics in 2026, mostly in the evenings. Not a project; a place to put thoughts until one appears.

- The field splits into reporting (what happened), analysis (why) and prediction (what will happen to whom). Pellmore does the first, a little of the second, and none of the third ([[sarmiento-orna-2026-ai-and-data-analytics-in-talent-management]]).
- Most attrition prediction papers use the same public datasets. High accuracy there says little about a hub with seasonal staff ([[pavithran-2025-explainable-attrition-risk-scoring]]).
- What makes analytics acceptable to employees seems to be governance: seeing how a result was reached and a human deciding ([[patnaik-2026-governing-algorithms-in-ai-enabled-hrm]]).
- Clean data comes first. Before 2026 our leaver reasons were mostly "other". That alone would sink any model ([[hr-data-quality]]).
- The survey threshold decision is the same principle applied to reporting: no group small enough to identify someone ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]], [[data-minimisation]]).

Possible material for diploma Unit 6 (People Data and Analytics, due 2027-01-29); not for the research project, which uses ticket counts only ([[diploma-research-project]]).

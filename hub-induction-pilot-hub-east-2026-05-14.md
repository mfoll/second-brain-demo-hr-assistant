# Hub Induction Pilot Hub East 2026-05-14

The first standard [[hub-induction-day]], run at Hub East with [[maeve-donnelly]] as host.

## What happened

- 22 direct starters, all for permanent operative roles.
- Hub trainer, [[ewan-galbraith]]'s safety trainer, a team leader, and me for the systems and pay slot.
- The paperwork slot took 75 minutes instead of 30: starters filled the payroll form on paper and several did not have bank details with them. The day finished at 16:20 instead of 15:30.
- The safety walk-round with 22 in one group was too large to hear; split into two after lunch.

## Outcome

Average feedback 4.0 out of 5 from 20 forms ([[hub-induction-pilot-feedback-hub-east]]). Maeve sent seven changes that evening; five were adopted the following week.

## Decisions

- [[deborah-okwuosa]] agreed on 2026-05-18 to roll out the standard day to the other hubs, after Hub North and Hub South each ran one in June.
- The [[new-starter-data-form]] moves before day one; this became possible once Talvento's pre-hire portal was live.
- The cap of 40 per session, with the walk-round in two groups of up to 20, set from this pilot.

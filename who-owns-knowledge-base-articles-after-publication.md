# Who Owns Knowledge Base Articles After Publication?

Before the rebuild, articles were written by whoever had time and owned by nobody: 34 of 212 had a nameable owner.

## Current Answer

The owner of the policy or process an article describes owns the article: [[priyanka-desai]] for policy areas, Group Payroll for pay ([[elspeth-harcourt]] named, [[gemma-ashworth]] in practice), [[yetunde-balogun]] for learning, the HRIS team for system access, Leon for helpdesk process. Agreed with [[leon-adjei]] and [[deborah-okwuosa]] on 2025-12-08 and recorded in every article's owner field.

Writers (often me) draft; owners check facts and confirm at each review ([[knowledge-article-lifecycle]]).

## Gaps

- Owners leave or change roles; Leon checks the owner list each quarter.
- Owners slow to sign off: since the 2026-10-20 checkpoint Deborah chases them, not me.

# Using selection tests to hire autistic employees: An exploration of methods.

Whelpley CE, Fezzey T, Batchelor JH, Weekley JA. PLoS One, 2026.

## Summary

Compares autistic and neurotypical adults on three common selection tools: general mental ability tests, personality inventories and situational judgement tests. All three showed subgroup differences unfavourable to autistic respondents, and the situational judgement test gap remained after controlling for ability, personality, gender and age. The authors argue that tools promoted as more objective than interviews can still have adverse impact.

## Why It Matters

Relevant twice. The graduate scheme loses most applicants at the online tests (412 of 1,340 passed, [[graduate-scheme-2026-applications-summary]]), which include a situational judgement test; I have raised with Callum that the 2027 round should check the reasonable-adjustment offer at that stage. And it is a reminder for [[ai-cv-screening-pilot]] that "objective" scoring is not automatically fair.

## Caveats

Described by the authors as preliminary; a research sample, not real applicants; read from the abstract. We have no data on our own test results by disability and would need a group of 10 or more to look.

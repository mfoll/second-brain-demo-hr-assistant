# Self-Scheduling Go-Live 2025-05-12

Hireloom self-scheduling links became the default for every first interview in all divisions.

## What happened

- The setting was switched on for all open vacancies at 08:00. 63 vacancies were open; managers of 41 had already published slots after the reminder on 2025-05-07.
- Callum and I phoned the other 22 managers that morning; all but 4 had slots by the end of the day.
- First week: 118 interviews booked through links, 9 by phone fallback.
- Two problems: invites showed the wrong time zone for candidates using one calendar app (fixed by switching to standard iCalendar invites, [[rfc-5545-icalendar]]), and depot managers without laptops could not publish slots (planners now do it for them).

## Decisions

- [[marek-zielinski]] confirmed no exceptions except assessment centres and second interviews.
- Phone fallback after 48 hours without a booking stays.

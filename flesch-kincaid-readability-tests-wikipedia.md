# Flesch–Kincaid readability tests

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes two readability tests for English text: Rudolf Flesch's Reading Ease score and the Grade Level test later developed by Flesch and J. Peter Kincaid for the US Navy. Both are based on sentence length and syllables per word.

## Why It Matters

Gave the [[knowledge-base-style-guide]] a rough, checkable number: Reading Ease 60 or above. The old knowledge base averaged about 38 on a sample of 30 articles I scored; rebuilt articles average 64.

## Caveats

Long HR words (maternity, pension, probation) drag the score down without making a text hard for the reader, so owners can override the check with a reason.

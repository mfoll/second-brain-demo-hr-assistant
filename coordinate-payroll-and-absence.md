# Coordinate Payroll And Absence

Getting People Services' changes to Group Payroll on time and right: new starters, leavers, contract changes and absence, for the monthly payroll (cut-off the 15th, paid the 26th) and the weekly payroll (cut-off Thursday 12:00). Group Payroll sits under Finance and is run by [[elspeth-harcourt]]; I am their main contact in People Services for anything that arrives late or looks wrong.

## Scope

- Monthly payroll for about 1,500 salaried staff and weekly payroll for about 2,600 hourly staff (drivers, hub operatives, workshop). Dates and who sends what: [[payroll-cutoff-calendar]].
- The cut-off checklist ([[payroll-cutoff-checklist-v2]]) and late changes log ([[payroll-late-changes-log]]).
- Monthly reconciliation of absence records between the HR system and payroll ([[absence-data-reconciliation]]).
- New starter details before the first weekly cut-off, especially during peak ([[enter-new-starters-before-weekly-cutoff]]).
- Not here: pay rates, pay queries about a named person, statutory pay calculations. Those belong to Group Payroll. I log the category and route the ticket ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

## Current State

- Late changes average 17 a month since the checklist went live in April 2025, down from 63 ([[payroll-cutoff-checklist]]).
- Absence mismatch between HR and payroll: 3.8% of absence records in January 2026, 0.9% in September. Target is under 0.5% ([[absence-data-reconciliation]]).
- Peak hiring brings up to 40 new hourly starters a week until mid-November; all must be in Talvento before Thursday 12:00 of their first week ([[warehouse-peak-hiring-2026]]).

# Background check

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Explains background checks as the process of verifying that a person is who they claim to be and checking their history: identity, education, employment and sometimes criminal records. Notes that what is allowed and expected varies widely by country, industry and role.

## Why It Matters

Read when Aoife and I wrote the pre-start checklist, to be clear about what we do and do not check. Pellmore does a [[right-to-work-check]] for everyone, references for some roles, licence checks for drivers, and no general criminal record check except for roles where the HR Advisors say the law requires it. Collecting more than that would go against [[data-minimisation]].

## Caveats

Mostly about the US; legal rules are not ours to interpret. Decisions on checks come from the HR Advisors and the DPO.

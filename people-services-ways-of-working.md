# People Services Ways of Working

How People Services actually works day to day, as I see it two years in. The written rules are in the [[people-services-team-charter]]; this is the practice around them.

## Rhythm

- Monday 09:15 huddle (15 minutes, [[deborah-okwuosa]]): who has what this week, payroll dates, anything going to a committee.
- Monday 10:00 knowledge base tracker with [[leon-adjei]].
- Payroll weeks shape everything: the monthly cut-off on the 15th and the weekly one every Thursday at 12:00 ([[payroll-cutoff-calendar]]). Nobody books long meetings on Thursday mornings.
- Committees: People Committee monthly, HRIS Steering Group fortnightly; papers due to Deborah two working days before.
- Friday mornings meeting-free, mostly respected.

## What works

- Writing handovers in the ticket or tracker. Since the January 2026 away day the "I thought you had it" problem has mostly gone.
- The case pointer rule ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]): we talk about categories and counts in shared spaces and keep cases in the system.

## What does not, yet

- Peak hiring borrows [[callum-rourke]] and [[aoife-brennan-hale]] from the helpdesk every autumn, and nobody plans for it until August ([[helpdesk-peak-season-staffing-2026]]).
- Chat is still used for quick decisions that should be in a ticket.

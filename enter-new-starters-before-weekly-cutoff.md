# Enter New Starters Before Weekly Cut-Off

How to make sure every hourly new starter is complete in Talvento before Thursday 12:00 of their first week, so their first pay is not a week late. Written for peak hiring 2026 with [[aoife-brennan-hale]]; applies all year.

## When to use

Every week a weekly-paid starter begins. During peak (August to mid-November) that is up to 40 a week across the hubs ([[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]]).

## Inputs

- Talvento "Starters this week" report: pending starters handed over from [[hireloom]].
- [[group-payroll-new-starter-requirements]].
- Induction day lists from the hubs ([[hub-induction-day]]).

## Steps

1. **Monday morning.** Run "Starters this week". For each pending starter, check every mandatory field. Before 2026-06-29 this was done in Staffora; since then only in Talvento, then keyed in Staffora until payroll cutover.
2. **Monday.** Missing bank or tax details: ask the hub's induction lead to make sure the starter completes them in the Talvento pre-hire portal, at the latest on the induction day. Never by email or phone.
3. **Tuesday.** Agency-to-direct transfers: confirm the agency end date with [[chiara-lombardi-ross]]'s team so there is no double payment.
4. **Wednesday, 14:00.** Second check, with Aoife: next week's direct starters are already in Talvento, and anyone still incomplete for this week goes to the hub.
5. **Thursday, 11:00.** Final check. Complete starters keyed in Staffora (until cutover). Incomplete starters: tell [[dariusz-nowicki]] the count and expected date; they go into next week's run.
6. **Thursday, 12:00.** Cut-off. Send the "nothing else to send" confirmation.

## Guardrails

- Do not key a starter without the right to work checked flag from onboarding. That check is onboarding's, not mine.
- Bank details are entered by the starter in the pre-hire portal or self-service, never typed by us from an email.
- No starter names in this vault: the weekly count of incomplete starters is enough ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

## Verification

Friday: compare Dariusz's count of starters paid in the run with Talvento's count of starters that week. Note the gap in the [[payroll-late-changes-log]].

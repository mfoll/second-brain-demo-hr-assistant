# HRIS Steering Group Terms Of Reference

Terms of reference agreed at the first HRIS Steering Group meeting on 2025-09-03.

## Summary

- **Purpose**: govern the Talvento programme; decide scope, dates, go/no-go and payroll cutover; accept risks.
- **Chair**: [[adrian-fairclough]], Chief People Officer and programme sponsor.
- **Members**: [[fiona-mcbride]] (programme manager), [[deborah-okwuosa]], [[elspeth-harcourt]], [[rashid-karimi]], [[olumide-fashola]] for IT, [[theo-varkey]] for Finance. [[beatrix-lowe]] and [[mirela-dumitru]] attend for their items. [[kasia-wrobel]] attends for data protection items.
- **Cadence**: every two weeks, Wednesdays 10:00, 90 minutes. Papers two working days before.
- **Quorum**: chair or deputy (Deborah) plus Fiona and Elspeth for any payroll decision.
- **Secretary**: programme office; from September 2025 I keep the action log.
- **Ends**: when Staffora is decommissioned, expected 2027-03-31.

## Why It Matters

It is why payroll decisions need Elspeth in the room, which matters for the cutover vote on 2026-11-04 ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]). It is also one candidate for data governance after the programme ([[who-owns-hr-data-quality-after-go-live]]).

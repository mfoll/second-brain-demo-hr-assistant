# Payroll Cut-Off Calendar

Group Payroll's calendar of cut-off and pay dates for 2026, with the People Services responsibilities I added in 2025. Published by [[elspeth-harcourt]] each December for the following year; this is the 2026 version as updated on 2026-10-05.

## Summary

Two payrolls, two rhythms:

- **Monthly payroll** for about 1,500 salaried staff. Cut-off on the 15th at 17:00; paid on the 26th. When the 15th falls on a weekend the cut-off is the Friday before; when the 26th falls on a weekend payday is the Friday before.
- **Weekly payroll** for about 2,600 hourly staff (drivers, hub operatives, workshop). Cut-off Thursday at 12:00 for the week ending the previous Sunday; paid on the Friday of the following week.

## Key Details

Who sends what, and by when:

| What | Who in People Services | Monthly deadline | Weekly deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New starters, complete with bank details | Onboarding (Aoife, me during peak) | 15th | Thursday 12:00 of first week |
| Leavers with last day and reason | HR assistant handling the ticket | 15th | Thursday 12:00 |
| Hours and contract changes | HR assistant, after HRBP approval if pay changes | 15th | Thursday 12:00 |
| Absence approved by managers | Managers in Talvento; I check the report | 15th | Thursday 12:00 |
| One-off payments | HRBP approval, then me | 12th | not used |
| "Nothing to send" confirmation | Me | 15th | Thursday 12:00 |

Remaining 2026 dates: monthly cut-off 2026-11-13 (the 15th is a Sunday), paid 2026-11-26; monthly cut-off 2026-12-15, paid early on 2026-12-22 for the holiday. Weekly cut-offs every Thursday except 2026-12-24 and 2026-12-31, which move to Wednesday 12:00 ([[year-end-payroll-calendar-2026]]).

Late changes go into the next run unless Elspeth approves an off-cycle payment.

## Why It Matters

Almost everything in [[coordinate-payroll-and-absence]] hangs off these dates. For peak hiring, the weekly line is the one that bites: a starter not in the system by Thursday 12:00 of their first week is paid a week late ([[warehouse-peak-hiring-2026]]). The checklist that sits on top of this calendar is [[payroll-cutoff-checklist-v2]]; the step-by-step is [[run-monthly-payroll-cutoff]].

## Caveats

The calendar is Group Payroll's document; I only maintain the People Services column. Check bank holiday changes with [[dariusz-nowicki]] each autumn.

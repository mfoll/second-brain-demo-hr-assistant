# AI Screening DPIA Screening 2026-07-08

DPIA screening meeting for the Hireloom AI ranking module: [[kasia-wrobel]] (DPO), [[marek-zielinski]], [[priyanka-desai]] for HR policy, and me.

## Decision

> [!note]
> Kasia concluded that a full data protection impact assessment is required before any use of the module, including a shadow test on past applications. Marek accepted this and parked the idea until after peak.

## Discussion

- Three screening triggers applied: scoring of individuals, new technology at scale, and significant effect on access to employment ([[dpia-screening-questions]]).
- Marek proposed a shadow test: run the module on 2025 peak applications without showing scores to anyone. Kasia said that is still processing candidates' data for a new purpose and needs the DPIA first.
- Priyanka raised the cut-off setting; agreed it must never be used ([[automated-decision-making]]).
- I raised that our past hires reflect the managers who made them, so calibration on them would carry their preferences forward ([[song-2026-algorithmic-bias-in-hr-recruitment-systems]]). Kasia added it to the vendor questions.

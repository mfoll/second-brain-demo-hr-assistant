# Hub Induction Day

One standard induction day for warehouse starters, run the same way at every hub, instead of four hubs each doing their own thing. At most 40 starters a session; weekly during peak hiring. I own the format and the bookings; hub managers own the room and the floor time.

## Outcome

A standard induction day run at every hub, at most 40 starters per session, weekly during peak hiring. The project closes when all four hubs have run the standard day through one full peak and the format is handed to the hubs as business as usual.

## Current State

- Running weekly at all four hubs since 2026-09-07 for [[warehouse-peak-hiring-2026]]. 191 peak starters inducted by 2026-10-20 across 28 sessions.
- The running order ([[hub-induction-day-running-order]]) is the same everywhere: 08:30 welcome and paperwork, safety walk-round, systems and pay, team leader time from 13:00, finish 15:30.
- Feedback from starters averages 4.3 out of 5 across the autumn sessions (aggregate, sessions of 10 or more only).
- Hub West's training room seats 32, so its sessions are capped at 32 ([[induction-room-capacity-by-hub]]).
- Open: a possible Saturday session for the last intake ([[should-induction-run-on-saturdays-during-peak]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Warehouse starters at the four hubs, direct and agency. Depots and Head Office keep their own inductions for now. Safety content belongs to [[ewan-galbraith]]; I only schedule it. No starter is named in session notes; attendance lives in Learnmoor.

## Working Context

The pilot at Hub East on 2026-05-14 with [[maeve-donnelly]] ([[hub-induction-pilot-hub-east-2026-05-14]]) had 22 starters and ran 50 minutes over, mostly because payroll forms were done on paper. We moved the [[new-starter-data-form]] to before day one and the overrun disappeared. The cap of 40 comes from the safety walk-round: one trainer walks at most 20 people at a time, and there are two walk-round slots.

Each session needs: a trainer from the hub, a safety trainer, a People Services person (me, [[aoife-brennan-hale]] or Callum) for the systems slot, and a team leader for the afternoon. The trainers are the bottleneck, which is why the buddy scheme was paused ([[buddy-scheme-pause-2026-09-07]]).

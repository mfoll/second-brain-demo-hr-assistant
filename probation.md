# Probation

The first period of employment during which the manager and the new starter check that the role works out, with a review at the end. General background in [[probation-workplace-wikipedia]].

## At Pellmore

- 26 weeks for permanent roles, with a mid-point conversation around week 10 and a final review by week 26.
- 8 weeks for seasonal peak contracts, one review at the end.
- Graduates: 26 weeks, reviewed by the scheme lead with the first rotation manager.
- The length is set in the offer letter ([[offer-letter-template-set]]).

## Who does what

- Managers hold the conversations and record the outcome in the HR system.
- HR Business Partners advise on extensions or concerns.
- My part is the reminders ([[probation-reminder-schedule]]) and the counts: how many reviews were recorded on time. I never see or write down an outcome for a named person.

## Why it is in my notes

Missed reviews were the most common gap in the old checklist ([[onboarding-checklist-2024]]). Since reminders started in May 2025, final reviews recorded by week 26 rose from 58% to 87%. Moving reminders into Talvento tasks is the next step ([[should-the-onboarding-checklist-live-in-talvento]]).

# Hybrid Policy Guidance

Writing and publishing the guidance for fixed anchor days in the five weeks between the Executive Committee decision and the 2026-04-01 start, then handling the first month of questions.

## Outcome

Manager guide and a 26-question FAQ published in Deskwell around the 2026-04-01 start.

## Current State

> [!success]
> Done. The [[hybrid-manager-guide]] went live on 2026-03-25 and the 26-question [[hybrid-policy-faq]] on 2026-03-27, both in [[deskwell]]. Both were superseded for v2 in August by [[hybrid-policy-review]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Guidance only, not the policy wording ([[priyanka-desai]]) or the internal announcement ([[shireen-qadir]]). Exceptions were pointed to the HR Business Partners and never answered in the FAQ.

## Working Context

Five weeks was too short. Priyanka wrote the manager guide; I drafted the FAQ from the [[hybrid-consultation-comment-summary]] themes, guessing what people would ask. I guessed well on desks and caring, badly on compressed hours and part-time staff: the FAQ assumed a five-day week, and in April hybrid questions hit 23% of tickets, a third of them from part-time or compressed-hours staff asking which three days counted ([[helpdesk-hybrid-ticket-themes-april-2026]]).

We added four FAQ answers on 2026-04-14 (part-time, compressed hours, travel to a depot on an anchor day, what to do when no desk is free), which helped but did not fix the desk problem.

Rejected: a live Q&A session per division (Shireen preferred one recorded briefing), and a chatbot over the FAQ (see [[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]).

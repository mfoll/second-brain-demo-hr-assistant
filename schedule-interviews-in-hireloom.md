# Schedule Interviews In Hireloom

How a recruitment coordinator gets a shortlisted candidate into a first interview. Written after the [[interview-scheduling-overhaul]]; revised for peak group sessions in August 2026.

## Inputs

- An open vacancy in [[hireloom]] with a hiring manager and an interview template.
- A shortlist marked by the manager or coordinator.

## Steps

1. **Check slots.** Every Monday, check that each open vacancy has at least six published slots for the next ten working days. If not, remind the manager; for drivers, the transport planner ([[should-first-interviews-for-drivers-use-self-scheduling]]).
2. **Send the link.** Set the candidate to Shortlisted and send the "Interview invitation, self-scheduling" template. Hireloom sends a standard calendar invite when they book ([[rfc-5545-icalendar]]).
3. **Peak group sessions.** For seasonal operative roles, send the "Group assessment" template instead; sessions hold 12 candidates at the hub.
4. **Chase after 48 hours.** Hireloom flags candidates who have not booked. Phone once; if no answer, send the reminder template. Do not chase a third time.
5. **Adjustments.** If a candidate asks for an adjustment, note "adjustment requested" in Hireloom and pass the request to the manager without the reason.
6. **After the interview.** The manager records the outcome and scoring sheet in Hireloom within two working days ([[structured-interviewing]]). Send the outcome template; never a free-written email.
7. **Offer.** For an offer, raise the letter from [[offer-letter-template-set]] after approval.

## Guardrails

- Interview notes live in Hireloom only.
- No candidate names in trackers, Teams messages to wider groups, or these notes ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).
- Second interviews and assessment centres are booked by the coordinator, not by link.

## Verification

Monthly: median [[time-to-schedule]] from the Hireloom report should stay under 2 working days. If one vacancy is far above, check whether its manager stopped publishing slots.

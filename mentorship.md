# Mentorship

## Summary

Mentorship is guidance given by someone with more experience to someone with less. In organisations, a mentor influences the mentee's personal and professional growth; mentors are often senior, but what matters is relevant experience, not rank.

## Why It Matters

Read the week before [[carys-whitlock]] started as our apprentice on 2026-09-07, when I was asked to mentor her. The point that a mentor does not have to be senior helped: I am two years in, which is exactly the experience Carys needs. It also matches how [[amina-yusuf]] works with me ([[mentoring-themes-with-amina]]).

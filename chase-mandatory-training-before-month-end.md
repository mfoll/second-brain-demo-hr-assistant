# Chase Mandatory Training Before Month End

The monthly cycle for mandatory training completion, agreed in January 2026 and run by me with [[yetunde-balogun]].

## Steps

1. First working day: run the Learnmoor "Compliance by Org Unit" report and apply the corrections in [[learnmoor-report-quirks]].
2. Same day: managers receive their team list through Learnmoor's manager view (not by email attachment). HRBPs receive a team-level summary with percentages only.
3. Week 2: a short reminder to managers of teams under 90%, with the number of people outstanding, not names.
4. Week 3: HRBP escalation for teams under 80%; for hubs, include the kiosk and practical session dates.
5. Last week: check payroll cut-off week does not clash; if it does, skip step 4 and note it.
6. Record the month's overall and divisional figures in the compliance workbook.

## Guardrails

- No bulk reminder emails to individuals ([[reminder-emails-do-not-move-training-completion]]).
- No named lists in shared folders, slides or HRBP summaries.
- During peak (August to mid-November) Warehousing gets step 2 only, by agreement with [[sanjay-mehrotra]].

## Verification

The figure each month should be reproducible from the saved report and the correction counts.

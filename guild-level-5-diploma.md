# Guild of People Practice Level 5 Diploma Programme Handbook

The learner handbook for the Guild of People Practice Level 5 Diploma in People Practice, 2025 to 2027 cohort, received on enrolment day ([[diploma-enrolment-and-induction-2025-09-15]]).

## Summary

Seven units by distance learning, each assessed by a written assignment, with an online tutor; the seventh is a workplace research project. Learners have up to 24 months to complete. Every unit must be passed; grades are pass, merit or distinction, with one resubmission per unit.

## Key Details

The seven units:

| Unit | Title | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Organisational Context and People Practice | 3,000-word report |
| 2 | Evidence-Based Practice | 3,500-word report with an evidence review |
| 3 | Employment Relationship Management | 3,500-word case study |
| 4 | Learning and Development Practice | 3,000-word report plus a session plan |
| 5 | Reward and Resourcing | 4,000-word report |
| 6 | People Data and Analytics | 3,000-word analysis with data tables |
| 7 | Workplace Research Project | research report up to 6,000 words |

Assessment rules worth remembering:

- Plan before you write: tutors must approve a one-page plan for each unit.
- Word counts are plus or minus 10%; tables and references do not count.
- Late work without an agreed extension is capped at a pass.
- A reflective log is required at the final portfolio review.

Research project guidance (Unit 7):

- The question must come from the learner's workplace and be answerable within about five months.
- An ethics form is required before any data collection, signed by the tutor and, where workplace data is used, by someone in the organisation with authority over it ([[research-ethics-form-2026-09]]).
- Personal data about colleagues or customers must not be collected unless strictly necessary, consented and approved; anonymised or aggregate data is preferred.
- The report must state its limitations and must not overclaim causation from observational data.

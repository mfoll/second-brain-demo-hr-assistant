# Interview Scheduling Overhaul Closure Report

Two-page closure note I wrote for [[marek-zielinski]] on 2025-07-25.

## Summary

Self-scheduling links used for 97% of first interviews in June and July 2025 (the rest were phone bookings for candidates who did not use the link). Median [[time-to-schedule]] 1.6 working days, down from 4.2.

## Key Details

- 704 first interviews booked through self-scheduling between 2025-05-12 and 2025-07-18.
- Rebooking rate down from 14% to 9%; candidate withdrawals before booking down from 6% to 3%.
- Depot and driver roles: median 2.9 days, still the slowest, because planners publish slots weekly.
- Candidate survey item "booking my interview was easy": 4.5 out of 5 ([[candidate-experience-survey-2025]]).
- Coordinator time saved: about six hours a week across Callum and me, estimated from email counts.

## Why It Matters

The evidence for keeping self-scheduling as the default and for the procedure [[schedule-interviews-in-hireloom]].

## Caveats

June and July are quieter months; peak 2025 later confirmed the effect held under volume (median 1.7).

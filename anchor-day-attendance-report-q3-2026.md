# Anchor-Day Attendance Report Q3 2026

Draft of the first quarterly anchor-day attendance report required by [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]], covering September 2026 only (v2 started 2026-09-01). Built by [[jun-takahashi]] with me; due to [[priyanka-desai]] by 2026-10-30 for the November People Committee and [[rosamund-oyelaran]].

## Summary

Draft version 0.3, 2026-10-21. Measure: share of hybrid-eligible staff with a desk booking or a site badge-in on Tuesdays, at Head Office and the hubs. Head Office Tuesdays: 88% of hybrid-eligible staff. Team-chosen days are reported as one figure per division, not per day.

## Why It Matters

Answers the COO's condition from the [[people-committee-2026-07-15]]. Its format is the open question in [[how-should-anchor-day-attendance-be-reported]].

## Caveats

- Draft; numbers will change.
- 23 of 78 hybrid teams have fewer than 10 people and are combined with others in their department; the draft shows 55 team rows plus combined rows.
- Bookings and badge-ins are proxies, and [[kasia-wrobel]] has asked that individual data stays with Facilities.

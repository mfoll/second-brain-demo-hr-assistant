# Engagement Survey 2026 Results Summary

The group-level results pack [[jun-takahashi]] presented to the People Committee on 2026-06-17 ([[engagement-survey-results-to-people-committee-2026-06-17]]).

## Summary

2,911 of 4,100 responded (71%, against 64% in 2024). Engagement index 64, up from 61. Strongest sections: safety (78 favourable) and my team (74). Weakest: workload and wellbeing (52) and change (49).

## Key Details

| | 2024 | 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Response rate | 64% | 71% |
| Engagement index | 61 | 64 |
| My manager | 66 | 69 |
| Communication | 55 | 58 |
| Change | 47 | 49 |
| Workload and wellbeing | 53 | 52 |

- Response by division: Head Office 88%, Forwarding 80%, Warehousing 73%, Road Freight 62%.
- Index by division: Forwarding 68, Head Office 66, Warehousing 64, Road Freight 61.
- Hybrid items (hybrid-eligible only): 46% favourable on "the office days work for my team", the lowest single item.
- Top free-text themes, counted by Jun from 3,870 comments: workload and staffing (19%), pay and benefits (16%), communication from senior leaders (12%), office days (9%), equipment and facilities (8%).
- 31 departments with their own reports; 13 rolled up to site level.

## Why It Matters

The base for every department action plan ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]). The hybrid item and the office-days comment theme are part of why the June pulse check happened ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]). The workload score is the reason [[wellbeing-and-burnout]] exists as a reading cluster.

## Caveats

The index rise of 3 points is within what Jun calls "probably real but modest". Road Freight's low response rate means its scores carry more uncertainty. Theme percentages are of comments, not of respondents.

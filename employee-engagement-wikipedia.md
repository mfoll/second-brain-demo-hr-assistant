# Employee engagement

## Summary

The article treats employee engagement as a way of describing the relationship between an organisation and its employees, qualitatively and quantitatively. An engaged employee is absorbed in and enthusiastic about the work and acts in the organisation's interest; disengagement ranges from doing the minimum to actively harming the organisation.

## Why It Matters

Read before the first Steering Group meeting ([[survey-steering-group-kickoff-2026-03-04]]) because I wanted to know what our [[engagement-index]] was supposed to measure. The useful point for me: engagement is a relationship, so a score is only the start of a conversation with a team, which is the logic of the action plans ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).

## Caveats

The article covers many competing definitions and measures; it does not settle which one a survey should use. Our index is our own construct, not a standard one.

# Self-Service Deflection

The share of employee questions answered by the knowledge base without anyone raising a ticket. The rebuild's target calls it "self-service resolution" and sets it at 30%.

## Why it is hard to measure

You cannot count a ticket that was never raised. Every measure is a proxy:

- **View-based** (Leon's): article views not followed by a ticket from the same person in the same category within seven days, as a share of views plus tickets. September 2026: 22%. Overstates it, because many views are browsing, not questions.
- **Click-based**: share of article views where "still need help?" was not clicked. 94% in September; useless, nearly everyone just leaves.
- **Survey-based**: asking "did this answer your question?" at the end of an article. Not switched on yet in [[deskwell]].
- **Trend-based** (mine): repeat tickets in a category after an article is published or rewritten. This is the approach of my [[diploma-research-project]]. A repeat query is the same employee raising another ticket in the same category within 30 days; [[leon-adjei]] counts them inside Deskwell and only monthly totals by category leave it.

The disagreement is recorded in [[how-do-we-measure-self-service-resolution]].

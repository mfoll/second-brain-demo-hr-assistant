# Should First Interviews For Drivers Use Self-Scheduling?

## Question

Driver hiring managers are on the road and at depots; most do not use Hireloom. Should driver first interviews be an exception to self-scheduling?

## Current Answer

No exception. Transport planners publish interview slots for their manager each Monday, and candidates book as for any other role. Agreed with [[lucia-arbeloa]] in May 2025 during the [[interview-scheduling-overhaul]].

## Evidence

- Driver [[time-to-schedule]] fell from 6.8 to 2.9 working days.
- The phone fallback is used more for drivers (about 15% against 3% overall), mostly candidates applying from a phone without email access.

## Caveat

Depends on planners keeping the slots up. When a planner is on leave, slots stop and time to schedule jumps; Callum checks driver vacancies every Tuesday.

# Hybrid Review Paper 2026-07

People Committee paper "Hybrid working: three-month review of anchor days", by [[priyanka-desai]] with evidence sections by me, [[jun-takahashi]] and [[nadia-haddou]]. Circulated 2026-07-08 for the [[people-committee-2026-07-15]].

## Summary

Reviews April to June under fixed anchor days. Evidence: desk bookings at 104% of capacity on anchor days in April and May; hybrid helpdesk tickets 23%, 14% and 11% of all tickets; the June 2026 pulse check on hybrid work at division level ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]); managers' views. Three options, recommending one fixed Tuesday plus two team-chosen days with a 95% per-floor cap.

## Why It Matters

The paper the People Committee decided on. My helpdesk appendix is the first time ticket counts went into a committee paper.

## Caveats

The pulse check results are summarised at division level only; small teams are not shown. The extra-floor option is costed but unfunded.

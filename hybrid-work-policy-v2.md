# Hybrid Work Policy v2

The hybrid work policy as published by People Services, version 2, in force from 2026-09-01, shown here side by side with version 1 (in force 2026-04-01 to 2026-08-31). Policy owner: [[priyanka-desai]]. Approved by the People Committee on 2026-07-15.

## Summary

Version 2 keeps three office days a week for hybrid-eligible roles but fixes only Tuesday company-wide; each team agrees its other two office days with its manager and records them in [[talvento]]. It adds quarterly reporting of anchor-day attendance. The full v1 text is archived in [[hybrid-work-policy-v1]].

## Why It Matters

This is the answer to "what is our hybrid rule now?". The change came from the [[hybrid-policy-review]]: Head Office desks at 104% on anchor days, hybrid questions at 23% of helpdesk tickets in April, and managers wanting one fixed meeting day. My FAQ and knowledge base articles must match it clause by clause.

## Key Details

| Clause | v1 (2026-04-01) | v2 (2026-09-01) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who it covers | About 1,150 hybrid-eligible roles listed by job family | Unchanged |
| Office days | Three a week | Three a week |
| Fixed days | Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday for everyone | Tuesday only, for everyone |
| Other days | None to choose | Two days chosen by each team, agreed with the manager and recorded in Talvento; reviewed every six months |
| Part-time and compressed hours | Not addressed (added to FAQ 2026-04-14) | Pro rata: office days are 60% of contracted days, rounded to the nearest day, always including Tuesday where Tuesday is a working day |
| Desks | Book through the desk tool | Unchanged; teams told their chosen days must not push any floor above 95% of desks |
| Exceptions | Manager with HR Business Partner | Unchanged; individual adjustments are handled as cases |
| Reporting | None | Anchor-day attendance reported quarterly to the COO, aggregated by team, with teams under 10 combined |
| Review | Three months after start | Annually, next review by 2027-09 |

## Caveats

- "Attendance" in v2 is not defined in the policy; it is being defined in practice for the first report ([[how-should-anchor-day-attendance-be-reported]]).
- Site-based roles are outside both versions ([[should-site-based-roles-get-a-flexibility-offer]]).
- I summarised the clauses; the policy text in the library is authoritative ([[hr-policy-library-index]]).

# Helpdesk Categories Go-Live 2025-06-16

The 14 new ticket categories and routing rules went live in [[deskwell]] at 07:00 on Monday 2025-06-16. Open tickets in the old categories (about 140) were re-categorised by hand over the weekend by [[leon-adjei]] and me.

## First week

- 1,034 tickets categorised in the first four weeks; 79% got a first response within two working days, against 48% in March.
- 23 tickets misfiled in week one, mostly Contracts versus Leavers (notice periods). Fixed with one line in the routing rules: anything about notice is Leavers.
- Two Employee relations tickets were briefly visible in the shared queue because of a rule order mistake; corrected within an hour by Leon, and [[tomas-ferreira-lund]] checked nothing had been opened. Logged with [[kasia-wrobel]] as a near miss, no personal data viewed.

## What changed afterwards

The near miss is why the ER rule is now the first rule evaluated ([[deskwell-routing-rules]]). The [[helpdesk-triage-categories]] project closed on 2025-06-20.

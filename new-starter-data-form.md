# New Starter Data Form

The form every new hire completes before day one, giving the details payroll and the HR system need. Rebuilt in June 2026 for Talvento.

## Summary

Collects bank details, tax and pension information, emergency contact and the starter's confirmed start date. Since 2026-06-29 the starter completes it in Talvento's pre-hire portal, and the data lands directly in their Talvento record; before that it was a PDF typed into Staffora by People Services.

## Key Details

- Sent automatically when the offer is accepted in Hireloom and the record is created in Talvento.
- Must be complete before the Thursday 12:00 weekly payroll cut-off of the first week for hourly staff, and before the 15th for salaried staff ([[payroll-cutoff-calendar]]).
- 26 fields, down from 41 on the old PDF; fields such as "previous employer" and "marital status" were dropped as not needed.
- Completion rate before day one in peak 2026: 94% of direct starters; the rest complete it in the systems slot at induction.

## Why It Matters

This is the step that decides whether a starter is paid on time. Moving it before day one also fixed the induction overrun at the Hub East pilot ([[hub-induction-pilot-hub-east-2026-05-14]]).

## Caveats

Agency workers do not complete it; they are paid by the agency. I see completion status only, not the content.

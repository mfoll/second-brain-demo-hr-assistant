# Acceptance testing

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

General article on acceptance testing: tests run to decide whether a system meets the requirements of a specification or contract, with a section on user acceptance testing, where intended users check the system against real business scenarios before accepting it.

## Why It Matters

I read it before writing my first test scripts to understand what sign-off actually commits the business to. It shaped how I wrote the scripts in the [[uat-test-script-register]]: real scenarios from People Services, not feature checklists.

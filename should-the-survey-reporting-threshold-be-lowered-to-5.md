# Should The Survey Reporting Threshold Be Lowered To 5

The Survey Steering Group set the minimum reporting group at 10 for 2026 and agreed to revisit it before the 2027 survey ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]). [[sanjay-mehrotra]] wants 5 so that small depots see their own results. The Steering Group decides, probably in February 2027; I am drafting the options paper.

## Why it matters

13 of 44 departments, mostly small depots, get only site-level results at 10. At 5, only 4 would. The trade-off is between managers acting on their own results and respondents trusting that they cannot be identified.

## Options for the paper

1. Keep 10 for everything.
2. 8 for scores, 10 for comments, as [[kasia-wrobel]] said would be defensible ([[dpo-advice-survey-reporting-threshold]]).
3. 5 for scores with no filters at all and no comments, 10 for anything else.
4. Keep 10, but invest in facilitated sessions for roll-up departments ([[action-planning-workshop-hub-east-2026-07-08]]).

## Evidence to collect before February

- How many of the 13 roll-up departments agreed a plan and how good it was ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).
- Response rates at small depots in 2026 compared with 2024. If trust rose, response should have too.
- What the Employee Forum thinks now; [[gabriel-santoro]] supported 10.

## Current Answer

None yet. My leaning is option 2 or 4 ([[threshold-of-10-is-right-for-now]]).

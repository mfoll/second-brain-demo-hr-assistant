# Employee Forum Hybrid Consultation 2026-01-21

Third and last Employee Forum session on hybrid working, chaired by [[gabriel-santoro]], 14:00 to 15:30 at Head Office with depot and hub representatives on video. [[priyanka-desai]] presented; I took notes and presented the comment themes.

## What I presented

The coded 412 comments ([[hybrid-consultation-comment-summary]]): desk availability 131, team choice of days 118, commute 97, caring 74, fairness for site-based staff 52, office time for learning and new starters 49.

## Forum positions

- Clear preference for team-chosen days (the third option in the [[employee-forum-hybrid-consultation-paper]]).
- If days are fixed, guarantee desks first.
- Gabriel asked formally that site-based staff get some flexibility offer as well, for example shift swaps or a predictable rota; Priyanka agreed to put it in the Executive Committee paper as a separate point ([[should-site-based-roles-get-a-flexibility-offer]]).

## After

Notes checked by Gabriel on 2026-01-23; consultation closed 2026-02-13 when the summary went into the [[exec-committee-hybrid-paper-2026-02]]. The Executive Committee chose fixed days anyway ([[exec-committee-hybrid-decision-2026-02-24]]); the Forum's desk warning was the one that came true.

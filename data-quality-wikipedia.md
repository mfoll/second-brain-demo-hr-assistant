# Data quality

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines data quality as how well data fits its intended uses, and lists the usual dimensions used to judge it, such as accuracy, completeness, consistency and timeliness. Notes that internal consistency between sources becomes a concern as the number of sources grows.

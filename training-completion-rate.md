# Training Completion Rate

The share of people required to hold a mandatory course who hold an in-date completion of it.

## Method agreed in January 2026

- Numerator: in-date completions recorded in [[learnmoor]].
- Denominator: employees required to hold the course ([[mandatory-training-catalogue]]), excluding long-term absence, parental leave and leavers in their notice period.
- New starters inside their 30-day window count as incomplete. This is stricter than it could be, but stable.
- Overall figure: the simple average of the four course rates.

## Why define it

Before January, three people produced three different numbers from the same system. Most disagreements were about the denominator. Fixing the method made the trend credible: 81% in January, 89% in October.

## What it does not tell you

Whether anyone can lift safely or spot a fire risk. Completion measures the formal part of learning only ([[bae-2021-quarry-workers-formal-and-informal-safety-training]]).

# Helpdesk Category Workshop 2025-04-23

Two-hour workshop in the Head Office training room to agree the new [[deskwell]] categories. Present: [[leon-adjei]] (chair), me, [[callum-rourke]], [[aoife-brennan-hale]], [[tomas-ferreira-lund]], and [[dariusz-nowicki]] for Group Payroll.

## What we did

I brought the [[helpdesk-baseline-report-2025-03]] printed out: 31 categories, nine with fewer than five tickets a month, 38% of tickets in "General enquiry". We sorted 200 recent ticket subject lines (subjects only, names removed by me beforehand) into piles on the table and named the piles.

## Decisions

- 14 categories, each with one owning queue and a default assignee ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).
- "General enquiry" abolished; an uncategorised ticket goes back to the triager.
- Employee relations stays separate and restricted, on Tomás's insistence.
- Pay stays separate from Pensions and benefits, on Dariusz's insistence: different people answer them.
- First-response target of two working days for all categories ([[what-first-response-target-should-the-helpdesk-use]]).

## Rejected

Eight broad categories (my first draft). Dariusz and Tomás both showed tickets that would have been misrouted.

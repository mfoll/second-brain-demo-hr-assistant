# Hub Induction Differences By Hub

What still differs between hubs after the standard [[hub-induction-day-running-order]], so whoever runs the systems slot is not caught out.

- **Hub North.** Badges printed at reception, not by the trainer; collect by 08:15. Canteen card issued at lunch.
- **Hub East.** Two safety trainers, so the walk-round runs in parallel and finishes 20 minutes early. [[maeve-donnelly]] often drops in at 15:00.
- **Hub South.** Biggest room (48) but poor mobile signal: starters download the Talvento app on the guest wifi; password is on the room whiteboard.
- **Hub West.** Room for 32 only and used for briefings outside Mondays. Lockers allocated after the walk-round. The team leader slot starts at 13:30 because of shift handover.

None of these are worth standardising; they come from the buildings.

# Algorithmic bias

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes algorithmic bias as systematic, repeatable errors in a computer system that produce unfair outcomes, such as favouring one group over another. Covers where bias comes from (pre-existing bias in the data, technical limits, emergent effects of use), well-known examples including hiring tools, and approaches to detection and regulation.

## Why It Matters

Read before the DPIA screening meeting. The section on bias carried over from historical data is the core of my worry about the Hireloom module's calibration on our own past hires ([[how-hireloom-ai-ranking-would-work]]).

## Caveats

Broad and fast-changing; examples are from other organisations and do not show anything about Hireloom.

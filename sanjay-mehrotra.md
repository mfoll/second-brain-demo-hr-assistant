# Sanjay Mehrotra

HR Business Partner for Pellmore Warehousing, about 1,900 people across the four hubs and most of the depots.

## Role

Sanjay advises the Warehousing leadership team, including [[conrad-baptiste]], on people matters, and owns the division's survey action plans. He sits on the Survey Steering Group.

## The threshold dissent

At the [[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10|Steering Group on 2026-06-10]] Sanjay argued for a minimum reporting group of 5, as in 2024, so that depot managers could see and act on their own results. The group followed [[kasia-wrobel]]'s advice and set 10; his dissent is minuted. He has not let it go and says so politely in most meetings. His point is fair: several small depots had good action plans in 2024 and now plan from a site average that hides them ([[how-can-small-depots-act-on-site-level-results]]). The threshold is to be revisited before the 2027 survey ([[should-the-survey-reporting-threshold-be-lowered-to-5]]).

## How we work together

- I send him the weekly list of missing Warehousing action plans on Mondays ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).
- During peak he is hard to reach; a short message with one decision needed works better than a meeting request.
- He also chases Warehousing mandatory training through his managers, which is most of why completion moves at all ([[mandatory-training-compliance]]).

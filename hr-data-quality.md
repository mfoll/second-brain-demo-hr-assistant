# HR Data Quality

Retrieval surface for whether Pellmore's HR records are right: complete, current, consistent between systems and fit for payroll, reporting and access. Most of what I know about it came from the Talvento migration.

## Current position

> [!info]
> Talvento is the master record since 2026-06-29. The monthly [[hr-data-quality-scorecard]] shows 99.2% of active records with a valid cost centre and 98.7% with the right line manager in September 2026. Ownership after the programme team shrinks is still open ([[who-owns-hr-data-quality-after-go-live]]).

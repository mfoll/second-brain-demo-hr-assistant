# DPO Case Notes Agreement 2025-03-19

Meeting with [[kasia-wrobel]] (DPO), with [[leon-adjei]] for the first ten minutes, after a slip in a shared People Services document.

## What happened

On 2025-03-17 a helpdesk note about a colleague's sickness absence, including health details, was pasted into the team's shared "tricky queries" document to ask for advice. About 25 people had access. Leon removed it the same day and reported it to Kasia. It was not my note, but I had been keeping my own working notes in a similar style, with names, and I asked Kasia to look at them too.

## What was agreed

- My notes, team documents and knowledge base drafts never record a named employee's or candidate's personal, medical, disciplinary, grievance or pay details.
- A case is described by category and count and points to the system that holds it (Staffora case reference, Deskwell ticket), with the reference written as a placeholder if the note is shared.
- Aggregates are fine if the group is not small enough to identify someone.
- I rewrote my existing notes the same week; Kasia checked a sample of ten on 2025-03-26 and was satisfied.
- Leon replaced the "tricky queries" document with a Deskwell queue for advice requests, which has access control.

The rule is written up as [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]] and applies to everything in this vault.

## Why it matters

The single most important rule in how I keep notes. It is also why the [[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]] is counts only and why my [[diploma-research-project]] uses ticket counts, not ticket text.

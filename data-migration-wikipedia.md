# Data migration

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes data migration as selecting, preparing, extracting and transforming data and moving it permanently from one system to another, and counts validation of the moved data and decommissioning of the old system as part of the process.

## Why It Matters

Useful mostly for the reminder that validation and decommissioning are part of a migration, not afterthoughts. Both are still open for us: the [[payroll-parallel-run]] is our validation of the payroll feed, and [[staffora-decommissioning-plan]] is still a draft.

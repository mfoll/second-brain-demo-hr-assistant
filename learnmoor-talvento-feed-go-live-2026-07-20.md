# Learnmoor Talvento Feed Go-Live 2026-07-20

The nightly completions feed from [[learnmoor]] to [[talvento]] ran for real for the first time, three weeks after the core HR go-live.

## What happened

- Historic load of 14,880 in-date mandatory completions done on 2026-07-18 ([[learnmoor-talvento-completion-feed-spec]]).
- First night: 412 rows sent, 37 rejected, almost all new starters entered in Talvento after the Learnmoor user file was cut.

## Why it matters

Ended my monthly manual upload. Managers in Talvento self-service can now see their team's mandatory training status, which helps the [[manager-self-service-rollout]].

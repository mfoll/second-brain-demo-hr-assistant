# Absence Reconciliation Reports 2026

The monthly one-page reports Dariusz and I produce after each reconciliation, January to September 2026.

## Summary

Each report gives: absence records compared (about 6,500 to 7,000 a month), mismatches, rate, mismatches by cause, by division, and actions. Rates: January 3.8%, February 3.1%, March 2.6%, April 2.2%, May 1.9%, June 2.4%, July 1.6%, August 1.2%, September 0.9%.

## Why It Matters

The trend is the measure for [[absence-data-reconciliation]]. Counts by division only; the reports go to Elspeth, Deborah and the three HRBPs. Causes over time: [[absence-mismatch-causes-2026]].

## Caveats

From October, absence approved after the cut-off but processed correctly in the next run is counted separately as "late but correct" ([[can-managers-approve-absence-after-the-cut-off]]), which will lower the rate by roughly a third. The trend line will show the break.

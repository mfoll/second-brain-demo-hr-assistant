# Onboarding Checklist Ownership Map

Who owns each group of items in the [[onboarding-stage-checklists-2025]]. Owners are roles, never names, so the map survives staff changes.

| Stage | Items | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-start | offer letter, Hireloom to HR system export | People Services recruitment coordinator |
| Pre-start | right-to-work check, references | People Services onboarding (currently Aoife's role) |
| Pre-start | IT account ready on day one, equipment | IT service desk |
| Pre-start | desk or locker, badge | Facilities or hub admin |
| Pre-start | first-week plan, induction booked | Hiring manager |
| Day one | welcome, team, safety briefing | Hiring manager, hub trainer |
| Day one | systems log-in, pay date explained | People Services |
| Day one | mandatory training enrolled | Learning and Development |
| First 90 days | week-one check-in, probation mid-point and review | Hiring manager |
| First 90 days | mandatory training complete | Starter, chased by Learning and Development |
| First 90 days | feedback survey | People Services |

17 items in the old list had no owner; the hardest to settle were IT ("requested" or "ready") and badges at depots (hub admin, after Facilities said they do not cover depots).

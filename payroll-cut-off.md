# Payroll Cut-Off

The last moment a change can reach payroll and still be paid in this run. After it, the change goes into the next run as an adjustment, unless the Payroll Manager approves an off-cycle payment.

## At Pellmore

- Monthly payroll: the 15th, paid the 26th. Weekly payroll: Thursday 12:00, paid the Friday of the following week ([[payroll-cutoff-calendar]]).
- A late change is not a lost change; it is a delayed one. But a new starter missing the weekly cut-off is a week without pay, and a leaver missing it is an overpayment that has to be recovered.

## Things that confuse people

- The cut-off is for the change reaching payroll, not for the event. A sickness absence on the 14th approved on the 16th misses the cut-off ([[can-managers-approve-absence-after-the-cut-off]]).
- The effective date can be before the cut-off and the change still be late ([[effective-dating]]).
- "Cut-off" and "payroll close" are different: Group Payroll closes the run a day or two later; the cut-off is for us.

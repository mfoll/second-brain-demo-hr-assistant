# Onboarding Buddy Scheme

Give every new office starter a trained colleague as a buddy for their first 90 days: someone who is not their manager and can answer the questions people do not like to ask. Set up by me and [[yetunde-balogun]] for [[deborah-okwuosa]].

## Outcome

Every new office starter has a trained buddy for 90 days.

## Current State

- Paused since 2026-09-07 ([[buddy-scheme-pause-2026-09-07]]). Deborah moved the trainers who ran buddy training onto weekly hub inductions for peak.
- Up to the pause: 34 buddies trained, 41 office starters paired, 38 of them with a buddy for the full period.
- Pairs already running were allowed to finish; no new pairs since the pause. Office starters in September and October (about 25) had no buddy.
- Restart planned for January 2027 ([[can-the-buddy-scheme-restart-in-january]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Office starters at Head Office and the Forwarding offices. Hub and depot starters were never in scope; they have team leaders on shift. Buddies are volunteers with their manager's agreement. Buddy conversations are private; we only collect aggregate survey results.

## Working Context

Buddy training is a 90-minute session, built by Yetunde in Learnmoor. The midpoint survey ([[buddy-scheme-midpoint-survey-2026]], 29 starters responded) showed the biggest value was in weeks two to six, for practical questions about systems, expenses and who to ask. The research on newcomers mostly looks at managers' feedback ([[zhao-2025-supervisor-regulatory-foci-and-newcomer-performance]]), but I read the buddy as filling the gaps a busy manager leaves.

I think the pause was the wrong trade-off: [[pausing-the-buddy-scheme-was-the-wrong-trade-off]]. Recorded here so the restart can be judged on numbers.

## Resume Condition

Resume when peak induction drops back from weekly to monthly, expected after 2026-11-23, and when Deborah confirms two trainer days a month for buddy training.

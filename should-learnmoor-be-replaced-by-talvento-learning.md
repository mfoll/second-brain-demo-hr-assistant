# Should Learnmoor Be Replaced By Talvento Learning

[[talvento]] has a learning module we have not bought. [[fiona-mcbride]] raised in August whether it could replace [[learnmoor]] when the Learnmoor licence renews on 2027-01-31.

## Considerations

- One system fewer and no completions feed to maintain ([[learnmoor-talvento-completion-feed-spec]]).
- [[yetunde-balogun]]'s concerns: Learnmoor's PIN login for operatives without email, classroom booking, and 140 SCORM packages that would need re-testing ([[sharable-content-object-reference-model]]).
- Nobody has checked whether either system supports xAPI for recording practical sessions ([[xapi-specification-adl]]).
- The HRIS team has payroll cutover first ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]).

## Status

Deferred on 2026-10-07 by the HRIS Steering Group: renew Learnmoor for one year in January 2027 and assess the Talvento module during 2027, after payroll cutover.

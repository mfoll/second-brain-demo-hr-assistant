# Chatbot

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

A chatbot is software that converses through text or speech. The article traces chatbots over decades to the deep-learning chatbots built on large language models, and names customer service and support as a long-standing area of use.

## Why It Matters

Background I read before the [[helpdesk-chatbot-trial]] so I could follow the vendor demo. The useful point for us: a modern chatbot generates text rather than picking from fixed answers, so it can be fluent and wrong. That shaped the test design ([[chatbot-test-question-set-2025]]).

## Caveats

A broad overview; says nothing about HR or about how to evaluate a bot.

# From Tool to Social Actor: A Systematic Review of the Psychological Mechanisms Through Which Conversational AI Reshapes the Employee Experience.

Liu L, Zhang R, Su X. *Behavioral Sciences*, 2026.

## Summary

A systematic review of 84 studies on how conversational AI at work affects employees. Most studies treat it as a tool, but a minority treat it as a partner or supervisor, driven by human-like cues. The review describes double-edged effects: augmentation against skill threat, constant support against erosion of social contact, inclusion against alienation. It notes the evidence is mostly cross-sectional.

## Why It Matters

Read in October 2026 when Leon floated a 2027 chatbot retry. It is about employees using AI in their own work, not as a helpdesk, but it is a reminder that a retry affects the helpdesk team as well as the people asking. If a bot takes the easy questions, the people on the rota get only the hard ones. Noted in [[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]].

## Caveats

Mechanisms are proposed, not tested; review searched one database. Read from the abstract.

# How Do We Close The Peak Hiring Gap?

## Question

On 2026-10-20 we were 46 operatives short of the 260 target, with hiring at about 17 a week and the last start date on 2026-11-16. How do we close the gap, and should the target change?

## Current State

Open. [[conrad-baptiste]] wants a plan from me and Marek by 2026-11-03.

## Options

1. **Agency top-up.** [[chiara-lombardi-ross]] can supply up to 40 at five working days' notice. Fastest; breaks the 60/40 split (would end about 52/48) and costs more per head.
2. **Keep direct hiring open to 2026-11-06.** The pipeline holds 61 direct candidates, of whom about 70% usually start, so maybe 40 more; but late direct hires reach induction only in the last week.
3. **Mix.** Direct to 2026-11-06, agency ordered on 2026-11-09 for whatever is left. My preferred option.
4. **Lower the target.** Conrad's forecast assumed +4% on 2025 volume; if the volume forecast is revised, 240 might be enough. Not my decision.

## Constraints

- Induction places: about 120 a week in practice, so capacity is not the limit ([[peak-induction-capacity-maths]]).
- Hub West sessions are capped at 32, and it has a 16-person gap.
- Any direct starter must be in Talvento before the Thursday 12:00 cut-off of their first week.

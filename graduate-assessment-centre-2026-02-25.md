# Graduate Assessment Centre 2026-02-25

The second 2026 graduate assessment centre, in person at Head Office.

## What happened

- 22 invited, 22 attended. Travel costs reimbursed for 17.
- Same exercises, assessors and scoring as the online centre ([[graduate-assessment-centre-pack-2026]]).
- Ran on time; wash-up finished at 16:50.
- Candidates had lunch with three current graduates, which several mentioned in the feedback.

## Outcomes

10 offers recommended from 22. Mean candidate feedback 4.6 out of 5, against 4.2 for the online centre. Assessors rated their confidence in scores higher for the group exercise.

## Comparison

The offer rate was similar (10 of 22 against 8 of 21), so the format did not obviously change who got through. The in-person centre cost more: travel for 17 candidates, two rooms and lunch, roughly three times the online centre according to Theo Varkey's figures. What we concluded is in [[do-assessment-centres-need-to-be-in-person]].

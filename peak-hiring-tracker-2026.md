# Peak Hiring Tracker 2026

The weekly tracker for [[warehouse-peak-hiring-2026]]. I update it every Monday by 17:00 for the Tuesday review. Counts only: no candidate or starter names, which stay in Hireloom, Talvento and the agency's own records.

## Summary

On 2026-10-20: 214 of 260 seasonal operatives hired (82%), 128 direct and 86 agency. 46 still needed, 34 of them at Hub North and Hub West. 191 inducted; 23 booked for 27 and 28 October. No direct starter has missed their first weekly pay this year.

## Key Details

Position on 2026-10-20 by hub:

| Hub | Target | Hired | Direct | Agency | Inducted | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hub North | 70 | 52 | 30 | 22 | 46 | 18 |
| Hub East | 75 | 69 | 42 | 27 | 63 | 6 |
| Hub South | 60 | 54 | 33 | 21 | 49 | 6 |
| Hub West | 55 | 39 | 23 | 16 | 33 | 16 |
| Total | 260 | 214 | 128 | 86 | 191 | 46 |

Other tabs:

- **Weekly hires.** Hires peaked at 31 in the week of 2026-09-14; the last four weeks averaged 17.
- **Induction slots.** Sessions booked per hub per week against capacity (40 a session, 32 at Hub West). Utilisation over the last four weeks: 68%.
- **Pipeline.** 61 direct candidates between interview and start on 2026-10-20; historically about 70% of these start.
- **Drop-outs.** 19 direct hires withdrew between offer and start (13%); 7 agency workers did not turn up on day one.
- **Pay check.** Every direct starter's Talvento entry date against the Thursday 12:00 weekly cut-off of their first week. Zero late entries.

## Why It Matters

The tracker is the shared truth for the Tuesday review with [[conrad-baptiste]], [[marek-zielinski]], [[chiara-lombardi-ross]] and the hub managers. It shows the gap is a Hub North and Hub West problem, and that induction capacity is not the limit (68% used): the limit is direct applicants at those two hubs. That framing is in [[how-do-we-close-the-peak-hiring-gap]].

## Caveats

- "Hired" means offer accepted and checks under way, not started. A direct hire who withdraws is removed the following Monday.
- Agency numbers come from Chiara's Monday sheet and can lag by a few days.
- Drop-out counts below 10 at hub level are not shown in the version circulated beyond the review.

# Run A Hub Induction Day

How People Services prepares, runs and closes a [[hub-induction-day]] session. Follows the [[hub-induction-day-running-order]].

## The week before

1. **Monday.** Book starters into sessions from the [[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]] and the agency's Monday sheet. Never more than 40, or 32 at Hub West.
2. **Monday.** Confirm the hub trainer, safety trainer and team leader with the hub; check the room ([[induction-room-capacity-by-hub]]).
3. **Wednesday 14:00.** Check every direct starter has a Talvento record and a complete [[new-starter-data-form]]. Chase anyone missing that afternoon. If they cannot be complete by Thursday 12:00 (the weekly payroll cut-off, [[payroll-cutoff-calendar]]), move their start to the following week and tell the hub ([[how-do-we-stop-new-starters-missing-first-pay]]).
4. **Thursday.** Send the joining email: time, address, what to bring, parking.
5. **Friday.** Send the hub the starter list (names and start time only) for badges.

## On the day

1. Arrive by 08:15; check badges and the attendance list in Learnmoor.
2. Run the systems and pay slot at 11:00: Talvento app log-in, rota, first pay date, who to ask. Catch anyone whose form is still incomplete.
3. Mark attendance in Learnmoor before leaving.

## After

1. Monday: mark no-shows in the tracker (count only); tell the agency about its no-shows.
2. Upload feedback forms; report averages only for sessions of 10 or more.
3. Any individual issue (pay, right to work, a health or access need) goes to the right system or person, never into a note ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

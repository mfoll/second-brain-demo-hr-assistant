# Retrieval-augmented generation

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Retrieval-augmented generation lets a large language model look up a specified set of documents and use them when answering, so it can draw on internal or more recent information than its training data. The article says the technique was proposed in 2020 and is now widely used, for example to let chatbots answer from company documents.

## Why It Matters

This is what Deskwell Assist was doing with our articles, which is why the quality of the articles decided the result. Re-read in October 2026 for [[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]: a retry only makes sense once the 138 articles are accurate and reviewed.

## Caveats

Describes the technique, not any product. Does not tell us how good the answers will be with our content.

# Candidate Email Templates 2025

The Hireloom candidate email set rewritten by Callum and me between July and September 2025, live from 2025-09-29.

## Summary

46 templates, replacing 71. Every template has one purpose, an owner, a review date, a plain subject line, and the next step in the first two sentences.

## Key Details

- Groups: acknowledgement (4), interview invitation and self-scheduling (9), assessment centre (6), outcome (12), offer and pre-start (10), withdrawal and data (5).
- Reading level aimed at age 12; average sentence length cut from 24 to 14 words.
- Every rejection says whether the candidate may reapply and when their data will be deleted ([[candidate-data-retention-schedule]]).
- Rejections do not yet give a reason category; Marek wanted to wait for the 2025 survey results.
- Owners: Callum for Road Freight and Forwarding variants, me for Warehousing and Head Office.

## Why It Matters

The emails are most of what candidates see of us. Rationale in [[candidate-email-tone-rewrite-notes]]; the previous set is archived as [[candidate-email-templates-2024]].

## Caveats

Hiring managers can still write their own emails outside Hireloom, which are not logged.

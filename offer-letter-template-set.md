# Offer Letter Template Set

The five Hireloom offer letter templates, reviewed by Priyanka Desai and Marek Zieliński in November 2025.

## Summary

One template per contract type: permanent salaried, permanent hourly, fixed-term, seasonal and graduate. Each merges role, site, start date, hours, pay rate and probation length from the approved offer.

## Key Details

- Probation length is set by contract type: 26 weeks for permanent roles, 8 weeks for seasonal contracts ([[probation]]).
- Seasonal letters state the end date and that the contract covers peak only; extension needs a new letter.
- Pay is a merge field from the approved offer; coordinators cannot type it.
- Every letter links to the [[new-starter-data-form]] and states the first pay date.
- The graduate letter includes the scheme's two-year rotation and the 2026-09-07 start.

## Why It Matters

The letters are the last thing I send before a starter becomes an onboarding case. Getting the probation length and first pay date right avoids most early helpdesk tickets from new starters.

## Caveats

Contract terms are HR Advisors' wording, not mine; I only maintain the merge fields.

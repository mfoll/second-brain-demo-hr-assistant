# Talvento Implementation Statement Of Work

The statement of work between Pellmore and Talvento's implementation partner, signed in August 2025. I read the summary version Fiona shared with the programme team; the commercial schedule is not in it.

## Summary

- Partner delivers: configuration of core HR, organisation structure, absence and self-service; data migration tooling and two rehearsals; the payroll interface; training for the HRIS analyst and "super users"; hypercare for four weeks after go-live.
- Pellmore delivers: cleansed data, test scripts and testers, the decision on every configuration choice, internal communications, end-user training.
- Payroll interface support runs until payroll cutover plus one month.
- Change requests for anything outside scope, priced per day. An archive of legacy records is listed as out of scope.

## Why It Matters

It is the reason the cleansing and UAT were ours to run, not the partner's ([[hris-data-cleansing]], [[hris-user-acceptance-testing]]). It also feeds Fiona's case for a November cutover: interface support ends a month after cutover, and a January cutover means paying for extended support ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]). Archiving Staffora history would be a change request ([[what-happens-to-staffora-history-after-2027-03-31]]).

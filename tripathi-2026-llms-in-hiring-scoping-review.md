# Mapping the use of large language models in hiring decisions: a scoping review.

Tripathi A, Tripathi A, Darena F, Mishra PK. Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 2026.

## Summary

A scoping review of research from 2018 to 2026 on large language models in recruitment and selection. The literature is growing fast but uneven: most studies look at early stages such as screening, they mostly measure efficiency and performance, and although ethical risks are widely discussed, few studies test whether the proposed safeguards actually work.

## Why It Matters

The best single answer I have to Marek's "everyone is using it": the evidence is mostly about speed, and almost nothing shows that the safeguards hold in a real workplace. That gap is what the DPIA will have to fill for [[ai-cv-screening-pilot]]. Also useful background for my diploma unit on people practice and technology.

## Caveats

A map of the literature, not a test of any tool. Hireloom's module may not use a large language model at all; the vendor has not said ([[dpia-screening-questions]]). Read from the abstract.

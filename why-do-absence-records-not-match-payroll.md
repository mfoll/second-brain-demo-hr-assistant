# Why Do Absence Records Not Match Payroll?

Why did 3.8% of absence records in December 2025 differ between the HR system and what payroll processed?

## Current Answer

> [!success]
> Mostly timing and coding, not missing data. Absence approved after the cut-off lands in the next run (about 40% of mismatches in January), absence types coded differently in the two systems (about 25%), part days recorded as full days or the reverse (about 20%), and the rest backdated corrections. Fixes: one-to-one absence codes in Talvento, manager self-service, and the absence line in the cut-off checklist. September 2026: 0.9%.

## What remains

Approval after the cut-off. Some of it is legitimate (a sickness absence approved on return to work, after the cut-off), so the target is not zero. Open follow-up: [[can-managers-approve-absence-after-the-cut-off]].

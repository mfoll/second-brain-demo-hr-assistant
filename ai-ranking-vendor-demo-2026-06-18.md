# AI Ranking Vendor Demo 2026-06-18

One-hour online demo of Hireloom's AI ranking module by [[bram-de-wit]] for [[marek-zielinski]], Callum and me.

## What we saw

- A test vacancy for warehouse operatives with 300 sample applications, ranked from 0 to 100, each with its top three factors.
- The calibration option, which re-weights scores using past hiring outcomes for the role family.
- The cut-off setting, off by default.

## What was said

- Marek: in peak, ranking could halve the time coordinators spend reading applications.
- I asked what the top factors would be for a role where almost everyone qualifies. Bram's answer: mostly availability and previous warehouse experience. Both are already screening questions on our form.
- Callum asked whether candidates would be told. Bram: that is the customer's choice.
- Bram could not say on the call what data the model was trained on; promised a written answer.

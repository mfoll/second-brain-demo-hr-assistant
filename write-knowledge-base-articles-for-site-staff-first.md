# Write Knowledge Base Articles for Site Staff First

## Stance

Every knowledge base article should be written for a hub operative or driver reading on a phone at the end of a shift, not for a Head Office colleague at a desk. If it works for them it works for everyone.

## Scope

Employee-facing articles. Manager pages can assume more context, and policy documents are a separate thing ([[hr-policy-library-index]]).

## Why I Think This

- About 3,350 of 4,100 people work at hubs and depots; most have no desk and read on phones.
- [[hollie-pritchard]] reported drivers who could not use the payslip article on a phone because of a wide table.
- Plain writing helps everyone ([[plain-language-wikipedia]]); Head Office staff do not complain about short articles.

## What Would Change My Mind

Deskwell device data showing most article views come from desktop at Head Office. In September 2026, 58% of views were from phones.

## Implications

Answer first, under 300 words, no wide tables, "applies to" line at the top ([[knowledge-base-style-guide]]). This sometimes conflicts with policy owners who want exact wording; the compromise is a plain answer plus a link to the clause.

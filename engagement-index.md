# Engagement Index

Pellmore's headline survey number: the average percentage of favourable answers ("agree" or "strongly agree") across five engagement items.

## The five items

Pride in working here, recommending Pellmore as a place to work, intending to be here in two years, motivation to go beyond what is required, and feeling that the work is worthwhile ([[engagement-survey-2026-questionnaire]]).

## Values

61 in 2024, 64 in 2026 ([[engagement-survey-2026-results-summary]]).

## How to read it

- It is a percentage favourable, not an average score, so neutral answers count against it ([[likert-scale]]).
- A change of 2 or 3 points at group level is modest; at department level it is often noise.
- It is our own construct. It is not comparable with other companies' "engagement" figures unless their items are identical.
- It says how people feel, not why; the "why" comes from the other sections and the team discussion.

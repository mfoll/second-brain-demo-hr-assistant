# Tailoring Safety Training Material to Migrant Farmworkers: An Ergonomic User-Centred Approach.

Caffaro F et al., International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 2020.

## Summary

Lectures often fail to get safety information across to migrant workers because of language and cultural barriers. The authors ran focus groups with trainers and migrant farmworkers, redesigned the training material with more visuals and interaction, and tested it with 20 workers: knowledge improved after training, perceived effort was low and satisfaction high.

## Why It Matters

Many hub operatives and peak temps do not have the local language as their first language. [[ewan-galbraith]] and I discussed this after the kiosk pilot ([[hub-training-kiosk-pilot-results]]): the Manual Handling e-learning is text-heavy. The paper supports asking operatives what they did not understand before buying translations. Added to the 2027 ideas list for [[mandatory-training-compliance]].

## Caveats

Agriculture, not warehousing; 20 participants and a before-and-after design without a control group.

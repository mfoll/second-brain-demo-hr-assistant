# Right To Work Check

The check, before anyone starts, that they are legally allowed to work for us. It is the one pre-start check with no exceptions: no check, no start, whatever the pressure in peak.

## How it works at Pellmore

- Done by [[aoife-brennan-hale]] for every direct hire, after the offer is accepted and before day one. The starter shows original documents or uses the approved online route; Aoife records that the check was done and keeps the evidence in the restricted right-to-work file.
- For agency workers, the agency does the check and confirms it in writing ([[agency-supply-agreement-peak-2026]]).
- Time-limited permissions get a follow-up date in the HR system.
- Evidence is never copied into Hireloom, email threads, trackers or these notes. The [[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]] only counts how many checks are complete.

## Why it matters for planning

In peak the right-to-work queue sets the earliest start date. On 2026-10-20, 9 direct hires were waiting for documents. A starter cleared on a Thursday afternoon misses that week's payroll cut-off and starts the following Monday at the earliest.

## Limits

What counts as acceptable evidence is decided by the HR Advisors; I do not interpret it. See [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]] for how I write about any query.

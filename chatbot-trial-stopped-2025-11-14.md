# Chatbot Trial Stopped 2025-11-14

[[leon-adjei]] stopped the Deskwell Assist trial at the People Services team meeting, with [[deborah-okwuosa]]'s agreement, and announced the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]] to start the following Monday.

## Decision

- Trial ended; the add-on switched off the same afternoon. Licence lapsed 2025-11-30 without a paid extension.
- Reason, in Leon's words in the [[chatbot-trial-closure-report]]: the bot was "only as good as the articles", and the articles were not good enough to put in front of 4,100 people.
- No retry before the rebuild is finished ([[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]).

## Options discussed and rejected

- Paid extension to keep testing: rejected, paying to test bad content.
- A restricted bot for 20 safe questions: rejected, too small to matter.

I take the rebuild tracker and about a third of the writing. Lessons in [[chatbot-trial-lessons-learned]].

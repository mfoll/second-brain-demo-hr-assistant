# Engagement Survey 2026

The 2026 all-staff engagement survey on [[pulsewright]], from design in March to department action plans in November. I do the administration: participant lists, reminders, reporting checks and the action plan tracker. [[jun-takahashi]] does the analysis; the [[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10|Survey Steering Group]] owns the rules.

## Outcome

Every department has an agreed action plan by 2026-11-30. The 31 departments with at least 10 respondents plan from their own results; the 13 below the threshold plan from their site-level results.

## Current State

- Fieldwork ran 2026-05-11 to 2026-05-29: 2,911 of 4,100 responded (71%). Engagement index 64, up from 61 in 2024 ([[engagement-survey-2026-results-summary]], [[engagement-survey-2024-results]]).
- Minimum reporting group set at 10 on 2026-06-10 on [[kasia-wrobel]]'s advice; 13 of 44 departments, mostly small depots, roll up to site level. [[sanjay-mehrotra]] dissented and still wants 5.
- Action plans agreed for 31 of 44 departments as of 2026-10-21: 26 of the 31 with their own results and 5 of the 13 roll-up departments ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).
- The 8 roll-up depots without a plan are all in Warehousing or Road Freight, and peak is the reason they are late, not refusal.

## Scope and Boundaries

In scope: the 2026 survey cycle and its action plans. The June hybrid pulse check ran on the same platform but belongs to [[hybrid-policy-review]]; I only supplied the participant list ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]). Out of scope for my notes: any free-text comment, any result below 10 respondents, and anything that could identify a respondent. Comments stay in Pulsewright.

## Working Context

- Questionnaire: 42 five-point Likert items plus two free-text questions; the engagement index is the mean favourable score on five of them ([[engagement-survey-2026-questionnaire]], [[engagement-index]]).
- Response by division: Head Office 352 (88%), Forwarding 280 (80%), Warehousing 1,380 (73%), Road Freight 899 (62%). Drivers on the road are still the hardest to reach; QR posters at depots helped a bit.
- The 2024 survey used a minimum group of 5 ([[engagement-survey-2024-reporting-rules]]). The change to 10 is why depot managers who saw their own results in 2024 did not this year. That is the conversation [[depot-results-rollup-explainer]] is for.
- [[shireen-qadir]] ran the communications plan ([[survey-communications-plan-2026]]) and wants the "you said, we did" round in January ([[you-said-we-did]]).

## Active Workflows

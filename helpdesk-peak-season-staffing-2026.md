# Helpdesk Peak Season Staffing 2026

How peak hiring is squeezing the helpdesk this autumn, written 2026-10-12 for my one-to-one with [[deborah-okwuosa]].

## The squeeze

- [[warehouse-peak-hiring-2026]] needs 260 seasonal operatives by 2026-11-16 (214 hired on 2026-10-20). [[callum-rourke]] and [[aoife-brennan-hale]] are on recruitment and onboarding most of the week, so each does one helpdesk half day instead of three ([[helpdesk-cover-rota-2026]]).
- At the same time tickets are up: Onboarding and new starters 129 in September, the highest yet; HR systems access 97 with managers new to Talvento approvals.
- Weekly induction days at the hubs ([[hub-induction-day]]) generate questions on the Friday after.

## Effect

First response within target was 91% for September as a whole but fell to 84% in the week of 2026-09-21. Thursday afternoons are hardest: weekly payroll questions after the 12:00 cut-off, including new starters checking they will be paid.

## What I proposed

- [[carys-whitlock]] takes Onboarding category tickets with me reviewing, from November ([[helpdesk-handover-notes-for-carys]]).
- A pinned article for new starters on first pay and the weekly cut-off.
- Plan helpdesk cover for peak in June next year, not August.

Deborah agreed the first two; the third goes to the January 2027 away day.

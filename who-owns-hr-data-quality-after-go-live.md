# Who Owns HR Data Quality After Go-Live?

Once the Talvento programme closes and Fiona's contract ends in March 2027, who is accountable for HR data quality: People Services, the HRIS analyst, the HRBPs, or each data owner?

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Open. In practice I run the monthly scorecard and Rashid fixes, but neither of us is accountable for the result and neither of us can make a manager update their team's records.

## Why It Matters

The [[hr-data-quality-scorecard]] is good now because the programme pushed it. The cleansing showed what happens without an owner: 388 leavers still active, 604 bad cost centres ([[hris-data-cleansing]]). Talvento's self-service moves data entry to managers and employees, which spreads the errors wider.

## Options I have heard

- **[[deborah-okwuosa]] as accountable owner, me as data steward.** Deborah's suggestion in passing. It would make it part of my job description, which I would want written down.
- **Each field has an owner**: Finance for cost centres ([[beatrix-lowe]]), HRBPs for organisation structure, managers for their teams. Closer to [[master-data-management-wikipedia]] thinking; harder to run.
- **The HRIS Steering Group continues as a data governance group** after go-live ([[data-governance-wikipedia]]).

## What would answer it

A decision from Deborah and Adrian before the programme closes, ideally written into the People Services operating model.

# Learning management system

## Summary

An LMS is software for administering, documenting, tracking, reporting, automating and delivering courses and training programmes. The concept grew out of e-learning; the first systems appeared in the late 1990s, and use grew sharply with remote learning during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Why It Matters

Background for the question of whether [[learnmoor]] should survive once [[talvento]] has a learning module ([[should-learnmoor-be-replaced-by-talvento-learning]]). The list of functions in the definition is a decent checklist for comparing the two: administration, tracking and reporting are where Learnmoor is strong, delivery on a warehouse floor is where both are weak.

## Caveats

Generic; much of the article is about higher education rather than workplaces.

# How Do We Measure Self-Service Resolution?

The [[knowledge-base-rebuild]] promises that self-service resolves 30% of queries without a ticket. We do not agree how to measure that, and the figure is about to go into 2027 team objectives.

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Open. Leon's view-based measure gives 22% for September 2026; I think it overstates it. To be settled with Jun Takahashi before the 2027 objectives are set in December.

## Options

- **View-based** ([[leon-adjei]]): views not followed by a same-category ticket within seven days. Easy from [[deskwell]], but counts browsing as success.
- **"Did this answer your question?"** at the bottom of each article. Direct, but needs Deskwell configuration and suffers low response.
- **Repeat-query trend** (mine): tickets per category before and after an article goes live, adjusted for headcount. Slow and noisy, but measures what we care about. My [[diploma-research-project]] tests it on the hybrid articles.
- [[jun-takahashi]] suggested reporting two of these together rather than choosing one.

## Constraints

Counts by category only; no tracking of individual employees' article views beyond what Deskwell does for the seven-day rule, which [[kasia-wrobel]] is checking.

# Employee Engagement

How Pellmore measures and acts on how people feel about working here: the engagement survey, pulse checks, and what happens after the results.

## What we measure

- The annual survey on [[pulsewright]]: 42 items, an [[engagement-index]] from five of them. 2024: 61. 2026: 64 ([[engagement-survey-2026-results-summary]]).
- Pulse checks on a single subject, such as the June 2026 hybrid check ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]).
- Rules for what can be reported: [[minimum-reporting-group]], set at 10 in [[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]].

## What happens after

- Department action plans ([[department-action-plan-template]], [[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]) and the "you said, we did" round ([[you-said-we-did]]).
- Small depots work from site-level results ([[how-can-small-depots-act-on-site-level-results]]).

# Mandatory Training Catalogue

The list of courses every Pellmore employee must hold, with owners, audiences and refresh rules, as published by L&D in January 2026.

## Summary

Four mandatory courses, all in [[learnmoor]]:

| Course | Owner | Who must hold it | Format | Refresh |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Safety Awareness | [[ewan-galbraith]] | everyone, including agency workers | 20-minute e-learning | every year |
| Manual Handling | [[ewan-galbraith]] | everyone; site roles also need a practical | 30-minute e-learning plus 90-minute practical for site roles | every 3 years |
| Data Protection Essentials | [[kasia-wrobel]] | all employees | 25-minute e-learning with a test (80% pass) | every 2 years |
| Respect at Work | [[priyanka-desai]] | all employees | 40-minute e-learning | every 3 years |

## Key Details

- New starters have 30 days to complete all four; site starters do Fire Safety and the Manual Handling practical on their induction day ([[hub-induction-day]]).
- Agency workers hold Fire Safety and Manual Handling only.
- People on long-term absence or parental leave are excluded until they return, then get 30 days.
- Completion is counted per course; the overall figure is the average across the four ([[training-completion-rate]]).

## Why It Matters

This defines the denominator for [[mandatory-training-compliance]]. Most arguments about the numbers turn out to be about who is required to hold which course.

## Caveats

The exclusions for absence are in the policy but not in Learnmoor's report logic, which is why I correct it by hand ([[learnmoor-report-quirks]]).

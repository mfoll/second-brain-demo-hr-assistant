# Knowledge Base Rebuild

Rebuilding the employee-facing knowledge base in [[deskwell]] after the [[helpdesk-chatbot-trial]] showed how bad the content was. Leon owns it; I run the tracker, write about a third of the articles and chase owners.

## Outcome

212 old articles reduced to 138, each with an owner and review date; self-service resolves 30% of queries without a ticket.

## Current State

- 117 of 138 articles published as of 2026-10-20 ([[knowledge-base-rebuild-tracker]]). Of the 21 left, 12 are payroll and absence articles waiting for the [[talvento]] payroll cutover decision ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]).
- Every published article has a named owner and a review date ([[knowledge-article-lifecycle]]).
- Self-service resolution was 22% in September 2026 on Leon's measure; the measure is contested ([[how-do-we-measure-self-service-resolution]]).
- Hybrid articles were rewritten twice: for v1 in April and for [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]] in August.

## Scope and Boundaries

Employee-facing articles and the manager pages. Not the policy documents themselves (owned by [[priyanka-desai]] and the policy library, [[hr-policy-library-index]]), not chatbot work (deferred, [[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]).

## Working Context

The [[knowledge-base-content-audit-2025]] sorted the 212 articles: 61 outdated, 23 contradicting another article, 47 duplicates or near duplicates, 81 usable with edits. We chose to rebuild by question rather than by policy: the article list came from the top ticket themes per category, not from the policy library index. Articles follow the [[knowledge-base-style-guide]]: plain language, written for a hub operative on a phone first ([[write-knowledge-base-articles-for-site-staff-first]]).

Order of work, agreed with Leon in December 2025 ([[knowledge-base-rebuild-priorities]]): pay and leave first, then hybrid (forced by the April launch), then onboarding and leavers, then the long tail.

The rebuild is also the case for my [[diploma-research-project]], which uses monthly ticket counts by category only ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).

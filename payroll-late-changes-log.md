# Payroll Late Changes Log

Monthly count of changes that reached Group Payroll after the cut-off, by cause. Elspeth's team counts; I keep the People Services copy and the causes.

## Summary

- December 2024: 63. The baseline that started the checklist project.
- May 2025: 19, the first month on checklist version 2.
- May 2025 to September 2026 average: 17 a month. Highest 31 (July 2026, go-live month); lowest 9 (February 2026).
- Biggest cause throughout: leavers notified after their last day. Second: new starter details incomplete at cut-off, which peaks in autumn.

## Why It Matters

The measure for [[payroll-cutoff-checklist]]. It also shows that peak hiring is when late changes climb ([[warehouse-peak-hiring-2026]]).

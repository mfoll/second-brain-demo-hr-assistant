# Onboarding Checklist 2024

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 2025-06-27 by the [[onboarding-stage-checklists-2025]]. Kept as the baseline for the [[onboarding-checklist-redesign]].

The single 64-item Word checklist sent to every hiring manager until June 2025. Items ran in no particular order, mixed tasks for HR, IT, Facilities and the manager, and 17 had no clear owner. In a sample of 40 completed checklists from late 2024, only 9 were fully ticked; the most often missed items were the probation mid-point and mandatory training enrolment.

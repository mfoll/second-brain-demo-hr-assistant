# Hybrid Work Profiles and Team Functioning: A Person-Centered Analysis of Hybrid Work Quality.

Margheritti S, Donati S, Gragnano A, Zappalà S, Miglioretti M. *Journal for Person-Oriented Research*, 2026.

## Summary

Uses latent profile analysis on 719 hybrid workers in four organisations to group people by the combination of hybrid work resources they report: workstations at home and in the office, flexible hours, and enabling management. Four profiles emerged. They differed in how strongly people identified with their team, how much they shared knowledge, how confident the team felt in its joint abilities, how satisfied they were with hybrid teamwork, and how efficient they judged the team to be. More balanced combinations of resources went with better perceived team functioning.

## Why It Matters

Read after v2 started, for my own understanding of why team choice might work better than fixed days. It suggests that how resources combine for a team (a usable desk on office days, a manager who enables the arrangement) matters more than any single rule. Relevant to [[does-team-choice-fix-the-desk-shortage]]: a team that picks Wednesday and cannot get desks loses one of those resources.

## Caveats

Cross-sectional and self-reported; four organisations whose sector and country I have not checked. Read from the abstract. Says nothing about anchor days specifically.

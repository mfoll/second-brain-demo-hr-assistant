# Survey Steering Group Reporting Paper June 2026

[[jun-takahashi]]'s options paper on reporting thresholds for the 2026 survey, circulated on 2026-06-05.

## Summary

Models three thresholds against the actual 2026 response counts. At 5, 40 of 44 departments get their own report; at 8, 34; at 10, 31. The 13 departments below 10 are ten depots and three Head Office teams; together they hold 214 respondents.

## Key Details

- Median department size 61; median respondents 43.
- The ten depots below 10 respondents have between 14 and 29 staff each.
- At a threshold of 10, site-level reports for the depots combine two to four depots each, grouped by the depot's parent hub.
- Jun recommended 10 for scores and comments, with a review after the action plan cycle.

## Why It Matters

The numbers behind the decision, and the starting point for the 2027 review ([[should-the-survey-reporting-threshold-be-lowered-to-5]]).

## Caveats

Uses respondents, not headcount; a depot with better response next year might clear 10 without any change in rules.

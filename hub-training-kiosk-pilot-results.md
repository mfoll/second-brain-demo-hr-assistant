# Hub Training Kiosk Pilot Results

My two-page summary of the eight-week kiosk pilot at Hub West, 2026-03-02 to 2026-04-24, written for [[ewan-galbraith]], [[yetunde-balogun]] and [[conrad-baptiste]].

## Summary

Three touchscreen kiosks in the Hub West break areas, logging in with employee number and PIN, plus 30 minutes of paid time per shift week for mandatory e-learning. Manual Handling e-learning completion at Hub West rose from 79% to 91%; overall mandatory completion there from 82% to 92%.

## Key Details

- 1,214 e-learning sessions started on the kiosks in eight weeks; 71% on night and late shifts.
- Main issues: the Data Protection Essentials test timing out on kiosks (fixed by Learnmoor in week 3), and headphones going missing.
- Team leaders said paid time mattered more than the kiosks; operatives said not having to use their own phone data mattered most.
- Hub North and Hub South, as comparison, moved by 1 and 2 points over the same weeks.

## Why It Matters

The strongest evidence we have for [[why-is-warehouse-training-completion-lagging]]: access and time, not reluctance. It is the basis of the 2027 kiosk bid ([[training-budget-2026]]) and of my stance in [[reminder-emails-do-not-move-training-completion]].

## Caveats

One hub, eight weeks, with attention on it; some of the rise is novelty. No control for the reminder campaign running at the same time.

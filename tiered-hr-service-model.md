# Tiered HR Service Model

The way People Services splits employee questions by how much expertise they need, so each goes to the cheapest level that can answer it properly.

## Tiers at Pellmore

- **Tier 0, self-service**: the knowledge base and manager self-service in [[talvento]]. Target: 30% of questions ([[self-service-deflection]]).
- **Tier 1, helpdesk**: HR Shared Services on the [[deskwell]] rota answers factual and process questions, about 85% of tickets.
- **Tier 2, advisors**: [[priyanka-desai]] on policy, [[tomas-ferreira-lund]] on employee relations process, Group Payroll on pay calculations.
- **Tier 3, HR Business Partners**: [[hollie-pritchard]], [[sanjay-mehrotra]] and [[ingrid-salvesen]] for their divisions: exceptions, adjustments, organisational issues.

Occupational health sits outside the tiers as a contracted service reached through managers and HR Business Partners ([[occupational-health-service-overview]]).

## Why it matters

Explains why the helpdesk does not answer some questions (exceptions, individual hybrid arrangements) and why the knowledge base rebuild is about moving tier 1 questions to tier 0, not cutting the helpdesk.

## Caveat

The tiers are how we describe it, not a formal structure in any policy.

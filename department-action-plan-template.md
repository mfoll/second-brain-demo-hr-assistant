# Department Action Plan Template

The one-page template managers use to agree survey actions with their team, revised by me and [[jun-takahashi]] in June 2026 from the 2024 version.

## Summary

Three boxes: what the results say (two strengths, two things to work on), what the team will do (at most three actions, each with an owner and date), and how the team will know it worked. A note at the top says plans come from a team discussion, not from the manager alone.

## Key Details

- A roll-up version for the 13 departments below the threshold starts from site results and asks "which of these sound like us?", so teams can claim or reject site findings.
- Action owners are roles, not names, to keep the tracker clean.
- Optional fourth box: something the team needs from someone else, routed to the HRBP.

## Why It Matters

Shorter than 2024's four pages, which [[sanjay-mehrotra]] said nobody filled in properly. The roll-up version is my attempt at [[how-can-small-depots-act-on-site-level-results]].

# Internal Comms For HR Changes

What [[shireen-qadir]] needs from People Services before any HR change goes to staff. Built from the hybrid policy launches and the survey.

1. One sentence on what changes and from when.
2. Who it affects, by group and rough number (for example "about 1,150 hybrid-eligible roles").
3. What people need to do, if anything, and by when.
4. Where to find more: the Deskwell article, ready and published before the message goes out.
5. Who managers ask: their HRBP, not the helpdesk inbox.
6. Channels: email for office staff; shift huddle script and noticeboard poster for hubs and depots; in-cab message for drivers.
7. Date in her calendar at least ten working days ahead.

Lessons:

- When hybrid v1 went out on 2026-04-01, the FAQ was published the same morning and tickets spiked to 23% of volume in April ([[hybrid-policy-guidance]]). For v2 the articles were live a week before, which is part of what my research project looks at ([[diploma-research-project]]).
- Plain words beat policy words. Shireen will cut "in accordance with" every time.

# Burnout Reading Notes

Notes from my reading in August 2026, after the survey's workload and wellbeing section came out lowest (52 favourable).

## What I have read

- [[hulsegge-2020-shift-work-burnout-and-distress]]: the schedule itself mattered less than whether people were satisfied with it and how much it hit their private life.
- [[presenteeism]]: lower absence is not proof of a healthier workforce.
- [[psychological-safety]] and [[du-2025-leader-mindfulness-and-psychological-safety]]: whether people feel able to say they are struggling.

## What I think it means for us

- Rota notice and some say over shifts may matter more than shift type. That is the most common Warehousing action theme already ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).
- Our survey has five workload items and no burnout measure. I should not describe a low workload score as burnout.
- Anything about individual wellbeing goes to managers and occupational health, never into these notes.

## Where this might go

A Unit 6 essay on what engagement survey data can and cannot say about wellbeing, or a pulse check on workload if the People Committee asks for one ([[should-pulse-checks-run-quarterly]]).

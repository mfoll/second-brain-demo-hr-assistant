# Candidate Experience Survey 2025

The annual summary of Hireloom's post-interview candidate survey for calendar year 2025, which I compiled in January 2026.

## Summary

612 responses from about 3,100 candidates interviewed (20%). Booking and communication scored well; the weakest item was hearing back after an unsuccessful interview.

## Key Details

- "Booking my interview was easy": 4.5 out of 5.
- "I knew what to expect on the day": 4.1.
- "I heard back in a reasonable time": 3.4.
- "The feedback I received was useful" (unsuccessful candidates only, 288 responses): 2.6.
- Most common free-text theme among unsuccessful candidates: a generic rejection with no reason (about one comment in three).
- Results by division only; no site breakdown, because several depots had fewer than 10 responses.

## Why It Matters

Behind my view that rejection emails should give a reason category ([[rejection-emails-should-give-a-reason-category]]) and the template refresh planned after peak. Also a check on the [[interview-scheduling-overhaul]].

## Caveats

Self-selected respondents; unsuccessful candidates answer less often. Free-text was read by me for themes, not coded formally.

# Induction Should Cap At 30 Not 40

## Stance

Outside peak, hub induction sessions would work better capped at 30. The cap of 40 is set by the safety walk-round (two groups of 20) and is fine for safety, but the systems and pay slot does not work well with 40 people.

## Why I Think This

- In sessions over 30, the systems slot overruns by 15 to 20 minutes: more people stuck on the Talvento app log-in, more questions about first pay.
- Feedback for "I know when and how I will be paid" averaged 4.5 in sessions under 30 and 3.9 in sessions over 30 (autumn 2026, sessions of 10 or more only).
- Capacity is not the constraint: we used 68% of places in the last four weeks of peak ([[peak-induction-capacity-maths]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

A second People Services person in the systems slot would solve the same problem without a lower cap. Or the 2027 peak forecast being much higher.

## Implications

Raise it at the December review with the hub managers, as a business-as-usual cap, not a peak one. Low confidence: one season of data, and I run that slot myself, so I notice its problems more than others.

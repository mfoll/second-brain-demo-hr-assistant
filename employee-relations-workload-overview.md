# Employee Relations Workload Overview

How employee relations work reaches People Services, described as workload and process only. No case is ever described here.

## Volume

- Employee relations tickets in [[deskwell]]: 15 to 25 a month (21 in September 2026). These are first contacts from managers or employees, routed to the restricted queue for [[tomas-ferreira-lund]].
- Tomás carries roughly 25 to 35 open matters at any time, according to his own monthly count to [[deborah-okwuosa]]. Counts only.

## Process support I give

- Booking rooms and video links for formal meetings, using the meeting reference, not names, in calendar titles.
- Sending template letters that Tomás has prepared and approved.
- Tracking deadlines in aggregate for his monthly report.

## Rules

- No case details in chat, email subject lines, shared documents or this vault ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).
- If a helpdesk ticket turns out to be ER, I recategorise it to the restricted queue and do not reply ([[triage-a-deskwell-ticket]]).
- Knowledge base articles on ER cover process only: what a grievance or disciplinary process involves, who to contact, timescales.

## Pressure points

Peak season and restructures both raise ER volume; Tomás has no deputy, and the HR Business Partners cover his leave.

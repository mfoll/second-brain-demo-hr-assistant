# Master data management

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes master data management as the discipline in which business and IT work together on the uniformity, accuracy, stewardship and accountability of an organisation's shared master data, and covers stewardship roles and the idea of a single trusted record.

## Why It Matters

Talvento is now Pellmore's master record for people, and cost centres are Finance's master data. The stewardship idea, one named owner per kind of data, is one of the options in [[who-owns-hr-data-quality-after-go-live]].

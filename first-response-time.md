# First Response Time

The time from a ticket being raised to the first reply from a person on the helpdesk, measured in working days.

## How we measure it

- Clock starts when [[deskwell]] creates the ticket; stops at the first public reply by a helpdesk member. Automatic acknowledgements do not count.
- Working days, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
- Target: two working days for 90% of tickets ([[what-first-response-target-should-the-helpdesk-use]]). 48% in March 2025, 91% in September 2026.

## What it does not tell you

A fast first response can be a holding reply. It says nothing about whether the question was answered or whether the person came back with the same question. That is why I want repeat queries measured too ([[repeat-queries-matter-more-than-first-response]]).

## Related terms

- Resolution time: until the ticket is closed.
- [[self-service-deflection]]: questions that never became tickets.

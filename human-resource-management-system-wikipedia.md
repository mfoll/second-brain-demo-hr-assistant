# Human resource management system

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Explains that an HR management system, also called an HRIS or human capital management system, is software bringing together HR processes and data: employee records, payroll, recruitment, benefits, time and attendance, performance and training records. It describes the usual modules and how such systems moved from on-premises installations to cloud services.

## Why It Matters

I read it in my first week on the programme to get the vocabulary straight, since people at Pellmore used HRIS, HR system and Talvento interchangeably. It also made clear how much of what [[staffora]] does is standard and how much is our own habits. Pellmore's move is the usual on-premises to cloud path ([[talvento-hris-migration]]).

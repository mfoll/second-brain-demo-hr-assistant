# People Committee 2026-07-15

Monthly People Committee, chaired by [[adrian-fairclough]]. The main item was the three-month review of fixed anchor days. The committee partly reversed the February rule. I attended for the hybrid item only, to present the helpdesk slide.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Decision: still three office days a week for hybrid-eligible staff, but only Tuesday fixed company-wide; each team chooses its other two days. Policy v2 in force from 2026-09-01. The COO accepted the change on condition that anchor-day attendance is reported to her each quarter.

## What was presented

The [[hybrid-review-paper-2026-07]], introduced by [[priyanka-desai]]:

- Desks at 104% on anchor days in April and May ([[head-office-desk-occupancy-2026]]).
- Hybrid questions 23% of helpdesk tickets in April, 11% in June (my slide, from [[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).
- The June 2026 pulse check on hybrid work ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]), presented in aggregate by [[jun-takahashi]]: 71% of respondents preferred one fixed day plus team choice.
- Managers' views gathered by [[ingrid-salvesen]]: keep one fixed day for meetings.

## Discussion

- [[rosamund-oyelaran]] argued to keep three fixed days and fix the desks instead. [[theo-varkey]] confirmed there is no budget for the extra floor Facilities costed.
- [[sanjay-mehrotra]] and [[hollie-pritchard]] raised fairness for site-based staff; noted, not decided ([[should-site-based-roles-get-a-flexibility-offer]]).
- Adrian asked whether fixing only Tuesday would bunch demand on Wednesday. Priyanka proposed that teams' choices keep each floor under 95% of desks; agreed.

## Decisions

1. Three office days a week unchanged.
2. Tuesday is the only company-wide anchor day from 2026-09-01; teams choose two more days, recorded in Talvento.
3. Policy v2, revised manager guide and FAQ to be published by 2026-08-31 ([[hybrid-work-policy-v2]]).
4. Anchor-day attendance reported to the COO quarterly, first report covering September ([[anchor-day-attendance-report-q3-2026]]); reporting must follow the minimum group of 10 ([[how-should-anchor-day-attendance-be-reported]]).

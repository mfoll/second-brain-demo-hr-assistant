# Reminder Emails Do Not Move Training Completion

## Stance

Automated or bulk reminder emails to people who have not completed mandatory training barely work. Team lists to managers, paid time and somewhere to do the course work.

## Scope

Mandatory e-learning at Pellmore. Classroom bookings are different: a reminder the day before does reduce no-shows.

## Why I Think This

- Three reminder rounds in January and February 2026 moved completion about one point each ([[mandatory-training-push-launch-2026-01-12]]).
- The monthly manager lists moved Head Office and Forwarding into the mid-90s by May.
- Hub West went from 82% to 92% in eight weeks with kiosks and paid time ([[hub-training-kiosk-pilot-results]]). Most hub operatives never see an email anyway.

## What Would Change My Mind

A reminder that comes from the person's own team leader and includes a time slot on shift might work. That is really the manager route by another name.

## Implications

The [[chase-mandatory-training-before-month-end]] procedure goes through managers, and I stopped the bulk reminders in March. Learnmoor's automatic expiry notices stay on because they cost nothing.

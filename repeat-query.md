# Repeat Query

A helpdesk ticket from an employee who had already raised a ticket in the same Deskwell category in the previous 30 days.

## Why it matters

A repeat suggests the first answer, or the knowledge base article behind it, did not settle the question. Repeats are the outcome measure in my [[diploma-research-project]].

## How it is counted

[[leon-adjei]] computes a monthly count per category inside Deskwell. I see only the count, never the tickets or who raised them ([[research-dataset-specification]]).

## Limits

- The 30-day window is a choice; a person asking about hybrid in April and again in September is not counted.
- A repeat may be a new, related question rather than a failed answer.
- Category errors at triage can create or hide repeats.

# HRIS Steering Group 2026-06-17

Last Steering Group before core HR go-live. Two decisions.

- **Go-live confirmed** for 2026-06-29 after UAT sign-off ([[uat-sign-off-2026-06-12]]).
- **Payroll cutover rule.** Payroll stays on Staffora until two consecutive monthly parallel runs have no unexplained differences; cutover then at the next monthly payroll, date to be set by this group. Weekly payroll compared on two sample weeks a month. [[elspeth-harcourt]] had asked for three clean runs; [[fiona-mcbride]] argued for two; two plus weekly samples was the compromise ([[payroll-parallel-run]]).
- "Unexplained" defined: a difference whose cause is not known, or is known and not yet fixed.
- I take over the differences log from Rashid ([[parallel-run-differences-log-2026]]).

This is the rule now being tested in [[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]].

# Anchor Days

Days of the week when a hybrid team, or the whole organisation, is expected in the office at the same time.

## Definition

An anchor day fixes when people are together, as opposed to a quota that fixes only how many office days they do. A policy can combine both: Pellmore's v2 has a quota of three days, one company-wide anchor day (Tuesday) and two team anchor days chosen by each team.

## Why the distinction matters

- **Collaboration.** Anchor days guarantee overlap; a quota alone does not.
- **Space.** Company-wide anchor days concentrate demand on the same days, which undoes the saving that shared desks rely on. Head Office went to 104% of desks under three company-wide anchor days ([[head-office-desk-occupancy-2026]]).
- **Fairness and fit.** Fixed days suit full-time staff with a five-day week; they do not map cleanly onto part-time or compressed hours, which is why v2 added a pro rata rule.

## At Pellmore

- v1 (2026-04-01): three company-wide anchor days, Tuesday to Thursday ([[hybrid-work-policy-v1]]).
- v2 (2026-09-01): one company-wide anchor day plus two team anchor days ([[hybrid-work-policy-v2]]).
- Attendance on anchor days is now reported quarterly ([[how-should-anchor-day-attendance-be-reported]]).

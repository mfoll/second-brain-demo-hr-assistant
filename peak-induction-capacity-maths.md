# Peak Induction Capacity Maths

Working out, before weekly induction started, whether induction places would limit peak hiring. Updated on 2026-10-20 with actuals.

## Theory

- Four hubs, one session a week each from 2026-09-07 to 2026-11-12: ten weeks.
- Caps: 40 at Hub North, Hub East and Hub South; 32 at Hub West ([[induction-room-capacity-by-hub]]). That is 152 places a week, 1,520 over ten weeks.
- Peak needs 260, plus around 20 non-seasonal warehouse starters a month. So on paper, capacity is about five times demand.

## Practice

- Trainer leave and room clashes cancelled or shortened 7 of the 28 sessions up to 2026-10-20. Realistic capacity is closer to 120 places a week.
- Starters cluster: most direct hires are cleared mid-week and want to start the next Monday, so Monday sessions fill and later ones are thin.
- Utilisation in the last four weeks: 68%.

## Conclusion

Induction is not the bottleneck for peak; applicants at Hub North and Hub West are ([[how-do-we-close-the-peak-hiring-gap]]). The real constraint is trainers, which is why the buddy scheme paid the price ([[buddy-scheme-pause-2026-09-07]]). The cap of 40 still matters for safety and must not be exceeded to catch up.

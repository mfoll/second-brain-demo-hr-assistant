# Should We Switch On Hireloom AI Ranking?

## Question

Should Pellmore switch on Hireloom's AI ranking module for high-volume operative roles, and if so under what conditions?

## Current State

> [!question]
> Open. Nothing can happen before a full DPIA, which Kasia Wróbel required on 2026-07-08 and which nobody has yet been asked to own. Marek Zieliński will decide in January 2027 whether to start it.

## What would have to be true for "yes"

- The DPIA is approved by [[kasia-wrobel]] and the vendor has answered the five questions in [[dpia-screening-questions]].
- Ranking only sorts; the cut-off stays off and a person reads every application ([[automated-decision-making]]).
- A test on past applications shows no meaningful difference in who ranks high by gender, age band or disability, for groups large enough to measure.
- A real time saving, measured from 2026 peak Hireloom timestamps rather than assumed.

## Positions

- [[marek-zielinski]]: in favour of a trial, mainly for peak.
- Kasia: neutral on the idea, firm on the DPIA.
- Me: against for warehouse roles, open for others ([[ai-ranking-is-not-worth-it-for-warehouse-roles]]).

# Graduate Scheme Offer Tracker 2026

The small tracker Callum and I kept for the 18 graduate offers between 2026-02-02 and 2026-03-27.

## Summary

18 offers, 16 accepted, 2 declined. Median time from assessment centre to offer: 4 working days.

## Key Details

- Offers by stream: operations 11, finance 4, commercial 3.
- Pre-start checks completed for all 16 by 2026-07-31.
- Start date 2026-09-07 for all; first salary on 2026-09-26 (monthly cut-off 15 September).
- Keep-warm contact between offer and start: three emails and an invitation to a June open day; 14 of 16 attended.

## Why It Matters

Showed that a long gap between offer and start (five to six months) did not lose anyone after acceptance, which the 2025 intake had (two withdrawals in summer).

## Caveats

The tracker held names during the campaign; it was cleared to counts on 2026-09-07 as agreed in [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]].

# Agency Versus Direct Hiring Comparison

Notes comparing the two routes for peak, prepared for the shortfall discussion. Counts from the [[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]]; no cost figures, which Theo Varkey holds.

| | Direct | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Hired by 2026-10-20 | 128 | 86 |
| Application or request to start | about 11 working days | about 5 working days |
| Drop-out before start | 13% | day-one no-shows 8% |
| Who does right-to-work check | Aoife | the agency |
| Payroll | Pellmore weekly payroll, needs Talvento entry | agency pays |
| Induction | our hub induction | our hub induction |
| Stays after peak | about a third in 2025 | rarely |

## What it means

Agency is faster and puts no load on Aoife or the payroll cut-off, which is why it is the realistic way to close the last part of the gap. Direct is cheaper per head (Conrad's reason for the 60/40 split) and is where next year's permanent hires come from. My preferred mix is in [[how-do-we-close-the-peak-hiring-gap]].

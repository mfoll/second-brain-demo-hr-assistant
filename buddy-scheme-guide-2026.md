# Buddy Scheme Guide 2026

The guide given to every trained buddy at the start of the [[onboarding-buddy-scheme]], written by Yetunde Balogun and me.

## Summary

What a buddy does and does not do: answers practical questions, introduces people, checks in weekly for six weeks and then fortnightly to day 90. Not a mentor, not a manager, never involved in probation.

## Key Details

- Commitment: about an hour a week for the first six weeks.
- First-day lunch paid from the People Services budget.
- Topics buddies handle: systems, expenses, rota or hybrid days, where things are, who to ask.
- Topics they refer on: pay problems (helpdesk), anything about performance or health (manager or HR Advisor).
- Buddies do not report on conversations. The only data collected is whether contact happened, by survey at day 45 and day 90.

## Why It Matters

The guide is the basis of the 90-minute buddy training in Learnmoor and will be reused for the January 2027 restart.

## Caveats

Written for office starters only.

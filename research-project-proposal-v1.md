# Research Project Proposal Version 1

My first Unit 7 proposal, submitted to Harriet on 2026-09-11 and withdrawn on 2026-09-17. Superseded by [[research-project-proposal]].

## Summary

Same question, but the method was to code a random sample of 200 hybrid tickets for "was this answered by an existing article" and "did the employee ask again", using ticket text, with names redacted by me.

## Why It Was Withdrawn

[[kasia-wrobel]] pointed out on 2026-09-15 that redacting names does not anonymise ticket text: people describe their team, their site, their caring arrangements and their health in hybrid requests. Reading 200 of them for a personal qualification was not proportionate when counts could answer most of the question. Harriet agreed. I agreed too, after a day of being annoyed about losing the richer data. The lesson went into [[aggregate-data-is-enough-for-my-research]].

# Hybrid Work Policy v1

Version 1 of the hybrid work policy, in force from 2026-04-01 to 2026-08-31. Superseded by [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]], which shows the two versions side by side. Kept for the record of what staff were told in April.

## Summary

Implemented the [[exec-committee-hybrid-decision-2026-02-24]]: hybrid-eligible staff (about 1,150 roles) work in the office three fixed anchor days a week, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and may work from home on Monday and Friday. Exceptions through the manager with the HR Business Partner. Review after three months.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for the [[hybrid-policy-review]]. Its gaps (part-time and compressed-hours staff, no desk guarantee) explain much of the April helpdesk spike ([[helpdesk-hybrid-ticket-themes-april-2026]]).

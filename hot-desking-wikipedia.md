# Hot desking

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Hot desking is an office arrangement where any worker can use any desk rather than having their own, so several people share the same desk over a day or week. The main motive is saving space and cost, which the article puts at up to 30% in some cases.

## Why It Matters

Read the week after the anchor-day launch, when Head Office hit 104% of desks. Hot desking only saves space if people are not all in on the same days; fixed Tuesday to Thursday attendance removed exactly the spread it depends on. That argument went into the [[hybrid-review-paper-2026-07]] and supports [[does-team-choice-fix-the-desk-shortage]].

## Caveats

The 30% saving is a general claim, not a figure for our building.

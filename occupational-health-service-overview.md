# Occupational Health Service Overview

What the helpdesk needs to know about occupational health: the service, the route and the turnaround. Never individual referrals; those stay between the manager, the HR Business Partner, the employee and the provider.

## The service

Calderline Occupational Health, contract to 2027-03-31 ([[occupational-health-service-contract]]); account lead [[sunita-ramaswamy]]. Management referrals, pre-placement questionnaires for safety-critical roles, health surveillance for workshop staff, a manager advice line.

## The route

1. A manager discusses a referral with the employee and their HR Business Partner.
2. The manager or HR Business Partner submits it on Calderline's portal.
3. First appointment offered within 5 working days of a complete referral; report within 2 working days of the appointment.

## What the helpdesk does

- Answers "how does it work?" by linking the two knowledge base articles (one for employees, one for managers).
- Routes anything about a specific person straight to the HR Business Partner, without asking for details ([[ticket-triage]]).
- Handles portal access requests for new managers and HR Business Partners.

## What the helpdesk does not do

Book appointments, see reports or discuss anyone's health. If a ticket contains health details, it goes to the HR Business Partner and the ticket text is not repeated anywhere ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

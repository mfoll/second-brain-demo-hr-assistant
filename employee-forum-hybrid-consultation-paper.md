# Employee Forum Hybrid Consultation Paper

The consultation paper [[priyanka-desai]] sent to the Employee Forum on 2025-11-19, a week before the first session.

## Summary

Sets out why hybrid working was being reviewed (inconsistent practice since 2022, new starters reporting little contact with colleagues, Head Office desks reduced from 610 to 520 in the 2025 refurbishment) and asks for views on three options: two flexible office days; three fixed anchor days; three days chosen by teams. States that the Executive Committee will decide after consultation.

## Why It Matters

The options framed the whole debate. The paper says the 2025 refurbishment removed 90 desks, which nobody connected to anchor days until April.

## Caveats

No desk modelling in the paper; it assumed attendance would spread.

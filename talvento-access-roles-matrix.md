# Talvento Access Roles Matrix

The table of Talvento roles, what each can see and change, and who approves granting it. Version 1.2, approved by Deborah and [[kasia-wrobel]] in June 2026, updated in September.

## Summary

Nine roles: employee, line manager, HR assistant, HR advisor, HRBP, people analytics, payroll, HRIS admin, auditor (read-only, time-limited). Each role has a scope (self, team, division, all). Pay data visible to HRBP, payroll and HRIS admin only; HR cases to HR advisor and HRBP only. People analytics sees de-identified extracts, not records.

## Why It Matters

Every access request I handle is checked against it ([[role-based-access]]). The pre-go-live review removed pay access from 23 Staffora users who no longer needed it.

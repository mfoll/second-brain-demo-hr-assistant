# Helpdesk Hybrid Ticket Review 2026-04-28

One-hour review of April's hybrid tickets with [[leon-adjei]], [[priyanka-desai]], [[callum-rourke]] and me. I brought counts and themes only ([[helpdesk-hybrid-ticket-themes-april-2026]]).

## Findings

- Hybrid and flexible working was 23% of April's tickets (about 300 of 1,310), against 4% in the first quarter ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).
- A third came from part-time or compressed-hours staff; a quarter were about desks; the rest split between travel, exceptions and "does this apply to me".
- First response slipped to 82% within two working days in April, from 90% in March.

## Decisions

- The hybrid holding answers become permanent FAQ entries (done 2026-04-14 for four; two more by 2026-05-08).
- Exceptions and adjustment requests are routed straight to the HR Business Partner, not answered on the helpdesk.
- Priyanka takes the part-time gap into the three-month review as a policy issue, not a FAQ issue. It became the pro rata clause in [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]].
- I keep monthly hybrid counts for the review paper.

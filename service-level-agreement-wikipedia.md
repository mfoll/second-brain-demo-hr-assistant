# Service-level agreement

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines a service-level agreement as an agreement, often a contract, between a provider and a customer that sets out aspects of the service such as quality, availability and responsibilities. SLAs usually contain service-level objectives for defined indicators, for example turnaround time or first-call resolution.

## Why It Matters

[[leon-adjei]] wanted a helpdesk "SLA". Reading this, I suggested we call the two-day first response a target, not an agreement: there is no contract with employees and nothing happens when we miss it. The distinction between an indicator (first response time) and an objective (two working days, 90% of tickets) is how the monthly report now reads ([[first-response-time]]).

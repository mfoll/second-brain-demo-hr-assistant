# Depot Results Roll-Up Explainer

The text I drafted for the 13 departments whose results roll up to site level, sent through HRBPs on 2026-06-24. Reviewed by [[shireen-qadir]] and [[kasia-wrobel]].

## The message

Thank you to everyone at your depot who took part. This year, results are shown only for groups of at least 10 people who answered. Your depot had fewer than 10 respondents, so your answers are included in the results for your site, together with one to three nearby depots.

This is different from 2024, when the limit was 5. We changed it because, in small teams, people could sometimes work out who said what, and the survey promised that nobody would be identified ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]).

What this means for you:

- Your site results are still your results, combined with your neighbours'.
- Your HRBP can run a short session with your team to go through the site findings and decide which ones apply to you ([[action-planning-workshop-hub-east-2026-07-08]]).
- Your team still agrees an action plan by 2026-11-30 ([[department-action-plan-template]]).
- The limit will be reviewed before the 2027 survey.

## Notes to self

The section on why we changed was the most-asked-about part. Two managers asked whether Pulsewright knows who answered: it holds a key so HR data can be used for cuts, but nobody sees individual answers ([[limesurvey-participant-settings-docs]] helped me explain the difference).

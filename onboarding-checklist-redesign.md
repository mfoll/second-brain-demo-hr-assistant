# Onboarding Checklist Redesign

Replace the single 64-item onboarding checklist, which nobody finished, with short checklists per stage, each item owned by a named role. Run by me with [[aoife-brennan-hale]] for [[deborah-okwuosa]].

## Outcome

Reached on 2025-06-27: the 64-item checklist was replaced by three stage checklists (pre-start, day one, first 90 days) owned by named roles.

## Current State

- Completed and in use: [[onboarding-stage-checklists-2025]]. The old list is archived as [[onboarding-checklist-2024]].
- The day-one checklist was adapted for hub starters in 2026 and is part of [[run-a-hub-induction-day]].
- Moving the checklists into Talvento tasks is deferred ([[should-the-onboarding-checklist-live-in-talvento]]).

## Working Context

When I joined, the checklist was one long Word document sent to managers. A sample of 40 completed checklists from late 2024 showed only 9 fully completed, and 17 items had no clear owner (IT, Facilities, manager or HR?). We split it by stage and gave every item one owner by role, never by name, so the list survives staff changes ([[onboarding-checklist-ownership-map]]). 64 items became 38: 14 pre-start, 11 day one, 13 first 90 days. 26 items were duplicates or no longer done.

Deborah signed it off at the People Services meeting on 2025-06-25 ([[onboarding-checklist-sign-off-2025-06-25]]).

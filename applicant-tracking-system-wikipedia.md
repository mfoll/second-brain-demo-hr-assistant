# Applicant tracking system

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes an applicant tracking system as software that handles recruitment electronically: posting vacancies, collecting applications, filtering and ranking candidates, and managing communication. Covers keyword-based filtering of CVs, newer machine-learning ranking, and the concern that automated filters can drop suitable candidates whose CVs do not match the expected wording.

## Why It Matters

Background before the Hireloom AI ranking demo in June 2026 ([[ai-ranking-vendor-demo-2026-06-18]]). Useful to separate what [[hireloom]] already does (workflow, scheduling, templates) from the ranking step that [[ai-cv-screening-pilot]] would add.

## Caveats

Generic; no evidence about any particular product. The content on filtering is short and partly unsourced.

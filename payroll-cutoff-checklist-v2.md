# Payroll Cut-Off Checklist v2

The checklist People Services uses before every payroll cut-off, agreed with Group Payroll on 2025-04-25 and updated for Talvento on 2026-07-01. Replaces [[payroll-cutoff-checklist-v1]].

## Summary

Two separate lists.

- **Monthly** (14 lines): starters, leavers, hours and contract changes, absence report check, one-off payments (by the 12th), maternity and paternity start and end dates, secondments, "nothing to send" confirmation. Each line names an owner and a day.
- **Weekly** (8 lines): starters for this week complete in Talvento, leavers, hours changes, absence report check for depots and hubs, agency-to-direct transfers, "nothing to send". All by Thursday 12:00.

The July 2026 update replaced "send form to payroll" with "keyed in Talvento and Staffora" for the double-keying period.

## Why It Matters

The core of [[coordinate-payroll-and-absence]]. Will need another update the week of payroll cutover ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]).

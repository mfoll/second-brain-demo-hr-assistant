# Chatbot Trial Closure Report

Two-page closure report by [[leon-adjei]] for [[deborah-okwuosa]], dated 2025-11-14. I drafted the results section.

## Summary

Recommends stopping the Deskwell Assist trial and rebuilding the knowledge base first. Gives the test result (62 of 150 correct and complete, 41%), the trace of failures to outdated, contradictory or missing articles, staff feedback from the internal trial, and the cost of a paid licence, which it does not recommend.

## Why It Matters

The official record of why the chatbot stopped, and the mandate for the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]]: the report sets the 138-article target and the rule that every article has an owner and a review date.

## Key Details

- Staff feedback (14 People Services users): liked the article citations; disliked confident answers on pay.
- Recommendation 3: revisit a chatbot "when the knowledge base has completed one full review cycle". That is the condition I use in [[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]].

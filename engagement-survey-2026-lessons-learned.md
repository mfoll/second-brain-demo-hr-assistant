# Engagement Survey 2026 Lessons Learned

What I would do differently for the 2027 survey, written in October 2026 while I still remember. For the 2027 timeline.

## Before fieldwork

- Decide the reporting threshold before launch, not after. We were right to look at real numbers in June, but managers spent three weeks expecting 2024-style reports. In 2027 the threshold should be in the invitation.
- Cut the participant file late and run a starter top-up ([[engagement-survey-launch-2026-05-11]]).
- Check one QR slip per depot batch.
- Talvento will hold the participant data in 2027, so department codes should be cleaner than Staffora's.

## During fieldwork

- Shift-huddle scripts beat emails ([[survey-communications-plan-2026]]).
- The in-cab driver message needs to go on day one, not day ten.
- Daily site figures to HRBPs only, never below site level. That held and nobody complained.

## After

- Facilitated sessions for roll-up departments from the start, before peak ([[how-can-small-depots-act-on-site-level-results]]).
- The tracker should exist on day one of the results, with the 3-month check dates filled in ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).
- Every report through [[check-survey-reports-before-release]]: it caught two filter combinations below 10 in June.

# Presenteeism

## Summary

Presenteeism, or working while sick, is the practice or culture of continuing to work despite reduced productivity or harmful consequences, often because of illness, injury or exhaustion. Working while contagious adds the risk of spreading illness through the workplace.

## Why It Matters

A useful counterweight to the absence work in [[absence-data-reconciliation]]: low recorded absence is not the same as a well workforce. When the survey's workload items dropped at hubs, someone in the People Committee pointed out that sickness absence there had fallen too. Read with the [[hulsegge-2020-shift-work-burnout-and-distress|shift-work paper]], it is why I would not read lower absence as good news on its own ([[burnout-reading-notes]]).

## Caveats

General overview. We have no measure of presenteeism at Pellmore, and I am not proposing one.

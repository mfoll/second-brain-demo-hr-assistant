# Peak Hiring 2025 Review

The review of 2025 peak hiring that Marek Zieliński and I wrote for Conrad Baptiste in January 2026.

## Summary

236 of 250 seasonal operatives hired (94%); 55% agency. 19 direct starters missed their first weekly pay because their record was not in Staffora before the Thursday 12:00 cut-off.

## Key Details

- Hires by month: August 38, September 92, October 71, November 35.
- Each hub ran its own induction; length varied from half a day to two days.
- Offer-to-start drop-outs among direct hires: 16%.
- The 19 pay slips generated 31 helpdesk tickets and an emergency payment run for 11 of them.
- Recommendations: a standard induction day at every hub, a weekly Talvento or Staffora check before cut-off, more direct hiring to reduce cost.

## Why It Matters

All three recommendations became 2026 practice: [[hub-induction-day]], the Wednesday check ([[how-do-we-stop-new-starters-missing-first-pay]]), and the 60/40 split in the [[peak-hiring-plan-2026]].

## Caveats

Drop-out reasons come from exit emails, which about half of withdrawals answered.

## Scope and Boundaries

The units, assignments and the study group. Assignments may draw on Pellmore only in anonymised or aggregate form and only after Deborah has seen the plan; the [[guild-level-5-diploma|programme handbook]] requires an ethics form for any workplace data. I took Unit 4 before Unit 3 because the Unit 3 case study on grievance handling made me nervous about using real work examples; Harriet suggested a fictional case, which worked ([[diploma-unit-3-tutor-feedback]]).

# Assessment centre

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes an assessment centre as a process in which candidates are examined for their suitability for a type of job, especially management roles, through several techniques: interviews, group exercises, presentations, tests and psychometric measures. Covers its origins in military officer selection and its later use in business.

## Why It Matters

Read while Marek and I planned the 2026 graduate assessment centres. Useful for explaining to new assessors why every competency is seen in at least two exercises ([[graduate-assessment-centre-pack-2026]]).

## Caveats

Short on evidence for online versus in-person delivery, which was our real question ([[do-assessment-centres-need-to-be-in-person]]).

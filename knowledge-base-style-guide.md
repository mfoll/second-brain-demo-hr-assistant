# Knowledge Base Style Guide

The two-page style guide for rebuilt articles, written by me and approved by [[leon-adjei]] on 2026-01-09. Version 1.3 since August 2026.

## Summary

- Title is the question as an employee would ask it ("Can I carry over holiday?"), not the policy name.
- Answer in the first two sentences; detail after.
- Under 300 words; a phone screen should show the answer without scrolling.
- Plain words; Flesch reading ease of 60 or more as a rough check ([[flesch-kincaid-readability-tests-wikipedia]]).
- Link to the policy clause rather than repeating it; say it in plain words and link ([[priyanka-desai]]'s condition).
- Every article: owner, review date, "applies to" line (all staff, salaried, hourly, hybrid-eligible, a division).
- Images need alt text; tables only where a phone can show them ([[wcag-2-2]]).
- No examples about real colleagues, ever.

## Why It Matters

The standard every one of the 117 published articles was checked against ([[publish-or-review-a-knowledge-base-article]]).

## Caveats

The reading ease score is crude on HR terms; owners can override it with a reason.

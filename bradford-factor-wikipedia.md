# Bradford Factor

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Explains the Bradford Factor, a score used in HR to measure absenteeism on the theory that short, frequent, unplanned absences disrupt more than long ones. The score multiplies the square of the number of absence spells by the total days absent. The article notes the uncertain origin of the name and criticism that it can disadvantage people with disabilities or recurring conditions.

# Knowledge Base Content Audit 2025

My audit of all 212 knowledge base articles in [[deskwell]], done in the first three weeks of the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]] and finished 2025-12-05.

## Summary

Every article rated on four checks: still accurate, contradicts another article, duplicates another article, has a named owner.

| Verdict | Articles |
| --- | --- |
| Outdated (wrong policy, dates or process) | 61 |
| Contradicts another article | 23 |
| Duplicate or near duplicate | 47 |
| Usable with edits | 81 |

Only 34 of 212 had an owner anyone could name.

## Why It Matters

Set the scale of the rebuild and explained the chatbot result. The 138 target came from merging duplicates and dropping 19 articles nobody had opened in a year.

## Caveats

Accuracy was checked by me against the policy library, then by owners only for the 61 I marked outdated. Some "usable" articles turned out wrong later (the carry-over one: [[kb-article-annual-leave-carry-over]]).

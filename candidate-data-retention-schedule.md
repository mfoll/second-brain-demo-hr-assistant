# Candidate Data Retention Schedule

The recruitment section of Pellmore's records retention schedule, rewritten by [[kasia-wrobel]] in April 2025 after I asked why Hireloom still held applications from 2022.

## Summary

Sets how long candidate data is kept and who deletes it. Hireloom's scheduled purge applies the periods automatically; People Services checks the purge twice a year.

## Key Details

- Unsuccessful candidates: all application data deleted six months after the vacancy closes.
- Talent pool (graduate scheme and hard-to-fill roles): 12 months, only with the candidate's explicit consent at the time of rejection; consent can be withdrawn by email.
- Successful candidates: the application moves to the employee file in the HR system; Hireloom keeps it six months after start, then deletes it.
- Interview notes: written in Hireloom only, never in personal notebooks or email; deleted with the application.
- Agency workers: not in Hireloom; the agency holds their data under its own schedule.
- Purge checks in May and November; I run them and log the number deleted, no names.

## Why It Matters

Before this, there was no purge at all: 38,000 old applications were deleted in the first run on 2025-05-06. It also answered [[how-long-do-we-keep-unsuccessful-candidate-data]] for most cases and shapes how I describe [[data-minimisation]] to hiring managers.

## Caveats

The "reopened vacancy" quirk in [[hireloom]] can trigger early deletion. Retention for right-to-work evidence of employees is set elsewhere, in the employee records schedule.

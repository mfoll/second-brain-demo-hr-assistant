# Bradford Factor Scores Do More Harm Than Good

## Stance

I would leave Talvento's Bradford Factor score switched off. A single number that squares the count of absence spells turns sickness into a ranking that managers will read as a trigger, whatever the policy says.

## Why I think this

- The formula weights frequency far more than length, which is its point, but it hits people with recurring conditions and part-time staff with short spells hardest ([[bradford-factor-wikipedia]]).
- It processes sickness data about individuals to produce a score. [[kasia-wrobel]] has already said that needs a DPIA.
- The hub problem Sanjay describes is real: short notice absence wrecks shift plans. But the fix he needs is earlier notice and team-level patterns, not a score per person.

## What would change my mind

A policy that uses the score only as a prompt for a return-to-work conversation, with an aggregate check showing it does not fall disproportionately on part-time or older staff. And managers trained to use it that way.

## Implications

If the 2027 absence policy review revives it ([[should-talvento-show-bradford-factor-scores]]), I would offer to run the aggregate check, at division level only.

# Absence Mismatch Causes 2026

How the causes of HR versus payroll absence mismatches shifted through 2026. Counts are mismatched records, from the monthly reports ([[absence-reconciliation-reports-2026]]).

| Cause | Jan | Apr | Jul | Sep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approved after cut-off | 103 | 58 | 51 | 34 |
| Absence type coded differently | 65 | 39 | 12 | 6 |
| Part day as full day or reverse | 52 | 33 | 26 | 13 |
| Backdated correction | 38 | 20 | 18 | 8 |
| Total | 258 | 150 | 107 | 61 |
| Rate | 3.8% | 2.2% | 1.6% | 0.9% |

## What I read into it

- Coding differences almost disappeared once Talvento's absence types matched payroll codes one to one at go-live.
- Part days improved with the self-service guide's example ([[manager-self-service-quick-guide]]).
- Approval after cut-off is now over half of what is left, and much of it is correct process ([[can-managers-approve-absence-after-the-cut-off]]). From October it is counted separately.

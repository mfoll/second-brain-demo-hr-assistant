# AI-based interviews and applicant perceptions: insights from China and Pakistan.

Zhao G, Liang Q, Alam F, Hossain MB. Frontiers in Psychology, 2026.

## Summary

Survey of 438 respondents in two countries comparing reactions to AI-based and human interviews. AI interviews were associated with lower perceived transparency, less sense of two-way communication and lower intention to pursue the job; the effect was stronger in one sample than the other. Higher trust in AI weakened the negative reactions.

## Why It Matters

Our ranking idea is not an AI interview, but the point carries over to [[candidate-experience]]: candidates value knowing how they are assessed and feeling heard. Hireloom's survey already shows our weakest score is hearing back after rejection ([[candidate-experience-survey-2025]]). Adding a ranking step candidates cannot see would not help that.

## Caveats

Convenience samples, hypothetical reactions, different labour markets from ours; about interviews, not CV ranking. Read from the abstract.

## Current rule

Policy v2, in force since 2026-09-01: three office days a week for about 1,150 hybrid-eligible roles, Tuesday fixed company-wide, two days chosen by each team ([[hybrid-work-policy-v2]]). Before that, from 2026-04-01, Tuesday to Thursday were all fixed ([[hybrid-work-policy-v1]], archived).

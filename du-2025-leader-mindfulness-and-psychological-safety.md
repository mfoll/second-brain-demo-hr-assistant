# The impact of leader mindfulness in communication on employees' psychological safety.

Du S and Xie W, Frontiers in Psychology, 2025.

## Summary

A two-wave questionnaire study of 506 employees, four months apart, testing whether leaders who communicate attentively and calmly raise employees' psychological safety. It reports a positive link, partly through leader empathy and interpersonal trust, and a stronger effect among employees who are themselves more mindful.

## Why It Matters

Background for the gap between "my team" and "I can raise a concern" in our results ([[psychological-safety]]). The practical reading for Pellmore is modest: how team leaders run shift huddles may matter more for whether people speak up than any survey. That is relevant to the manager toolkit [[shireen-qadir]] is planning for the "you said, we did" round ([[you-said-we-did]]).

## Caveats

Self-report on both sides of the relationship, one sample, no details I could check on the sector. The recommendation to train leaders in mindfulness goes beyond what a correlational design shows.

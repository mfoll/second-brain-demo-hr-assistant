# Executive Committee Hybrid Paper 2026-02

"Hybrid working arrangements from April 2026", paper for the Executive Committee of 2026-02-24, by [[priyanka-desai]] for [[adrian-fairclough]]. Submitted 2026-02-13.

## Summary

Presents the three consultation options, the consultation results (Forum sessions and my [[hybrid-consultation-comment-summary]] as an appendix), and a recommendation framed as the choice between options 2 and 3. Notes 520 desks for 690 Head Office hybrid-eligible staff and assumes about 70% of them in on any anchor day (about 480 people, under the 520 desks), allowing for leave, sickness, travel and site visits.

## Why It Matters

The basis of the February decision; the 70% assumption is where the desk problem started.

## Caveats

People Services' recommendation was option 3 (team choice); the paper lets the committee choose, and it chose option 2.

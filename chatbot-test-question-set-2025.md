# Chatbot Test Question Set 2025

The 150 test questions I wrote for the [[helpdesk-chatbot-trial]], each with an agreed correct answer. Finished 2025-09-30.

## Summary

Questions were written in employees' words from ticket themes, never copied from real tickets, and spread across the 14 categories roughly in proportion to ticket volume (28 on pay, 19 on annual leave, 16 on absence reporting, none on employee relations). Each answer was signed off by the owner of the policy area.

## Why It Matters

The basis of the 41% result ([[chatbot-test-run-2025-10-22]]). Reusable as a regression test for the rebuilt knowledge base and for any future chatbot ([[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]).

## Caveats

- Written by one person; [[callum-rourke]] reviewed the wording of 50.
- About 20 answers are now out of date because of Talvento and hybrid v2. I have marked them but not rewritten them.

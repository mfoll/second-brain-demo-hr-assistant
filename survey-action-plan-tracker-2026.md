# Survey Action Plan Tracker 2026

The spreadsheet I keep of department action plans after the 2026 survey. Updated weekly from HRBP emails; snapshot 2026-10-21.

## Summary

44 departments. 31 plans agreed: 26 of the 31 departments with their own results and 5 of the 13 that roll up to site level. Deadline 2026-11-30.

## Key Details

| Division | Departments | Agreed | Missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warehousing | 17 | 11 | 6 |
| Road Freight | 15 | 9 | 6 |
| Forwarding | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| Head Office | 7 | 6 | 1 |

- Columns: department, own results or roll-up, HRBP, manager, date of team discussion, number of actions, date agreed, date of 3-month check.
- Average 3.2 actions per plan; the most common themes are rota notice, equipment, and team briefings.
- No column holds anything about individuals or comments.

## Why It Matters

The outcome measure of [[engagement-survey-2026]]. In 2024 there was no tracker and nobody could say what happened to the plans ([[engagement-survey-2024-results]]).

## Caveats

"Agreed" means the HRBP has confirmed the team discussed it; it says nothing about delivery. The 3-month check column is the real test and starts in January.

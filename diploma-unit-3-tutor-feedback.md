# Diploma Unit 3 Tutor Feedback

[[harriet-osei]]'s written feedback on my Unit 3 assignment (Employment Relationship Management), graded merit on 2026-09-18.

## Summary

Strong on process and on the difference between informal and formal resolution; weaker on linking the case to evidence. She liked that I used a fictional case built from the policy rather than anything real.

## Key Details

- Best section: the timeline of the procedure and where it most often stalls.

- She noted that choosing a fictional case was the right call for a learner who works near employee relations, and that the Guild's guidance supports it.

## Why It Matters

The feedback point about evidence is now on the planning sheet for Units 5 to 7. The choice of a fictional case is how employee relations work stays out of my notes and assignments, in line with [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]].

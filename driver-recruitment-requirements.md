# Driver Recruitment Requirements

Road Freight's checklist for hiring HGV and van drivers, owned by [[lucia-arbeloa]], last revised in August 2025.

## Summary

Lists what a driver candidate must have and when each check happens, so that coordinators do not ask for documents too early.

## Key Details

- Application: licence categories and any endorsements, declared on the form. No licence copy at this stage (changed in 2025 under [[data-minimisation]]).
- First interview: with a transport planner, booked through self-scheduling by the planners on Lucía's behalf.
- Driving assessment: 90 minutes at the depot with a driver trainer; booked by me or Callum.
- Offer stage: licence check through the licensing authority's online service, driver qualification card check, [[right-to-work-check]].
- Medical: a fitness-to-drive declaration handled by the occupational health provider as a service; HR sees only "fit" or "not yet fit", never the detail.
- Typical time to fill: 34 working days in 2025.

## Why It Matters

Drivers are the steadiest volume outside peak. The checklist explains why driver hiring is slower than warehouse hiring and why the form was shortened.

## Caveats

Requirements for specialist loads (hazardous goods) are in a separate Road Freight document I have not read.

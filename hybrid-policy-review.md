# Hybrid Policy Review

The three-month review of fixed anchor days that the Executive Committee asked for when it decided in February. It went to the People Committee on 2026-07-15, which partly reversed the rule; the revised policy v2 came into force on 2026-09-01.

## Outcome

Three-month review of fixed anchor days taken to the People Committee; revised policy v2 in force from 2026-09-01.

## Current State

> [!success]
> Done. The People Committee kept three office days a week but fixed only Tuesday company-wide; teams choose their other two days. Policy v2 in force from 2026-09-01 ([[hybrid-work-policy-v2]]). The COO asked for quarterly anchor-day attendance reporting, carried on in [[hybrid-work-policy-update]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Evidence gathering and the paper. The decision was the People Committee's, chaired by [[adrian-fairclough]]. I assembled the data; [[priyanka-desai]] wrote the recommendation.

## Working Context

Evidence in the [[hybrid-review-paper-2026-07]]:

- Desks: Head Office reached 104% of its 520 desks on anchor days in April and May; people worked from meeting rooms and the canteen; Monday and Friday sat under 30% ([[head-office-desk-occupancy-2026]], from [[nadia-haddou]]).
- Helpdesk: hybrid questions 23% of tickets in April, 14% in May, 11% in June ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).
- Staff view: the June 2026 [[pulsewright]] pulse check on hybrid work run by [[jun-takahashi]] ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]): 742 responses (65%), 71% preferring one fixed day plus team choice, 41% unable to get a desk on at least one anchor day in May.
- Managers: [[ingrid-salvesen]] collected views from Head Office and Forwarding managers; most wanted to keep one fixed day for meetings.

Options in the paper: keep Tuesday to Thursday and lease extra space (Facilities costed it and [[theo-varkey]] said no budget); drop to two fixed days; one fixed day plus two team-chosen days. [[rosamund-oyelaran]] argued for keeping the fixed days; the committee chose the third option with her quarterly reporting condition.

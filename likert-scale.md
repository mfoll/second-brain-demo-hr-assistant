# Likert scale

## Summary

Describes the Likert scale, named after the social psychologist Rensis Likert, as the most widely used way of scaling answers in survey research, so common that "Likert scale" is often used to mean any rating scale. The article distinguishes the scale (a sum over several items) from a single Likert-type item.

## Why It Matters

Our questionnaire is 42 five-point agreement items ([[engagement-survey-2026-questionnaire]]). Reading this helped me explain to managers why we report "percent favourable" rather than an average of 1 to 5 scores, and why one item is weaker evidence than a section of five ([[engagement-index]]).

## Caveats

General background; it says nothing about which reporting convention is best for an employee survey. [[jun-takahashi]] chose ours.

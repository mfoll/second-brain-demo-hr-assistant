# Data cleansing

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes data cleansing as finding and correcting or removing inaccurate, incomplete or irrelevant records, interactively or in batches, and sets out typical steps: auditing the data, defining rules, running the fixes and checking the result.

## Why It Matters

The audit, rules, fix, verify sequence is roughly how [[hris-data-cleansing]] ran, though we learned it by doing. The idea of keeping rules running after a one-off clean-up is what became the [[hr-data-quality-scorecard]].

# Pausing The Buddy Scheme Was The Wrong Trade-Off

## Stance

Deborah was right that peak induction had to come first, but pausing the whole buddy scheme was more than needed. We could have stopped training new buddies and kept pairing office starters with the 34 already trained.

## Why I Think This

- The trainers were needed for training sessions, not for pairing. Pairing an already trained buddy takes me about ten minutes per starter.
- The midpoint survey ([[buddy-scheme-midpoint-survey-2026]]) suggests buddies cut early helpdesk tickets (1.1 against 1.9 per starter), so the pause shifts work onto the helpdesk during its busiest season.
- About 25 office starters in September and October had no buddy.

## Scope

Office starters only. For hub starters, induction is the priority and I agree with the decision.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the trained buddies themselves were mostly too busy in autumn (Head Office finance and planning teams are), pairing would have failed anyway. I will ask them in December.

## Implications

At the restart, propose that pairing never pauses again even if training does ([[can-the-buddy-scheme-restart-in-january]]). This is my view; the decision was [[deborah-okwuosa]]'s and I have carried it out.

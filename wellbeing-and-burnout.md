# Wellbeing And Burnout

Exploration cluster: reading on work-related strain, shift patterns and presenteeism, collected because the 2026 survey's workload items were the lowest-scoring section and because I want to understand what survey data can and cannot say about it.

No Project owns this yet. If the People Committee commissions wellbeing actions from the survey, or my Unit 6 essay uses it, the cluster moves.

## Scope

- Work organisation and strain in general workplaces: shift work, workload, presenteeism, psychological safety.
- Not individual health. Occupational health is a service we buy, described in [[sunita-ramaswamy]]'s note; no referral or sickness case ever goes here ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

## Reading so far

- [[hulsegge-2020-shift-work-burnout-and-distress]]: shift work and burnout in blue-collar workers; satisfaction with the schedule mattered.
- [[presenteeism]]: working while unwell, and why absence data alone undercounts the problem.
- [[psychological-safety]] and [[du-2025-leader-mindfulness-and-psychological-safety]].
- My notes: [[burnout-reading-notes]].

## Why it is here

The survey's workload section scored 52 favourable against the index of 64 ([[engagement-survey-2026-results-summary]]). Hub night shifts scored lowest. That is a site-level aggregate, which is as far as I can take it.

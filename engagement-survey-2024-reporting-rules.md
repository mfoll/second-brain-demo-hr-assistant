# Engagement Survey 2024 Reporting Rules

The one-page reporting rules used for the 2024 survey. Superseded on 2026-06-10 by the threshold decision ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]); kept because it explains what depot managers were used to.

## Summary

- Minimum reporting group of 5 for scores.
- Free-text comments shown to any department with 5 or more respondents, unscreened.
- Demographic filters allowed down to 5.
- No rule against differencing.

## Why It Matters

Every point here was tightened in 2026. When a depot manager asks why they cannot see what they saw in 2024, this is the comparison. See [[depot-results-rollup-explainer]].

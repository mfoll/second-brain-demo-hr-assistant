# Pulsewright

The employee survey platform Pellmore uses for the engagement survey and pulse checks. Jun is the platform administrator; I have a survey-administrator role.

## Summary

Pulsewright runs the questionnaire, sends invitations and reminders, and produces dashboards and exports for managers. It holds responses with a respondent key but shows reports only in aggregate. Its reporting threshold and anti-differencing options are set per survey.

## Key Details

- Participant file: built by me from the HR system (Staffora in 2024, Talvento for the June 2026 pulse check). Fields: department, site, division, grade band, length of service band, contract type. No names go into reporting.
- Access for staff without email: unique QR code on a printed slip, or a site kiosk link with a one-time code.
- Thresholds: minimum reporting group set to 10 for 2026 ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]); the platform default was 5. Anti-differencing on.
- Free-text: stored in Pulsewright, screened for names by Jun before any report; never exported to shared folders.
- Data retention: raw responses deleted 24 months after close, under the contract ([[pulsewright-order-form-2026]]).
- Manager dashboards: 31 department managers got logins for 2026; roll-up departments see their site.

## Why It Matters

The [[engagement-survey-2026]] and the [[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]] both ran here. The per-survey threshold setting is why the 2027 threshold can be changed without a new contract.

## Caveats

The exports include the respondent key; they stay on Jun's restricted drive. Check the threshold setting each time a new survey is created, because new surveys start at the platform default of 5 ([[check-survey-reports-before-release]]).

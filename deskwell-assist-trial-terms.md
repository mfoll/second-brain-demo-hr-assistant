# Deskwell Assist Trial Terms

The trial order form and data annex for Deskwell Assist, signed by [[leon-adjei]] on 2025-05-27.

## Summary

- Six-month free trial from 2025-06-02, ending 2025-11-30 unless converted to a paid licence.
- The assistant answers from published knowledge base articles only; ticket content is excluded by configuration.
- Questions typed into the assistant are logged for 30 days for troubleshooting, then deleted.
- Up to 25 named internal users.

## Why It Matters

The limits that [[kasia-wrobel]] checked before the trial started ([[could-the-chatbot-use-ticket-history]]). The licence lapsed on 2025-11-30 after the trial stopped.

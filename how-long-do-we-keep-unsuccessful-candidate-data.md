# How Long Do We Keep Unsuccessful Candidate Data?

## Question

When I joined, Hireloom held applications going back to 2022 and nobody knew the rule. How long should unsuccessful candidates' data be kept, and what about talent pools?

## Current Answer

Six months after the vacancy closes, then deleted by the scheduled purge. Talent pool candidates (graduate scheme and hard-to-fill roles) can be kept 12 months only with explicit consent given at the time of rejection. Set by [[kasia-wrobel]] in April 2025 ([[candidate-data-retention-schedule]]).

## How it was decided

- I raised it in March 2025 after a candidate asked what we held about them.
- Kasia set six months as long enough to handle queries or complaints about a decision, and no longer.
- First purge on 2025-05-06 deleted about 38,000 old applications.

## Open edge

Consent wording for the graduate talent pool was rewritten in October 2026 for the 2027 round; Kasia has the draft. Not a reason to reopen the Question.

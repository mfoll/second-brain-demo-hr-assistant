# Data minimization

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines data minimisation as collecting, processing and storing only the personal information needed for a specific purpose. The reasoning is that unnecessary data creates risk for the person without benefit, from identity theft to wrong inferences and decisions. Notes that the principle appears in the GDPR and in other privacy frameworks.

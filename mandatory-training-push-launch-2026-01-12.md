# Mandatory Training Push Launch 2026-01-12

Start of the [[mandatory-training-compliance]] project, agreed with [[deborah-okwuosa]], [[yetunde-balogun]] and [[ewan-galbraith]] after the People Committee asked in December why completion was 81%.

## What we decided

- Target 95% across the four courses ([[mandatory-training-catalogue]]).
- One method for the figure, corrected for absence and leavers ([[training-completion-rate]]).
- Monthly team lists to managers on the first working day, not reminder emails to individuals ([[chase-mandatory-training-before-month-end]]).
- A kiosk pilot at one hub; Hub West volunteered ([[hub-training-kiosk-pilot-results]]).

## Baseline

81% overall; Manual Handling 75%, Respect at Work 80%, Data Protection Essentials 83%, Fire Safety 86%.

## What I got wrong

I also sent three rounds of reminder emails to everyone incomplete in January and February. They moved the figure by about a point each, which is why I now hold [[reminder-emails-do-not-move-training-completion]].

# Talvento Go-Live Cutover Plan

Fiona's revised go-live plan for 2026-06-29, first issued on 2026-03-25 and finalised on 2026-06-19. Replaced the April plan ([[talvento-go-live-plan-april-2026]]).

## Summary

- Freeze Staffora changes Friday 2026-06-26 at 17:00; changes received after that held in a log and keyed on Monday.
- Saturday: final migration, then record count reconciliation by division and site.
- Sunday: spot checks of 200 records (I did 80), absence balances, manager hierarchy, provisioning test accounts. Go/no-go call at 18:00.
- Monday: Talvento live; hypercare desk in People Services for two weeks; Deskwell category for Talvento questions.
- Payroll stays on Staffora; double keying from Monday.
- Rollback: restore Staffora as master, if the go/no-go fails.

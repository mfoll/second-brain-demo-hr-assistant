# Candidate Email Tone Rewrite Notes

The rules Callum and I used to rewrite the candidate emails in summer 2025, and why.

## Rules

1. Say what happens next in the first two sentences.
2. One email, one purpose. If two things need saying, send two templates or merge them properly.
3. Subject lines say the content: "Your interview on [date]", not "Update on your application".
4. Write as a person: "we", "you", no passive "it has been decided".
5. Rejections say no in the first sentence, then thanks, then whether to reapply and when data is deleted.
6. No promises we do not keep (the old "within 48 hours").
7. Plain-text version for every email ([[wcag-2-2]]).

## Examples

- Old acknowledgement: 118 words, next step in paragraph three. New: 46 words, next step in sentence one.
- Old rejection opened with three sentences of thanks. New: "Thank you for interviewing with us. On this occasion we will not be offering you the role."

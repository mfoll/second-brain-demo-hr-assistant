# Graduate Assessment Centre 2026-01-28

The first of two 2026 graduate assessment centres, run online.

## What happened

- 22 candidates invited, 21 attended; one withdrew the evening before.
- Eight assessors from operations, finance and People Services. [[marek-zielinski]] led; I ran the logistics and the online rooms, [[callum-rourke]] ran timekeeping.
- Two connection failures during the group exercise; both candidates rejoined within five minutes and were given extra time on the written task.
- Three reasonable-adjustment requests, all met (extra time, camera-off rest breaks).
- Wash-up finished at 17:40, 1 hour 40 minutes over plan.

## Outcomes

8 offers recommended from 21 candidates. Assessors found the group exercise harder to score online: 4 of 8 said they could not see who was driving the discussion.

## Lessons

Breakout rooms need an assessor each, not one per two groups. Wash-up needs its own scheduled hour. Both in [[graduate-scheme-2026-lessons-learned]].

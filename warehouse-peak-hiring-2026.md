# Warehouse Peak Hiring

Hire, check and induct 260 seasonal warehouse operatives across the four hubs before peak starts on 2026-11-23. Sponsor [[conrad-baptiste]] (Operations Director, Warehousing); recruitment lead [[marek-zielinski]]; I coordinate interviews, offers, pre-start checks and induction bookings.

## Outcome

260 seasonal operatives hired, through pre-start checks, entered in Talvento and inducted by 2026-11-16, one week before peak, so that every one of them is paid on time in their first week.

## Current State

- 214 of 260 hired on 2026-10-20 (82%): 128 direct hires through Hireloom, 86 agency workers through [[chiara-lombardi-ross]]'s agency. 46 still needed, mostly at Hub North and Hub West ([[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]]).
- 191 of the 214 have completed a [[hub-induction-day]]; the other 23 are booked for the sessions of 27 and 28 October.
- Induction days have been weekly at every hub since 2026-09-07, at most 40 starters a session. Capacity is about 160 places a week in theory, about 120 once room clashes and trainer leave are counted ([[peak-induction-capacity-maths]]).
- No first-pay slips so far this year: every direct starter has been in Talvento before the Thursday 12:00 weekly cut-off of their first week. Last year 19 starters slipped ([[peak-hiring-2025-review]]).
- Open: how to close the last 46 ([[how-do-we-close-the-peak-hiring-gap]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Direct seasonal hires and agency workers for the four hubs. Not in scope: permanent warehouse vacancies (business as usual), depot hiring, driver hiring for peak (Road Freight runs its own). Agency workers are inducted by us but paid by the agency, so the payroll cut-off only applies to direct hires. No candidate or starter is named in these notes; the tracker is counts only.

## Working Context

The plan ([[peak-hiring-plan-2026]]) splits the 260 by hub: Hub North 70, Hub East 75, Hub South 60, Hub West 55. Conrad wanted 60% direct, 40% agency to cut agency fees; we are at 60/40 on the hires so far, so the split held. Direct hires take about 11 working days from application to start; agency workers about 5. That is why the end-of-campaign gap will almost certainly be filled by agency.

Every direct hire goes through [[schedule-interviews-in-hireloom]] (group assessment sessions of 12 at the hub), offer, [[right-to-work-check]], the [[new-starter-data-form]], then Talvento and induction. Since 2026-06-29 the starter record goes into Talvento, not Staffora. [[aoife-brennan-hale]] holds the right-to-work evidence; I check on Wednesday afternoons that every Monday starter is in Talvento, because the cut-off is Thursday 12:00.

To free trainers for weekly induction, [[deborah-okwuosa]] paused the [[onboarding-buddy-scheme]] on 2026-09-07 ([[buddy-scheme-pause-2026-09-07]]). I disagree with that trade-off ([[pausing-the-buddy-scheme-was-the-wrong-trade-off]]) but it was her call.

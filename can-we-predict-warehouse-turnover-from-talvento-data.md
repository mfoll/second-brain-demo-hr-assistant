# Can We Predict Warehouse Turnover From Talvento Data?

Could Talvento data show which hub teams are likely to lose staff in their first six months, early enough for a team leader to do something?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Deferred. Talvento has four months of clean history; Staffora's leaver reasons are unreliable before 2026. Kasia Wróbel said any model scoring individuals would need a DPIA and probably would not pass. Team-level reporting of early leavers, by hub and month, is possible now and Jun Takahashi has it on his list.

## Why I asked

Warehouse peak hires 260 seasonal operatives every year, and hubs lose permanent operatives in their first six months at a rate nobody has measured properly. The papers I read ([[pavithran-2025-explainable-attrition-risk-scoring]], [[sarmiento-orna-2026-ai-and-data-analytics-in-talent-management]]) predict at the level of the individual, on public datasets. That is not what a hub needs, and it is not something I would want built.

## What would revive it

A year of Talvento leaver data with consistent reasons, and a question framed at team level. Notes: [[people-analytics-reading-notes]].

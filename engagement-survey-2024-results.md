# Engagement Survey 2024 Results

The group results of the 2024 survey, run before I joined, which I read in February 2025 to understand where the 2026 survey would start.

## Summary

64% response rate (about 2,560 of 4,000), engagement index 61. Lowest sections were change (47) and communication (55). 40 of 44 departments got their own reports under the 2024 threshold of 5.

## Key Details

- Ran on Pulsewright in May 2024; reports in July.
- 38 department action plans were recorded; there was no tracker, so nobody could say how many were delivered.
- Depot managers in several small depots used their own results well, which is the history behind [[sanjay-mehrotra]]'s position.
- The survey was biennial until then; in late 2024 the People Committee made it annual from 2026, but no 2025 survey ran because of the Talvento programme.

## Why It Matters

The trend baseline for the [[engagement-survey-2026-results-summary]] and the reason we built a tracker this time ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]). The reporting rules used then are archived in [[engagement-survey-2024-reporting-rules]].

## Caveats

The 2024 participant file came from Staffora and had known errors in department codes; [[jun-takahashi]] estimates up to 3% of responses were attributed to the wrong department.

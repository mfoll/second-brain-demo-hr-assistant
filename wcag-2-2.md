# Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2

W3C Recommendation, the current version of the web accessibility guidelines.

## Summary

The W3C standard for making web content accessible to people with disabilities, organised under four principles (perceivable, operable, understandable, robust), with testable success criteria at levels A, AA and AAA. Version 2.2 adds criteria to 2.1, including several on focus visibility, target size and consistent help.

## Why It Matters

Used for three rules in the [[knowledge-base-style-guide]]: alt text on every image, no information carried by colour alone, and help placed consistently (the "still need help?" button at the bottom of every article). I am not an accessibility specialist; these are the criteria I could check myself.

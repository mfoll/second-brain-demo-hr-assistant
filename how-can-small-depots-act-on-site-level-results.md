# How Can Small Depots Act On Site-Level Results

Ten depots and three small Head Office teams fall below the minimum reporting group of 10 and see only results for their site, which combines two to four depots ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]). How do they make a plan that is about them?

## What we know

- Posting a site pack by email produced nothing; by October only 5 of the 13 had agreed plans ([[survey-action-plan-tracker-2026]]).
- A facilitated session in which teams "claim or reject" site findings worked at Hub East: three depots, three drafts, two agreed within a week ([[action-planning-workshop-hub-east-2026-07-08]]).
- The roll-up version of the [[department-action-plan-template]] supports that format.

## Current Answer

Partial: a 60 to 90-minute facilitated session per site works, but needs an HRBP or me there, and peak makes that hard from August to November. Next step is to run it for the eight remaining roll-up depots after 2026-11-23, which is after the plan deadline; I need to ask [[adrian-fairclough]] whether a late plan for those is acceptable.

## What would settle it

Plan quality and the 3-month checks for the roll-up departments, compared with own-results departments, in spring 2027.

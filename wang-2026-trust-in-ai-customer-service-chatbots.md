# Building user trust in AI chatbots for customer service through human-like cues and perceived reliability.

Wang S, Fatima N, Shahbaz M, Asif M. *Scientific Reports*, 2026.

## Summary

A qualitative interview study with 28 users of AI customer service chatbots. Two themes: human-like interaction and emotional connection (naturalness, empathy, personalisation, social presence), which build affective trust; and perceived reliability and system competence (accuracy, transparency, responsiveness, data security), which build cognitive trust. Both shape user confidence and satisfaction.

## Why It Matters

Read for [[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]. Our trial failed on the second theme: the bot sounded natural and was wrong 37 times in 150. For an HR bot, accuracy and transparency (citing the article) come first; a friendly tone on a wrong pay answer would do more harm than a blunt one.

## Caveats

Small qualitative sample of consumers, not employees; read from the abstract.

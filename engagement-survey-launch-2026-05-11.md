# Engagement Survey Launch 2026-05-11

The 2026 engagement survey opened on [[pulsewright]] at 06:00 so early shifts could answer on their break.

## What happened

- 4,100 invitations: 1,620 by email, the rest as printed QR slips handed out at shift briefings ([[survey-communications-plan-2026]]).
- Day-one response 14%; Head Office 31%, Road Freight 6%.
- Two problems: 58 slips for Depot 09 printed with the wrong survey link (reprinted the same day), and a batch of bounced emails for new starters entered in Staffora after the participant file was cut. Those starters got slips at their induction.

## What I learned

- Cut the participant file as late as possible and add a top-up for starters.
- Check one QR slip per depot batch before they go out.
- Both went into [[engagement-survey-2026-lessons-learned]].

The survey closed on 2026-05-29 at 71%.

# Diploma Study Routine

How I fit the diploma around a full-time job, and what has changed after a year.

## Week

- Tuesday and Thursday, 19:00 to 21:00: reading and drafting.
- Sunday, 09:00 to 12:00: the longest block, used for writing.
- Every second Wednesday: study group call with [[rory-pennington]] and [[felipe-arruda]].
- Nothing in payroll cut-off week except reading. The 15th always wins.

## Per unit

1. Read the brief twice, a week apart.
2. One-page plan to [[harriet-osei]].
3. Evidence first, then draft, then cut 10%.
4. Reflective log entry after submission ([[reflective-practice]]).

## Study days

Five a year under the [[study-support-agreement]]. I use them for the hardest part of a unit, not the deadline day.

## What changed

- I stopped studying at lunch at work: it blurred both.
- Peak season (August to mid-November) means lighter units; I chose Unit 5 for autumn because I know the material.
- When I miss a session, I move it rather than double up.

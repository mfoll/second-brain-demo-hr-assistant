# Hybrid Work Policy Update

Umbrella for the hybrid work policy: consultation, the Executive Committee decision on fixed anchor days, guidance for the April 2026 launch, the three-month review and the revised policy v2. [[priyanka-desai]] is the policy lead; I support her with the consultation analysis, the FAQ, the helpdesk side and the papers.

## Outcome

The revised hybrid work policy in force with guidance live, and hybrid questions back under 5% of helpdesk tickets.

## Current State

> [!info]
> Policy v2 has been in force since 2026-09-01: three office days a week, Tuesday fixed company-wide, each team chooses its other two days. Hybrid questions were 9% of tickets in September, down from 23% in April but not yet under 5%.

- Decision trail: [[exec-committee-hybrid-decision-2026-02-24]] set three fixed anchor days (Tuesday to Thursday) from 2026-04-01; [[people-committee-2026-07-15]] kept three days but fixed only Tuesday. Side by side in [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]].
- [[rosamund-oyelaran]] accepted the revision on condition that anchor-day attendance is reported each quarter. The first report, covering September, is being drafted ([[anchor-day-attendance-report-q3-2026]]); how to report it without identifying anyone is [[how-should-anchor-day-attendance-be-reported]].
- Head Office desks: 104% of 520 desks booked on anchor days in April and May; Tuesday now 96%, other days well under capacity ([[head-office-desk-occupancy-2026]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

About 1,150 hybrid-eligible roles, 690 of them at Head Office. Site-based roles (drivers, hub operatives, workshop) are out of scope; the Forum's request for flexibility for them is parked ([[should-site-based-roles-get-a-flexibility-offer]]). Individual exceptions and adjustments are handled by the HR Business Partners as cases and never recorded here ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

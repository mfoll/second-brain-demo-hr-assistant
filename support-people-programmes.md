# Support People Programmes

My share of the group-wide people programmes: helping get HR policy changes out of committee and into practice, running the admin side of the engagement survey, and keeping training records straight in [[learnmoor]]. None of this ends; each year brings a new survey cycle, a new set of refresher dates and at least one policy rewrite.

## Scope

- Policy updates after a decision is made: drafting FAQs, Deskwell articles, manager briefings, and working out what changes in the systems. The hybrid policy is the big one ([[hybrid-work-policy-update]]), run by [[priyanka-desai]].
- The engagement survey on [[pulsewright]]: participant lists, communications with [[shireen-qadir]], reporting checks with [[jun-takahashi]], tracking department action plans.
- Training administration in [[learnmoor]]: the four mandatory courses, booking, completion reports, chasing.
- Not here: the decisions themselves. Those sit with the People Committee, the Executive Committee and the [[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10|Survey Steering Group]]. My notes record what they decided and what I have to do about it.
- Not here: anything about an individual's survey answers, training failures tied to a named person, or appraisal outcomes. See [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]].

# Induction Room Capacity By Hub

A small sheet from the four hub managers listing training rooms, seats and the days each room is free, collected in August 2026 for peak.

## Summary

Hub North, Hub East and Hub South have rooms for 40 or more; Hub West's room seats 32. Rooms are free on at least two weekdays at every hub.

## Key Details

- Hub North: 44 seats, free Monday and Wednesday.
- Hub East: 40 seats, free Monday and Thursday.
- Hub South: 48 seats, free Tuesday and Friday.
- Hub West: 32 seats, free Monday only during peak (the room is used for team briefings on other days).
- Safety trainers: one per hub, two at Hub East; each can walk 20 people at a time.

## Why It Matters

The physical limit behind the cap of 40 and the lower cap at Hub West, used in [[peak-induction-capacity-maths]].

## Caveats

Room availability changes when hubs hold their own events; checked again each Monday when I book.

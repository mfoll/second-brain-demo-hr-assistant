# Interview Scheduling Baseline 2025

My analysis of Hireloom timestamps for first interviews booked between October 2024 and February 2025, done in the first two weeks of the [[interview-scheduling-overhaul]].

## Summary

Median [[time-to-schedule]] was 4.2 working days from shortlist to booked interview, across 612 first interviews. Most of the delay was waiting for the hiring manager's availability, not for the candidate.

## Key Details

- Shortlist to availability received from the manager: median 2.6 working days.
- Availability to candidate confirming: median 1.1 working days.
- 14% of interviews were rebooked at least once; 6% of shortlisted candidates withdrew before being booked.
- Slowest: depot and driver roles (median 6.8 days). Fastest: Head Office (3.1).
- Emails per booked interview: about 5.

## Why It Matters

It turned the project from "candidates are slow to reply" into "managers need to publish slots", which is what self-scheduling does. The figure of 4.2 is the baseline quoted in the project outcome.

## Caveats

Hireloom only logs the booking time, so the shortlist date is the date the status changed, which some managers set late. Interviews booked outside Hireloom (about 40, mostly Forwarding) are missing.

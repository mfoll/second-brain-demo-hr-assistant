# Diploma Unit 5 Assignment Brief

The Guild's assignment brief for Unit 5, Reward and Resourcing, released 2026-09-21. Due 2026-11-20.

## Summary

A 4,000-word report evaluating one resourcing or reward practice in the learner's organisation against published evidence, with recommendations and a cost-benefit section.

## Key Details

- Must cover: the practice and its purpose, evidence on what works, how it is measured in the organisation, and two costed recommendations.
- Data from the organisation must be aggregate; no individual pay data.
- Assessment criteria weight the evidence review and the quality of recommendations most.

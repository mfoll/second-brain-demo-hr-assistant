# Survey Reporting Threshold Decision 2026-06-10

The Survey Steering Group set the minimum reporting group for the 2026 engagement survey at 10 respondents, on the advice of [[kasia-wrobel]] (DPO). [[sanjay-mehrotra]] dissented and argued for 5. I took the minutes.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Minimum reporting group of 10 for every cut of the 2026 survey, scores and comments alike. 13 of 44 departments fall below it and see only site-level results. Threshold to be revisited before the 2027 survey. Sanjay's dissent is minuted.

## Who was there

[[adrian-fairclough]] (chair), [[kasia-wrobel]], [[jun-takahashi]], [[sanjay-mehrotra]], [[hollie-pritchard]], [[ingrid-salvesen]], [[gabriel-santoro]] for the Employee Forum, [[shireen-qadir]], and me as secretary.

## What was on the table

The survey had closed on 2026-05-29 with 2,911 responses (71%). Jun's paper ([[survey-steering-group-reporting-paper-2026-06]]) showed how many of the 44 departments would get their own report at each threshold:

| Threshold | Departments with own report | Below threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 40 | 4 |
| 8 | 34 | 10 |
| 10 | 31 | 13 |

In 2024 the threshold was 5 ([[engagement-survey-2024-reporting-rules]]). Kasia's written advice ([[dpo-advice-survey-reporting-threshold]]) made three points: with 5 respondents, people in a small depot can often work out who said what, especially with the demographic filters and the free-text comments; the survey promises confidentiality, so the risk falls on the employer; and in 2024 one depot manager had asked a team who wrote a comment, which was handled at the time and is why she looked at this again. That case is described there only as a category.

## Decision

- Minimum reporting group of 10 for every report: department, site, division and any demographic filter.
- Free-text comments shown only for groups of 10 or more, after screening for names.
- Groups below 10 roll up to the next level, usually the site. 13 departments are affected, ten of them depots of under 30 staff, plus three small Head Office teams.
- No "differencing": a report cannot be filtered so that a small group can be calculated by subtracting one result from another. Jun set the Pulsewright option that blocks this; [[owen-tremaine]] confirmed it applies per survey.
- Revisit before the 2027 survey, with evidence on what roll-up departments did with site-level results.

## The dissent

Sanjay argued for 5. His case: the depot managers who acted best on their 2024 results were in small depots; a site average hides a good or a bad depot; and if people cannot see their own results, response rates there will fall. Gabriel supported the threshold of 10 for the Forum, saying members at small depots had told him they did not trust the 2024 anonymity. Hollie said she could live with either. Adrian asked for the dissent to be minuted and for Sanjay to bring evidence to the 2027 review.

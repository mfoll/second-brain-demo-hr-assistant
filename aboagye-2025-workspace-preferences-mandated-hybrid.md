# Employee workspace preferences in a mandated hybrid work policy: A discrete choice experiment.

Aboagye E, Botha W, Ljungberg H, Danielsson C, Jensen I. *Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health*, 2025.

## Summary

A discrete choice experiment with 433 operational support staff at a university working under a mandated hybrid model. Each participant chose between 12 pairs of hypothetical office designs varying on seven attributes. Having a dedicated desk (no desk sharing) was the strongest preference, followed by personalisation and territoriality, opportunities for teamwork and social interaction. Private offices and quiet rooms were not strongly preferred. Preferences varied with gender, age, commute distance and home environment.

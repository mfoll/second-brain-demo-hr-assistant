# Promoting the Quarry Workers' Hazard Identification Through Formal and Informal Safety Training.

Bae H et al., Safety and Health at Work, 2021.

## Summary

A case study at one surface mining site, with interviews of eight quarry workers plus field notes, a meeting with safety managers and site photographs. Workers identified hazards by applying what they learned in formal training, but also through hands-on experience, learning from co-workers and sharing responsibility within teams.

## Why It Matters

Cited in my Unit 4 assignment next to the [[70-20-10-model]], as a small piece of evidence that formal safety courses are only part of how site workers learn. It shaped my argument that completion rates measure the formal part only ([[training-completion-rate]]).

## Caveats

Eight interviews at one site; qualitative and not generalisable. Mining, not logistics.

# Publish or Review a Knowledge Base Article

How to take a knowledge base article from draft to published in [[deskwell]], and how to handle its scheduled review. Written January 2026 for the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]].

## Publish

1. **Start from a ticket theme**, not a policy. The title is the employee's question.
2. **Draft** to the [[knowledge-base-style-guide]]: answer in two sentences, under 300 words, readable on a phone, "applies to" line, link to the policy clause.
3. **Check readability**: Reading Ease 60 or more, or a reason why not.
4. **Owner check**: send to the owner ([[who-owns-knowledge-base-articles-after-publication]]) with a five-working-day deadline. Owner confirms the facts in writing.
5. **Fill the fields**: owner, review date (12 months; 6 for pay, absence and anything tied to a system change), category, search keywords, one-line search description.
6. **Publish**, then retire or redirect any old article it replaces.
7. **Update the tracker** ([[knowledge-base-rebuild-tracker]]).

## Review

1. Deskwell emails the owner 30 days before the review date; I copy myself.
2. The owner confirms, edits or retires. Edits go back through steps 2 to 4.
3. Set the next review date.
4. If the owner does not respond within 10 working days of the date, the article is flagged to Leon. Since 2026-10-20, [[deborah-okwuosa]] chases owners who are still late.

## Unscheduled review

When a policy or system changes (hybrid v2, Talvento go-live, payroll cutover), list every article linking to the policy and review them all before the change takes effect.

## Guardrails

No real cases or colleagues as examples. No personal data in screenshots. Articles never give advice on one person's situation.

# Do Assessment Centres Need To Be In Person?

## Question

For the graduate scheme, is an online assessment centre as good as an in-person one, given the cost difference?

## Current Answer

Run the first 2027 centre online and the final one in person. Decided by [[marek-zielinski]] on 2026-03-30 from the 2026 comparison.

## Evidence

- Offer rates were similar: 8 of 21 online, 10 of 22 in person.
- Candidate feedback was higher in person (4.6 against 4.2), and assessors were more confident scoring the group exercise in person.
- In person cost roughly three times as much.
- Online widened the field: 9 of 21 online candidates said they could not easily have travelled.

## Caveat

One year, two centres, 43 candidates. Not enough to say the format made no difference to who got offers; enough for a practical choice.

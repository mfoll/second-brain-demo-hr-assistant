# Should Talvento Show Bradford Factor Scores?

Talvento can calculate a Bradford Factor score for each employee and show it to their line manager. Should Pellmore switch it on?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Deferred to the absence policy review planned for 2027. The People Committee did not want a monitoring change bundled with the system migration. The feature stays off.

## Positions

- [[sanjay-mehrotra]] (HRBP, Warehousing) wanted it on at the hubs, where short frequent absence on Mondays and Fridays disrupts shift planning and team leaders currently keep their own spreadsheets.
- [[hollie-pritchard]] (HRBP, Road Freight) against: drivers' absence patterns are driven by rota and licence checks, and a score would be read as a trigger.
- [[kasia-wrobel]] said switching it on would need a DPIA, because it processes sickness data to produce a score about an individual.
- My view: [[bradford-factor-scores-do-more-harm-than-good]].

## What would revive it

The 2027 absence policy review, or a People Committee request. If revived, it should go with a DPIA, a defined use (conversation prompt, never automatic trigger) and an aggregate check of how scores differ for part-time staff.

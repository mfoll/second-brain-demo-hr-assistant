# Hallucination (artificial intelligence)

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Defines an AI hallucination as a generated response containing false or misleading information presented as fact, borrowing the term loosely from psychology.

## Why It Matters

Read two days after the [[chatbot-test-run-2025-10-22]], because Leon called the 14 confidently wrong pay answers "hallucinations". Most were not: the bot faithfully repeated outdated articles. Only the 17 answers given where no article existed fit this definition. The distinction mattered for the closure report: fixing content fixes most of the errors, not all ([[the-chatbot-failed-on-content-not-technology]]).

## Caveats

The article is about the general phenomenon, not about systems restricted to a document set.

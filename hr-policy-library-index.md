# HR Policy Library Index

The index of Pellmore's HR policies on the intranet, maintained by [[priyanka-desai]]: 46 policies and procedures, each with owner, approving body, version and review date.

## Summary

Approval levels: Executive Committee (pay, hybrid principles, restructuring), People Committee (most HR policies, including [[hybrid-work-policy-v2]]), Head of People Services (procedural guidance). 11 of 46 policies were past their review date in May 2026.

## Why It Matters

Knowledge base articles link to policies here instead of repeating them. Article owners are matched to policy owners where possible ([[who-owns-knowledge-base-articles-after-publication]]).

## Caveats

The index lists policies, not articles; the two are maintained separately, which is how they drifted apart before the rebuild.

# Guild Mentoring Scheme Guide

The Guild of People Practice's guide for mentees, received when I applied in December 2025.

## Summary

Mentees are matched with an experienced member from a different organisation for twelve months. Mentees set the agenda; sessions are confidential; mentors do not review assessed work.

## Key Details

- Suggested rhythm: an hour every four to eight weeks.
- A short goals statement at the start and a review at six and twelve months.
- Confidentiality covers what is said; it does not allow sharing third parties' information, so mentees are asked to keep colleagues and cases out.

## Why It Matters

The frame for my sessions with [[amina-yusuf]]; the third-party rule fits our own case rule ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]). My twelve months end in January 2027, and both of us want to continue informally.

# Hub Induction Pilot Feedback Hub East

Feedback from the pilot induction day at Hub East on 2026-05-14: the starters' feedback forms (aggregate) and [[maeve-donnelly]]'s list of changes sent that evening.

## Summary

22 starters, 20 forms returned, average 4.0 out of 5. The day ran 50 minutes over. Maeve proposed seven changes; five were adopted.

## Key Details

- Highest rated: the safety walk-round (4.6) and team leader time (4.4).
- Lowest: the paperwork session (3.1), which took 75 minutes on paper.
- Maeve's changes adopted: forms before day one, badges printed in advance, lunch moved to 12:30, the walk-round split into two groups, a team leader named in advance.
- Not adopted: a second day for forklift awareness (belongs to job training) and an extra hour for site history (time).

## Why It Matters

Shaped version 2 and then version 3 of the [[hub-induction-day-running-order]], and led to the [[new-starter-data-form]] moving before day one.

## Caveats

One session at one hub; starters were all direct hires.

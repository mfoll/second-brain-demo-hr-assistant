# Buddy Scheme Pause 2026-09-07

[[deborah-okwuosa]] paused the [[onboarding-buddy-scheme]] from today to free two trainers for weekly hub inductions during peak.

## Decision

- No new buddy pairs and no buddy training until peak induction drops back to monthly; pairs already running finish their 90 days.
- The two trainers who ran buddy training each cover one hub induction a week.
- Restart planned for January 2027, subject to trainer time.

## Context

Weekly induction at four hubs needs about eight trainer-days a week; the hubs could find six. Deborah chose the buddy scheme because it affects about 12 office starters a month against roughly 40 to 70 hub starters a week in peak.

## My view

I said in our one-to-one that office starters in September and October would lose out and that the midpoint survey showed fewer helpdesk tickets with a buddy ([[buddy-scheme-midpoint-survey-2026]]). Deborah's answer was that peak starters who are not inducted cannot work at all, which is true. I still think we could have kept buddy pairing without the training.

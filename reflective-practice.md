# Reflective practice

## Summary

Reflective practice is the habit of examining one's own thoughts, actions, decisions and assumptions in order to improve one's practice, rather than acting on autopilot. The article's central claim is that deliberate reflection on experience is what turns experience into meaningful action.

## Why It Matters

The Guild requires a reflective log for the final portfolio review ([[guild-level-5-diploma]]). I read this in the third week of the course to work out what a log should contain. My format: what happened, what I did, what I would do differently, what I need to learn. Kept after each assignment and after anything at work I got wrong ([[diploma-study-routine]]).

## Caveats

Overview of many models; it does not prescribe a format.

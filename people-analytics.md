# People Analytics

Exploration cluster on using HR data to answer workforce questions: turnover, absence patterns, hiring speed. I started reading in 2026 because Talvento makes reporting easier than Staffora ever did, and because [[jun-takahashi]] keeps getting asked for things the data cannot yet support.

## Current position

> [!info]
> Nothing here is a project. Jun's team does reporting; nobody at Pellmore builds predictive models on employees, and [[kasia-wrobel]] has said any such model would need a data protection impact assessment first. My question about warehouse turnover is parked: [[can-we-predict-warehouse-turnover-from-talvento-data]].

## What I want to understand

- Which questions Talvento data can answer honestly now that it is cleaner, and which still need a year of history.
- Where analytics stops being reporting and starts being decisions about individuals.
- Whether the absence data is good enough for anything beyond reconciliation ([[absence-data-reconciliation]]).

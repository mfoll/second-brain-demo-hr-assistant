# Payroll Cutover Options Comparison

My draft comparison for the cutover paper to the 2026-11-04 HRIS Steering Group. Written to be neutral; Fiona and Elspeth have both read it and each added a line.

| | November 2026 | January 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Talvento-fed monthly payroll | Paid 2026-11-26 | Paid 2027-01-26 |
| First Talvento-fed weekly payroll | Week of 2026-11-30, in peak | Week of 2027-01-04, seasonal leavers week |
| Clean monthly parallel runs before | 2 (if October clean) | 4 (if October to December clean) |
| Full weekly parallel runs before | 0 unless added in November | Could add 4 in December |
| Months of double keying left | 1 | 3 |
| Year-end on one system | No (Talvento for December) | Yes (Staffora) |
| Time between cutover and Staffora licence end | 4 months | 2 months |
| Implementation partner support | Covered | Extension needed ([[talvento-statement-of-work]]) |
| Owner preference | Fiona | Elspeth |

## Risks each way

- **November**: first weekly runs in peak, with up to 40 new hourly starters a week ([[enter-new-starters-before-weekly-cutoff]]); December bank holiday cut-offs on a new feed ([[year-end-payroll-calendar-2026]]).
- **January**: three more months of backdated-change risk ([[parallel-run-difference-categories]]); first weekly run is the big seasonal leavers week; tight time to decommission Staffora ([[staffora-decommissioning-plan]]).

## A middle option nobody has proposed yet

Monthly payroll cuts over in November, weekly in January. Elspeth says two feeds into one payroll system is worse than either date. Recorded so it is not raised again without that answer.

My view is separate: [[payroll-cutover-should-wait-for-january]].

# Payroll Cut-Off Checklist v1

The first version of the payroll cut-off checklist, trialled from 2025-02-14 to 2025-04-25. Retired and replaced by [[payroll-cutoff-checklist-v2]].

## Summary

One table of 22 lines covering both monthly and weekly payroll, with columns for owner, monthly deadline and weekly deadline.

## Why It Matters

Kept to show why version 2 is split. [[dariusz-nowicki]]'s verdict was that nobody could use it on a Thursday morning: the weekly lines were lost among monthly ones, and there was no way to say "nothing to send". Late changes in March 2025 were still 41.

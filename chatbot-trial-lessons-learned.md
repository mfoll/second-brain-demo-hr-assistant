# Chatbot Trial Lessons Learned

What I took from the [[helpdesk-chatbot-trial]], written 2025-11-21, a week after it stopped.

1. **Test before showing anyone.** Keeping the bot internal meant 37 wrong answers stayed with 14 HR staff, not 4,100 employees.
2. **Write the test set from themes, not tickets.** It kept us clear of personal data and gave us something reusable ([[chatbot-test-question-set-2025]]).
3. **Trace every failure to its source.** The trace turned "the bot is bad" into "the articles are bad", which is a problem we can fix ([[chatbot-test-run-2025-10-22]]).
4. **Set the success bar before the test.** Leon's 70% and no confident pay errors made the decision easy.
5. **A free trial is not free.** I spent about 60 hours on the test set and scoring.
6. **Vendors measure differently.** The vendor reported 68% "helpful" answers by counting any answer with a cited article. We counted correct and complete.

What I would do differently: run the content audit first and the chatbot second. We learned in six months what an audit would have shown in three weeks.

# Probation Reminder Schedule

The spreadsheet I use to remind managers about probation reviews. Built in 2025 when we found that reviews were missed because nobody prompted managers.

## Summary

For each starter on [[probation]], two reminders: at week 10 (mid-point conversation) and week 22 (final review due by week 26). Reminders are emails from a template; the review outcome is recorded by the manager in the HR system, not here.

## Key Details

- The sheet holds the start date, manager and division only, keyed to the HR system employee number. No outcomes, no ratings.
- About 60 to 90 open probations at any time, 300 or more in peak months (seasonal contracts have a shorter, 8-week probation and only one reminder).
- Since the reminders started in May 2025, final reviews recorded by week 26 rose from 58% to 87% (Staffora report, March 2026).

## Why It Matters

A manual step that belongs in the HR system. Talvento can create manager tasks, which is why moving it is tied to [[manager-self-service-rollout]].

## Caveats

Counts come from Staffora until June 2026 and Talvento after, so the 2026 figures mix two systems.

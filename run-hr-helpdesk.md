# Run HR Helpdesk

My share of the People Services front door: answering employee queries in [[deskwell]], triaging tickets to the right person, keeping the knowledge base honest, and the team's shared ways of working. It has no end date; the projects under it come and go.

## Scope

- Helpdesk tickets in the shared HR Shared Services queues, on the rota with [[leon-adjei]], [[callum-rourke]] and [[aoife-brennan-hale]] (see [[helpdesk-cover-rota-2026]]).
- Ticket categories, routing rules and the first-response target ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]], [[deskwell-routing-rules]]).
- The employee-facing knowledge base: articles I own, the review dates I chase for other owners, the [[knowledge-base-style-guide]].
- Team ways of working in People Services ([[people-services-team-charter]]).
- Not here: employee relations casework (routed straight to [[tomas-ferreira-lund]]'s restricted queue; I only see counts), occupational health referrals (a service run by managers and HRBPs, see [[occupational-health-service-overview]]), and policy decisions, which belong to the People Committee and the HR Business Partners.
- Privacy: tickets are described here by category and count only, following [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]].

# Tuesday 28 April 2026

## April's hybrid tickets

An hour with Leon, Priyanka and Callum on the hybrid spike. Hybrid questions were close to a quarter of all tickets in April, against a few per cent before. I brought counts and themes only, no ticket text ([[helpdesk-hybrid-ticket-themes-april-2026|April themes]]). The biggest group was part-time and compressed-hours staff asking which three days counted, which my FAQ had not foreseen because it assumed a five-day week. Desks were the next theme.

We agreed that the holding answers become permanent FAQ entries, that exception requests go straight to the HR Business Partners, and that Priyanka takes the part-time gap into the three-month review as a policy question. Decisions in the [[helpdesk-hybrid-ticket-review-2026-04-28|review note]].

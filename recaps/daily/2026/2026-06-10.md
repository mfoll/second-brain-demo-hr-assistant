# Wednesday 10 June 2026

## The reporting threshold

Survey Steering Group, with me as secretary. Kasia's advice was to set the minimum reporting group at 10, because at 5 people in a small depot can often work out who said what. Jun's paper showed the cost: a block of departments, mostly small depots, would only see site results. Sanjay argued for 5 so depot managers can act on their own numbers, and Gabriel said the Forum backed 10. Adrian took 10 for 2026, minuted Sanjay's dissent and put the question back on the table before the 2027 survey. Detail in the [[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10|decision note]] and the idea in [[minimum-reporting-group|minimum reporting group]].

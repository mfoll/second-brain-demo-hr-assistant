# Wednesday 11 March 2026

## Go-live moves to June

The long Steering Group on the first round of testing. The carry-over defect I found in February was the item everyone came for: 312 absence balances for part-time and compressed-hours staff had converted wrongly from hours to days. Mirela wanted to go live on 1 April and correct them by hand; Elspeth would not accept two systems of record for absence at the start of the leave year. Adrian's group chose to move core HR go-live to 29 June and fix the rule properly. The options and decisions are in the [[uat-defect-review-2026-03-11|defect review note]], and the April plan is now replaced ([[talvento-go-live-plan-april-2026|old plan]]).

## My actions

Retest all 312 records with Rashid once the conversion is rewritten, against a hand calculation. The idea behind the fix is in [[compressed-hours|compressed hours]] and [[annual-leave-carry-over|carry-over]].

## What I think

Relief more than disappointment. I put it in writing that evening: [[moving-go-live-to-june-was-right|moving go-live was right]].

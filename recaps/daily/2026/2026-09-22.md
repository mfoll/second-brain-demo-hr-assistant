# Tuesday 22 September 2026

## Research project scope approved

A short call with Harriet and Kasia, and both signed the ethics form in the afternoon. My diploma project may use monthly Deskwell ticket counts by category and aggregate article views, never ticket text, names or ticket-level rows, and no published cell under 10. The repeat-query count is produced inside Deskwell by Leon so I never see who asked twice. Harriet's condition is that the report says plainly what two policy changes in one company can and cannot prove. The agreement is in the [[research-project-scope-approval-2026-09-22|approval note]] and the data in the [[research-dataset-specification|dataset specification]].

This follows last week, when Kasia turned down my first proposal to code ticket texts and I withdrew it ([[research-project-proposal-v1|first proposal]]).

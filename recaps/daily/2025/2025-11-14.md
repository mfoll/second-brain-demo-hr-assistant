# Friday 14 November 2025

## The chatbot stops

At the team meeting Leon stopped the chatbot trial, with Deborah's agreement. The test run in October had settled it: the bot got well under half of our test questions fully right, and most failures traced back to old or contradictory articles rather than the tool. A paid extension and a cut-down bot for a handful of safe questions were both discussed and dropped. The [[chatbot-trial-stopped-2025-11-14|meeting note]] has the decision and the [[chatbot-trial-closure-report|closure report]] has Leon's reasoning.

## What replaces it

The [[knowledge-base-rebuild|knowledge base rebuild]] starts on Monday. I take the tracker and about a third of the writing. Nobody is to talk about a second chatbot until the rebuild is done ([[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot|open question]]).

## For me

Started my [[chatbot-trial-lessons-learned|lessons learned]] list on the way home. Main one: we tested the tool before we tested the content.

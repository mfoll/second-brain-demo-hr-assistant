# June 2026

The month Talvento went live. Almost everything else was survey results and getting evidence together for the hybrid review.

## Talvento

Testing was signed off on 12 June ([[uat-sign-off-2026-06-12|UAT sign-off]]), and the Steering Group on 17 June confirmed the date and set the payroll rule: payroll stays on Staffora until two consecutive clean parallel runs ([[hris-steering-group-2026-06-17|Steering Group note]]). Elspeth had asked for three. Core HR went live on 29 June after a quiet cutover weekend, with double keying into Staffora for payroll from that day. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-06-29|29 June]].

## Engagement survey

The survey closed at the end of May with a higher response and index than 2024. On 10 June the Steering Group set the minimum reporting group at 10 on Kasia's advice, so small depots see only site results; Sanjay dissented. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-06-10|10 June]]. The People Committee endorsed it a week later and set action plans for the end of November ([[engagement-survey-results-to-people-committee-2026-06-17|results to the committee]]).

## Hybrid review

Jun ran the pulse check on hybrid work in the second half of the month, and the [[hybrid-review-meeting-2026-06-24|review meeting]] on 24 June settled the options for July: one fixed Tuesday plus team choice was the recommendation, an extra floor costed but unfunded.

## AI ranking

Bram demonstrated Hireloom's ranking module on 18 June ([[ai-ranking-vendor-demo-2026-06-18|vendor demo]]). Marek asked Kasia for a data protection screening; that is for July.

## Carried into July

Parallel payroll runs, the People Committee on hybrid, survey dashboards for managers, the DPIA screening.

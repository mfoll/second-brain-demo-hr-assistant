# Help desk

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes a help desk as a person or department that gives assistance and information, mostly for technical problems. Larger help desks have someone who manages incoming issues and their queues and assigns each issue to a team that specialises in that kind of problem; queue design depends on the size and structure of the desk.

## Why It Matters

Read at the start of the [[helpdesk-triage-categories]] project to find words for what we did badly: we had no queue manager role, so tickets went to whoever picked them up. The triager role in [[triage-a-deskwell-ticket]] is our version of the queue manager, and each of the 14 categories has one owning team.

## Caveats

Written mainly about IT support; HR adds confidentiality rules (the restricted ER queue) that the article does not cover.

# Why Is Warehouse Training Completion Lagging

Warehousing has been the lowest division for mandatory training all year: 74% in January 2026, 85% in October, against 96% at Head Office ([[learnmoor-completion-report-2026-10-16]]).

## Current Answer

Answered in May 2026 after the kiosk pilot, and still holding:

- Access: most hub operatives have no work email and no PC. Doing e-learning on their own phone uses their own data and time.
- Time: no protected time on shift until the pilot gave 30 minutes a week.
- Churn: peak starters arrive in large groups and sit inside their 30-day window, which counts as incomplete.
- Manual Handling needs a practical, and practical slots are limited by trainers.

It is not reluctance. At Hub West, kiosks plus paid time took completion from 82% to 92% in eight weeks ([[hub-training-kiosk-pilot-results]]).

## What follows

Kiosks for the other hubs in the 2027 bid ([[training-budget-2026]]), practical catch-up after peak ([[training-compliance-review-2026-10-16]]), and simpler visual material for people whose first language is not ours ([[caffaro-2020-tailoring-safety-training-to-migrant-workers]]).

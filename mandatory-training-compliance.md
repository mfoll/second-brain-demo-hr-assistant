# Mandatory Training Compliance

Getting completion of the four mandatory courses in [[learnmoor]] from 81% to 95% across the group. [[yetunde-balogun]] administers Learnmoor; [[ewan-galbraith]] owns the two safety courses; I run the reports and the chasing.

## Outcome

95% completion across the four mandatory courses, measured as in-date completions over employees required to hold each course ([[training-completion-rate]]).

## Current State

- 89% on 2026-10-16, up from 81% on 2026-01-12 ([[learnmoor-completion-report-2026-10-16]]).
- By division: Head Office 96%, Forwarding 94%, Road Freight 90%, Warehousing 85%. Warehousing is lower because of peak starters and because operatives have no work email or PC time ([[why-is-warehouse-training-completion-lagging]]).
- By course: Fire Safety Awareness 93%, Data Protection Essentials 91%, Respect at Work 88%, Manual Handling 85%.
- Since 2026-07-20 completions flow nightly from Learnmoor to Talvento, which ended the monthly CSV upload ([[learnmoor-talvento-feed-go-live-2026-07-20]]).
- The Hub West kiosk pilot lifted Manual Handling there from 79% to 91% in eight weeks ([[hub-training-kiosk-pilot-results]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The four mandatory courses only ([[mandatory-training-catalogue]]). Licences for forklift and other equipment are tracked by Health and Safety, not here. Reports go to managers by team; I never send a list of named non-completers outside the line manager and HRBP for that team.

## Working Context

My early assumption was that reminder emails would do it. They did not: three rounds moved completion by about a point each ([[reminder-emails-do-not-move-training-completion]]). What moved it was managers getting a team list on the first working day of the month, kiosks on the hub floor and paid time on shift.

## Active Workflows

- [[chase-mandatory-training-before-month-end]]: report on the first working day, manager reminders in week 2, HRBP escalation for teams under 80%.
- [[learnmoor-report-quirks]] lists what to correct before trusting a report.

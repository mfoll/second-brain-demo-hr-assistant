# How Should Anchor-Day Attendance Be Reported?

The People Committee on 2026-07-15 agreed quarterly anchor-day attendance reporting to the COO. Nobody defined attendance or the level of detail.

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Open. The draft for September reports Tuesday attendance by team, with teams under 10 combined with others in their department, and team-chosen days by division only. Rosamund Oyelaran's office wants every team shown. Due to Priyanka by 2026-10-30.

## Points in dispute

- **What counts as attendance**: a desk booking, a building badge-in, or either. Bookings overstate it by the no-show rate (about 9%); badge data is held by Facilities.
- **Level**: the COO's office asked for every team. 23 of 78 hybrid teams have fewer than 10 people. [[kasia-wrobel]] advised applying the same minimum group of 10 as the survey ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]], [[minimum-reporting-group]]).
- **Individual data**: must stay with Facilities; the report gets aggregates only.

## My view

Use the survey threshold; it is already explained to staff and it avoids a report that reads like a register of who was in. I said this to Priyanka in writing on 2026-10-16.

# Survey Communications Plan 2026

[[shireen-qadir]]'s plan for the 2026 engagement survey, agreed with me and the Steering Group on 2026-04-20.

## Summary

Four phases: a "why it matters" launch from the CEO, manager toolkits, two reminder waves aimed at the lowest-responding sites, and results with "you said, we did" afterwards ([[you-said-we-did]]).

## Key Details

- Launch on 2026-05-11: email to all with work email; printed slips with QR codes for hub and depot staff, handed out by team leaders at shift briefings.
- Manager toolkit: a one-page briefing, a five-minute script for shift huddles, and the anonymity explanation.
- Daily response rates by site sent to HRBPs only (never below site level), from day three.
- Reminder waves on 2026-05-18 and 2026-05-26, targeted at Road Freight depots and Hub South.
- Drivers: a short message on the in-cab tablet asking them to complete the survey at the depot during their paid break.

## Why It Matters

Response rose from 64% to 71%. Shireen thinks the shift-huddle script did more than the emails; the daily site figures showed jumps the day after huddles at Hub East and Hub West.

## Caveats

Road Freight stayed at 62%. The in-cab message went out late, on 2026-05-20.

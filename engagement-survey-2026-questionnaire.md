# Engagement Survey 2026 Questionnaire

The final questionnaire signed off by the Survey Steering Group on 2026-04-15, as loaded into [[pulsewright]].

## Summary

42 statements on a five-point agreement scale (strongly disagree to strongly agree), grouped in nine sections, plus two optional free-text questions. 34 items are unchanged from 2024 so trends hold; 8 are new, including three on hybrid working for hybrid-eligible staff only.

## Key Details

- Sections: engagement (5 items, the [[engagement-index]]), my manager (6), my team (5), workload and wellbeing (5), learning and growth (4), communication (4), change (4), safety (4), hybrid working (3, routed).
- Free text: "What is one thing that would make Pellmore a better place to work?" and "Anything else you want to tell us?" 3,870 comments received in 2026.
- Demographics come from the HR file, not from questions, to keep the survey short and avoid self-identification. Only department, site, division, grade band, service band and contract type.
- Translated into four languages for hub staff; translations checked by colleagues at Hub East and Hub West.
- Median completion time in testing: 11 minutes.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for comparing 2024 and 2026 ([[engagement-survey-2024-results]]). Keeping 34 items identical was [[jun-takahashi]]'s condition for any trend claim. The hybrid items are the only survey data on hybrid before the June pulse check ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]).

## Caveats

Agreement scales favour agreeing; the index is a favourable percentage, not a mean of scores ([[likert-scale]]). The three hybrid items were answered by about 790 people, so most hybrid cuts are division-level only.

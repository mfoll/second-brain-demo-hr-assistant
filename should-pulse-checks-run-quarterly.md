# Should Pulse Checks Run Quarterly

After the June hybrid pulse check worked well, [[owen-tremaine]] offered a quarterly pulse package and [[rosamund-oyelaran]] asked whether quarterly pulses could feed her anchor-day reporting.

## For

- The June check took two weeks and changed a policy ([[hybrid-pulse-check-june-2026]]).
- Faster feedback than an annual survey.

## Against

- Survey fatigue, especially at hubs where we already push the annual survey hard.
- Every pulse needs the same reporting checks and threshold, which means work for [[jun-takahashi]] and me.
- Attendance should be measured from desk booking data, not asked ([[head-office-desk-occupancy-2026]]).

## Status

Deferred on 2026-09-09 by [[deborah-okwuosa]]: no quarterly pulses before the 2027 annual survey; reconsider afterwards, topic by topic rather than as a package.

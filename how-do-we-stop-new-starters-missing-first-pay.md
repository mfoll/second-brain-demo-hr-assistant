# How Do We Stop New Starters Missing First Pay?

## Question

In peak 2025, 19 direct starters missed their first weekly pay because they were not in the HR system before the weekly payroll cut-off. How do we stop that?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> A starter must be in Talvento, with the new-starter form complete, before the weekly cut-off (Thursday 12:00) of their first week. I check every Wednesday that next week's starters are in; anyone missing is chased that afternoon or moved to the following Monday start.

Agreed at the peak kickoff on 2026-08-03 with [[gemma-ashworth]] and [[dariusz-nowicki]] from Group Payroll, and written into [[run-a-hub-induction-day]].

## Evidence it works

- Zero late entries for direct peak starters up to 2026-10-20 ([[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]]).
- The [[new-starter-data-form]] moved into the Talvento pre-hire portal, so 94% complete it before day one.
- Monthly salaried starters follow the same idea with the 15th as the cut-off ([[payroll-cutoff-calendar]]).

## Still watched

The answer depends on Talvento's pre-hire portal and on payroll still accepting starters until Thursday noon. If payroll cuts over to Talvento ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]), the cut-off times may change; check with Dariusz.

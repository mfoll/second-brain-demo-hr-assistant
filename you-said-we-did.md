# You Said, We Did

The step after survey action planning where each team, and the company, tells people what changed because of what they said.

## Why it matters

If people see nothing happen, they stop answering honestly, or at all. [[shireen-qadir]] considers it the single most important message of the survey cycle, and the [[shen-2025-inclusive-climates-and-employee-voice|research on voice]] points the same way: people use channels they believe work.

## How we do it

- Company level: three or four group changes in a January message from the CEO.
- Team level: managers report back at shift huddles at the 3-month check of their plan ([[department-action-plan-template]]).
- Depot noticeboards: a poster per site with the site's actions.
- Never: anything attributed to a person or a comment.

## Status

First full round planned for January 2027.

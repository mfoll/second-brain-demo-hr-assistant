# Peak Hiring Plan 2026

[[conrad-baptiste]]'s plan for seasonal warehouse hiring, agreed with Marek Zieliński and the four hub managers on 2026-07-27.

## Summary

260 seasonal operatives for peak from 2026-11-23 to mid-January, hired between 2026-08-03 and 2026-11-16, split 60% direct and 40% agency.

## Key Details

- Targets by hub: Hub North 70, Hub East 75, Hub South 60, Hub West 55.
- Direct target 156, agency target 104. The direct share rose from 45% in 2025 to cut agency fees.
- Weekly hiring curve: ramp to about 30 a week by mid-September, then 15 to 20 a week.
- Group assessment sessions at each hub, up to 12 candidates, booked through Hireloom.
- Induction at every hub weekly from 2026-09-07, capped at 40 a session ([[hub-induction-day]]).
- Seasonal contracts, 8-week probation, weekly pay.
- Risk named in the plan: first pay slipping if starters are not in the HR system before the weekly cut-off, after 19 slips in 2025 ([[peak-hiring-2025-review]]).

## Why It Matters

The plan sets the numbers the [[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]] measures against. The 60/40 split is the reason the gap is now mostly a direct-hire gap.

## Caveats

Targets assume the same volume forecast as 2025 plus 4%; Conrad said he might revise them in October and has not.

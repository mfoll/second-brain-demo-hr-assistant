# Team Leaders Need Floor Training, Not E-Learning

## Stance

Warehouse and depot team leaders learn Talvento self-service in a live session at their hub, with a laptop each and their own team on screen. E-learning alone does not work for them.

## Why I think this

- In July, e-learning only: 46 managers trained in a month, many with errors in their first requests (part days as full days, wrong absence type). Since live sessions started in August, 242 more and fewer errors ([[manager-self-service-training-tracker]]).
- Team leaders do not sit at desks. A 25-minute module competes with the shift.
- Confidence matters as much as knowledge. [[dansa-2026-technostress-and-technology-self-efficacy]] is about bank staff, not hubs, but its point that technology self-efficacy supports engagement fits what I see: people who have done it once with help keep doing it.

## What would change my mind

If the error rate for live-trained managers drifts back up after three months, the session is not the answer either, and we need floor-walkers in the first weeks.

## Implications

Hub sessions restart after peak, from 2026-11-30, with the quick guide on the wall ([[manager-self-service-quick-guide]]).

# Refresher Interval

How long a mandatory course completion stays valid before it must be repeated.

## At Pellmore

Fire Safety Awareness 1 year; Data Protection Essentials 2 years; Manual Handling and Respect at Work 3 years ([[mandatory-training-catalogue]]).

## Why it matters for the numbers

Expiry dates cluster. Many people did Manual Handling in a 2023 drive, so a wave expired in 2026 and pulled the rate down during peak. When an interval changes, both [[learnmoor]] and the expiry date sent to [[talvento]] have to change, otherwise the two systems disagree.

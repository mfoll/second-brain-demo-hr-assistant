# Study Support Agreement

The agreement between me and Pellmore for the Level 5 Diploma, signed on 2025-08-28 under the qualifications support policy.

## Summary

Pellmore pays the Guild fees in two instalments and gives five paid study days a year. If I leave within 12 months of finishing, I repay a share on a sliding scale.

## Key Details

- Study days are booked like leave, with two weeks' notice, and not during payroll cut-off week or peak.
- Exam or resubmission fees are mine unless my manager agrees otherwise.
- Work material in assignments needs my manager's agreement.

## Why It Matters

Sets the rhythm of my study year ([[diploma-study-routine]]) and is why [[deborah-okwuosa]] sees my assignment plans.

# Rejection Emails Should Give A Reason Category

## Stance

Every rejection after an interview should tell the candidate which broad category the decision fell into (for example: another candidate had more relevant experience; availability did not match the shifts; the assessment score was below the level needed), chosen from a fixed list by the hiring manager. Not personal feedback, which we cannot give at volume, but more than "unsuccessful on this occasion".

## Why I Think This

- Our lowest candidate survey score is useful feedback, 2.6 out of 5, and a third of comments from unsuccessful candidates are about generic rejections ([[candidate-experience-survey-2025]]).
- Scoring sheets already capture the reason when managers use them ([[structured-interviewing]]).
- It makes managers decide on a reason, which is itself a fairness check.

## What Would Change My Mind

Advice from the HR Advisors that fixed categories create more risk of complaints than they solve, or evidence from a pilot that reason categories increase follow-up queries beyond what we can answer.

## Implications

Propose a pilot with the Head Office vacancies after peak. [[marek-zielinski]] wanted to wait for the survey, which now exists. [[priyanka-desai]] needs to agree the category wording.

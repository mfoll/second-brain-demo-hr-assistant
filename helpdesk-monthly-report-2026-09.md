# Helpdesk Monthly Report 2026-09

The one-page monthly helpdesk report for September 2026 that I prepare for [[leon-adjei]] and [[deborah-okwuosa]] from the [[deskwell]] export. Sent 2026-10-05.

## Summary

- 1,140 tickets (1,120 in September 2025).
- First response within two working days: 91% (target 90%). Median 1.2 working days.
- Satisfaction: 78% satisfied or very satisfied, 412 responses ([[helpdesk-satisfaction-results-2026]]).
- Hybrid and flexible working 9% of tickets, down from 12% in August.
- Onboarding and new starters 129, the highest since the category existed: peak hiring.
- HR systems access 97: managers locked out of Talvento approvals after password resets.
- Knowledge base: 41,300 article views; "still need help?" clicked on 6.1% of views.

## Why It Matters

The September figures quoted in the hybrid umbrella and the rebuild checkpoint. Also the last monthly report before the payroll cutover decision.

## Caveats

Counts only, by category. Satisfaction responses are from about a third of closed tickets.

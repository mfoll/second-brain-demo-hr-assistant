# Repeat Queries Matter More Than First Response

## Stance

Our helpdesk reports lead with first response time, but the better measure of whether we are helping is how often the same kind of question comes back. A two-day reply that does not settle the question just creates the next ticket.

## Scope

The HR helpdesk and knowledge base. First response still matters for pay in payroll week, where a delay costs people money.

## Why I Think This

- First response went from 48% to 91% within target, but ticket volume did not fall ([[helpdesk-monthly-report-2026-09]]).
- In April 2026 satisfaction dropped to 66% while first response stayed above 80%: people got replies that did not answer them ([[helpdesk-satisfaction-results-2026]]).
- The hybrid tickets fell when the FAQ answered the real questions, not when replies got faster.

## What Would Change My Mind

If my [[diploma-research-project]] finds that repeat queries in a category do not fall after an article is rewritten, then content is not the lever I think it is.

## Implications

I want a repeat-query indicator in the monthly report, counted by category only. [[leon-adjei]] is open to it once the self-service measure is settled ([[how-do-we-measure-self-service-resolution]]).

# Deskwell Category List 2024

The 31 Deskwell ticket categories in use until 2025-06-15. Superseded by the 14 in [[helpdesk-ticket-categories]].

## Summary

Included "General enquiry", three overlapping leave categories ("Holiday", "Annual leave", "Leave requests") and nine categories with fewer than five tickets a month, such as "Long service awards" and "Car parking".

## Why It Matters

Needed only to compare ticket counts from before June 2025; my mapping from old to new categories is in the same spreadsheet.

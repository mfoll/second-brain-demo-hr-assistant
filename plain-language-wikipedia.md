# Plain language

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Plain language is writing meant to be understood as quickly, easily and completely as possible, avoiding verbose language and jargon. The article notes that laws in many countries require public agencies to use it, and that the UN disability convention includes plain language in its definition of communication.

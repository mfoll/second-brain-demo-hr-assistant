# Staffora Decommissioning Plan

Fiona's draft plan for retiring Staffora, version 0.4 of 2026-10-14. Not yet approved.

## Summary

1. Payroll cutover (date open).
2. Staffora read-only the day after the first Talvento-fed payroll is paid.
3. Report inventory: replace or retire each report still used (Rashid and me).
4. History: extract to a read-only archive, "subject to decision".
5. Licence ends 2027-03-31; server decommissioned by IT in April 2027.

## Why It Matters

Steps 1 and 4 depend on open questions ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]], [[what-happens-to-staffora-history-after-2027-03-31]]). A January cutover leaves under three months for steps 2 to 4.

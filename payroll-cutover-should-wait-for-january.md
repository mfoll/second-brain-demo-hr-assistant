# Payroll Cutover Should Wait For January

## Stance

If October is clean, I would still cut payroll over in January 2027 rather than November. The decision is the HRIS Steering Group's on 2026-11-04, and I have not argued this in the group; my job there is to present both options fairly.

## Why I think this

- The weekly payroll has never had a full parallel run, only two sample weeks a month. The first Talvento-fed weekly runs would land in peak, with up to 40 new hourly starters a week and the December cut-offs moved for bank holidays ([[year-end-payroll-calendar-2026]]).
- A missed weekly starter is someone without pay for a week. During peak, that is not a theoretical risk ([[enter-new-starters-before-weekly-cutoff]]).
- Two more months of double keying is painful but known. We have a daily check for backdated changes.

## Where Fiona is right

Moving the goalposts after the rule is met is bad practice, and the backdated-change risk is real every month we wait. If Group Payroll can run three full weekly parallel runs in November, my main objection goes away.

## What would change my mind

A clean October run plus full weekly parallel runs in November. Or evidence that double keying errors are rising, not falling.

## Implications

Whatever is decided, peak starters must be in the system before Thursday 12:00, and I want a named fallback if the first Talvento weekly export fails.

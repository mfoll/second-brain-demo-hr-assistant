# Graduate Assessment Centre Pack 2026

The assessor and coordinator pack for both 2026 graduate assessment centres, written by Marek Zieliński with my timetable and logistics sections.

## Summary

Four exercises (group case study, written analysis, presentation, competency interview), each scored by two trained assessors on anchored 1 to 5 scales, followed by a wash-up meeting.

## Key Details

- Six competencies, each assessed in at least two exercises.
- Competency interview: six fixed questions with follow-up prompts, the same for every candidate ([[structured-interviewing]]).
- Assessors score independently before discussing; the wash-up uses the total and the spread, not first impressions.
- Reasonable adjustments are offered in the invitation; requests go to me and are passed to the lead assessor without the reason.
- Online version: same exercises, breakout rooms for the group task, presentation shared on screen.
- Timetable: 08:45 to 16:00, 22 candidates, 8 assessors.

## Why It Matters

The format we compared across the online and in-person centres ([[do-assessment-centres-need-to-be-in-person]]).

## Caveats

Scoring sheets and notes are kept in Hireloom and deleted with the application; nothing from them is copied here.

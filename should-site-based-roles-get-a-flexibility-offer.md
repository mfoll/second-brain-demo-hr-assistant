# Should Site-Based Roles Get a Flexibility Offer?

Raised by [[gabriel-santoro]] for the Employee Forum on 2026-01-21: about 2,950 site-based staff get nothing from the hybrid policy. 52 consultation comments made the fairness point.

## Current Answer

Deferred. The Executive Committee noted it in February and the People Committee in July, and neither took it on. Out of scope for the hybrid policy, which covers hybrid-eligible roles only.

## Ideas on the table

- Shift swaps through Talvento without manager pre-approval ([[hollie-pritchard]] for drivers).
- Rotas published four weeks ahead at hubs ([[sanjay-mehrotra]]).
- Nothing called "hybrid" for roles that cannot be done from home.

## Revisit when

Talvento scheduling features are reviewed in 2027, or the Forum raises it formally again. Not mine to decide; I keep this so the request does not get lost.

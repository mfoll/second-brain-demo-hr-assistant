# Onboard New Hires

Everything between an accepted offer and the end of probation: pre-start checks, getting the starter into the HR system before the payroll cut-off, induction days, first-week logistics, and the probation reminders that go to managers. New people start every week, so this never closes.

## Scope

- Pre-start checks: [[right-to-work-check]], references where the role needs them, and the new-starter form ([[new-starter-data-form]]).
- Entering starters in the HR system: in Staffora until 2026-06-28, in Talvento from 2026-06-29 when core HR went live.
- Induction: office starters on the Monday Head Office induction, hub starters on the [[hub-induction-day]].
- Probation reminders to managers at week 10 and week 22 of a 26-week [[probation]] ([[probation-reminder-schedule]]).
- Not mine: probation outcomes and extensions (managers with the HR Business Partners), equipment (IT), site safety training content ([[ewan-galbraith]]).
- Starters never get Person notes and are never named here.

## Current State

- [[hub-induction-day]] runs weekly at all four hubs during peak, capped at 40 starters a session.
- The [[onboarding-buddy-scheme]] is paused since 2026-09-07 to free trainers; restart planned for January 2027.
- [[onboarding-stage-checklists-2025]] are the working checklists; owners by role are in [[onboarding-checklist-ownership-map]].
- The risk this autumn is the weekly payroll cut-off: a hub starter not in Talvento by Thursday 12:00 of their first week is paid a week late ([[how-do-we-stop-new-starters-missing-first-pay]]).

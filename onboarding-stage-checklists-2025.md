# Onboarding Stage Checklists 2025

The three checklists that replaced the 64-item list on 2025-06-27: pre-start, day one and first 90 days. Signed off by Deborah Okwuosa on 2025-06-25.

## Summary

38 items in total, each owned by a role (hiring manager, People Services, IT, Facilities, the starter). Each checklist is short enough to finish in one sitting.

## Key Details

- **Pre-start (14 items):** offer accepted, [[right-to-work-check]] done, references where required, [[new-starter-data-form]] sent, HR system record, IT account requested, equipment, desk or locker, induction booked, first-week plan from the manager.
- **Day one (11 items):** welcome, ID badge, safety briefing, systems log-in, payroll and pay date explained, mandatory training enrolled, meet the team, buddy introduced (office starters).
- **First 90 days (13 items):** week-one check-in, mandatory training complete, probation mid-point at week 10, final review by week 26, feedback survey.
- Full ownership in [[onboarding-checklist-ownership-map]].

## Why It Matters

The working checklists for [[onboard-new-hires]]. The day-one list became the base of the [[hub-induction-day-running-order]].

## Caveats

Written for office starters first; the hub version drops desk and buddy items. Not yet in Talvento ([[should-the-onboarding-checklist-live-in-talvento]]).

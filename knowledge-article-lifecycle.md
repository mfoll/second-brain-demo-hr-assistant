# Knowledge Article Lifecycle

The stages a knowledge base article moves through, and the two fields that keep it alive: an owner and a review date.

## Stages

1. **Draft**: written from a ticket theme, in the style of the [[knowledge-base-style-guide]].
2. **Owner check**: the policy owner confirms the facts.
3. **Published**: live in [[deskwell]] with owner, review date and "applies to" line.
4. **Review**: by the review date (12 months, or 6 for pay, absence and anything tied to a system change), the owner confirms, edits or retires it.
5. **Retired**: unpublished, redirected to its replacement, kept in the archive for 24 months.

A policy change triggers an unscheduled review of every article that links to the policy.

## Why it matters

The old knowledge base had stages 1 and 3 only. Of 212 articles, 34 had a nameable owner and none had a review date; that is how 61 went out of date and how the chatbot learned the wrong carry-over rule ([[knowledge-base-content-audit-2025]]).

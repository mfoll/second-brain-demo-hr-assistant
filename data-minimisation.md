# Data Minimisation

Collect and keep only the personal data a defined purpose needs, for no longer than it needs it. One of the data protection principles in the GDPR ([[general-data-protection-regulation-wikipedia]], [[data-minimization-wikipedia]]). I use the British spelling in my notes; the Wikipedia article uses the American one.

## What it means in my work

> [!info]
> Before collecting or writing down anything about a person, ask what it is for and whether a count, a category or a pointer to the system that holds it would do instead. Usually it would.

**Candidate data.** Application forms ask only for what the role needs. In 2025 we removed date of birth and "current salary" from the Hireloom form, and the driver form asks about licence categories rather than a copy of the licence at application stage ([[driver-recruitment-requirements]]). Unsuccessful candidates' data is deleted six months after the vacancy closes ([[candidate-data-retention-schedule]]).

**Survey reporting.** Results are only reported for groups of 10 or more respondents, so small depots roll up to site level ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]). The purpose is action planning, which does not need results small enough to point at individuals.

**My own notes.** This vault records cases by category and count and points to the system reference, never to the person ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]). The peak tracker is counts only ([[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]]).

**My diploma project.** Uses only monthly ticket counts by Deskwell category, never ticket text ([[diploma-research-project]]).

**AI tools.** A ranking model trained on past applications uses far more data than "can this person work these shifts", which is one reason I am wary of [[ai-cv-screening-pilot]].

## What it does not mean

It does not mean deleting records we are required to keep (payroll, right-to-work evidence for the period after employment that [[kasia-wrobel]] specifies). It does not mean refusing to collect data a process genuinely needs. The test is purpose, not volume.

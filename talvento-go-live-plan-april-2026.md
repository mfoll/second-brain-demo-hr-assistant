# Talvento Go-Live Plan April 2026

The original plan for a single Talvento core HR go-live on 2026-04-01, version 1.1 of November 2025. Retired after the [[uat-defect-review-2026-03-11]] moved go-live to 2026-06-29; superseded by [[talvento-go-live-cutover-plan]].

## Summary

Two UAT rounds (January to March 2026), migration rehearsals in February and March, cutover weekend 2026-03-27 to 2026-03-29, payroll from Talvento "from the May or June monthly payroll". No parallel run length was specified.

## Why It Matters

Kept for the record of what the programme expected before testing. It had no slack for a conversion defect and left the payroll cutover vague, which is why Elspeth insisted on a separate rule ([[hris-steering-group-2026-06-17]]).

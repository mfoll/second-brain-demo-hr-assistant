# Buddy Scheme Midpoint Survey 2026

Aggregate results of the day-45 survey for starters paired with a buddy between January and April 2026, closed on 2026-05-29.

## Summary

29 of 33 starters responded. 26 said their buddy had made their first weeks easier; the value was highest in weeks two to six.

## Key Details

- "My buddy helped me find my way around systems and processes": 4.4 out of 5.
- "I would have asked these questions anyway": 2.7 (a low score means the buddy got questions that would otherwise have gone unasked).
- Median contacts in the first six weeks: 7.
- Most common topics: systems log-ins, expenses, hybrid days, who to ask.
- Comparison group: office starters from autumn 2025 without a buddy raised 1.9 helpdesk tickets each in their first 90 days; paired starters raised 1.1.

## Why It Matters

The best evidence I have that the scheme saves helpdesk work and helps starters, and why I disagree with the pause ([[pausing-the-buddy-scheme-was-the-wrong-trade-off]]).

## Caveats

Small numbers, self-selected; the ticket comparison is across different seasons and is not a controlled comparison.

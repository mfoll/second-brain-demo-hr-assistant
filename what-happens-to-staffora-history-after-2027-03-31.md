# What Happens To Staffora History After 2027-03-31?

When Staffora's licence ends on 2027-03-31, what happens to the about 9,800 leaver records and the history of current employees that were not migrated to Talvento?

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Open. Three options are on the table and no owner has been named for the decision. Fiona wants a recommendation to the HRIS Steering Group before the end of 2026.

## Options

1. **Migrate leaver records into Talvento's archive store.** Searchable, one system. Costs extra under the [[talvento-statement-of-work]] and moves records that may be past their retention date.
2. **Extract to a read-only archive** (database export plus a set of standard PDF reports per record), kept on a restricted share for the retention period, then deleted on schedule. Cheapest; Rashid could do it.
3. **Extend the Staffora licence for one year in read-only mode.** Buys time. Elspeth likes it for payroll queries about 2026; it does not solve anything.

## What we know

- [[kasia-wrobel]] has said whatever is kept must follow the retention schedule, and records past their retention date should be deleted, not moved ([[data-minimisation]]). Nobody has yet counted how many of the 9,800 are past it.
- Payroll needs pay history for six years; Group Payroll keeps its own copy, so HR may not need to.
- The decommissioning plan assumes option 2 but says "subject to decision" ([[staffora-decommissioning-plan]]).

## What would answer it

A count of leaver records by retention status (Rashid), Kasia's view on option 2, and a costed quote for option 1 from [[mirela-dumitru]].

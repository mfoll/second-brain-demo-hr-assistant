# Agency Supply Agreement Peak 2026

My summary of the statement of work with the staffing agency for peak 2026, from the copy Procurement shared on 2026-08-05. Contact at the agency: [[chiara-lombardi-ross]].

## Summary

The agency supplies seasonal warehouse operatives on request at five working days' notice, runs its own pre-placement checks including right to work, and pays its workers. Pellmore inducts them and manages them on shift.

## Key Details

- Planned volume: up to 104 workers; extra volume above that at the same rate if requested five working days ahead.
- The agency confirms each Monday who starts the following week.
- Pellmore provides induction on the standard [[hub-induction-day]]; agency workers count against the 40 cap.
- No-shows on day one are not charged.
- The agency handles its workers' data; Pellmore receives names and start dates only, for site access and induction.

## Why It Matters

Explains why agency hires are faster (about five working days against eleven for direct) and why they are the likely way to close the remaining gap ([[how-do-we-close-the-peak-hiring-gap]]).

## Caveats

Rates and fees are in the contract held by Procurement and are not recorded here.

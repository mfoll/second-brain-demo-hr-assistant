# Hybrid Consultation Comment Summary

My summary of the 412 free-text comments from the hybrid consultation survey, finished 2026-02-06 and attached to the [[exec-committee-hybrid-paper-2026-02]].

## Summary

Comments from an open question in a short [[pulsewright]] survey to the 1,150 hybrid-eligible staff (December 2025 to January 2026), coded by theme. A comment could carry several themes.

| Theme | Comments |
| --- | --- |
| Desk availability at Head Office | 131 |
| Team choice of office days | 118 |
| Commuting cost and time | 97 |
| Caring responsibilities and school runs | 74 |
| Fairness for site-based colleagues | 52 |
| More office time for learning and new starters | 49 |
| Other (IT kit, parking, meeting rooms) | 63 |

## Why It Matters

The only record of what staff said before the decision. The desk theme predicted the 104% occupancy; the team-choice theme is roughly what the People Committee adopted in July.

## Caveats

- Coded by me alone; [[jun-takahashi]] later found about 30 comments where "team choice" and "flexibility" overlapped. Order of themes unchanged.
- Quotes in the summary were paraphrased and stripped of team, site and role.
- Responders were self-selected.

# Employment discrimination

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes employment discrimination as unlawful treatment at work based on protected characteristics, distinguishing deliberate disparate treatment from practices that look neutral but have a disparate impact on a group. Much of the article is about US law.

## Why It Matters

Read after the DPIA screening meeting, to understand why Kasia asked the vendor for a bias report. The difference between treatment and impact is the point: a ranking model can treat everyone by the same rule and still disadvantage a group ([[ai-cv-screening-pilot]], [[whelpley-2026-selection-tests-and-autistic-applicants]]).

## Caveats

Legal detail is US-centred and not advice; questions of law go to the HR Advisors and the DPO.

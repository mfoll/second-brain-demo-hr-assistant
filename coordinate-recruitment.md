# Coordinate Recruitment

Interview scheduling, candidate communication, offer paperwork and Hireloom hygiene for hiring managers across Road Freight, Warehousing, Forwarding and Head Office. It has no end date: there is always a hiring round open somewhere, and peak and the graduate scheme come round every year.

## Scope

- Booking interviews and assessment centres in [[hireloom]], sending candidate emails from the template set, raising offer letters once the hiring manager and [[marek-zielinski]] have approved the offer.
- Keeping Hireloom clean: closed vacancies archived, candidate records deleted on the retention schedule ([[candidate-data-retention-schedule]]), hiring managers set up with the right access.
- Not mine: deciding who gets hired (hiring managers), pay offers (HR Business Partners and Talent Acquisition), agency contracts ([[conrad-baptiste]] and Procurement). Right-to-work and other pre-start checks sit under [[onboard-new-hires]].
- No candidate is ever named in these notes. Volumes, funnel counts and timings are fine; anything about one person stays in Hireloom ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

# 70/20/10 model (learning and development)

## Summary

The model suggests that people learn roughly 70% from experience, 20% from others and 10% from formal training. The article notes that it rests on a 1996 survey in which nearly 200 executives reported how they believed they had learned.

## Why It Matters

Used in my Unit 4 assignment on learning and development practice ([[level-5-diploma]]). Harriet's comment was that I had treated it as a finding when it is a self-report from executives. It still works as a prompt: the mandatory courses are the "10", and the kiosk pilot showed that what happens on the floor with team leaders is most of the rest ([[hub-training-kiosk-pilot-results]]).

## Caveats

Weak evidence base, as the article itself makes clear. Do not quote the percentages as fact.

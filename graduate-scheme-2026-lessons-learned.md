# Graduate Scheme 2026 Lessons Learned

My notes from the wash-up with Marek and Callum on 2026-04-01, cleaned up as a handover for the 2027 round.

## Keep

- Two assessment centres, first online, final in person ([[do-assessment-centres-need-to-be-in-person]]).
- Two assessors scoring independently before discussion ([[graduate-assessment-centre-pack-2026]]).
- Keep-warm contact between offer and start: no withdrawals after acceptance.
- Current graduates at lunch on the in-person day.

## Change

- Online group exercise: one assessor per breakout room.
- Book a full hour for wash-up.
- Video interview stage: candidates waited up to 12 days for an outcome; aim for 5.
- Check the reasonable-adjustment offer at the online test stage, where most applicants drop out (412 of 1,340 pass); prompted partly by [[whelpley-2026-selection-tests-and-autistic-applicants]].
- Move the talent pool consent question to the rejection email, not the application form ([[how-long-do-we-keep-unsuccessful-candidate-data]]).

## Numbers to beat

Funnel and offer rate in [[graduate-scheme-2026-applications-summary]]; 16 of 18 acceptances.

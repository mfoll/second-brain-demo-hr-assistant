# General Data Protection Regulation

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes the GDPR, the European Union regulation on information privacy in the EU and EEA, which also governs transfers of personal data outside them. Covers its principles (including lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation and storage limitation), data subject rights such as access and erasure, the role of data protection officers and impact assessments, and rules on automated decision-making and profiling.

# Compressed Hours

Working full-time contractual hours in fewer, longer days: at Pellmore usually 37 hours over four days of 9.25 hours, or a nine-day fortnight. About 180 employees work compressed hours, mostly transport planners and Head Office staff; it is separate from hybrid working, though people often ask about the two together.

## What it changes in HR data

- Leave is still an entitlement in days, but a day off is a 9.25-hour day, so balances in hours and days do not convert at 7.5 ([[annual-leave-carry-over]]).
- Bank holidays falling on a non-working day need a balancing adjustment.
- Sickness absence of one day is 9.25 hours for payroll deductions where they apply.

## Why I know about it

71 of the 312 wrongly converted balances in the Talvento migration were compressed-hours staff ([[uat-defect-review-2026-03-11]]). The fix uses each person's contracted daily hours ([[absence-conversion-specification]]). Compressed-hours questions also turn up in the helpdesk: "carry-over for compressed hours" was a recurring ticket theme in spring 2026 ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).

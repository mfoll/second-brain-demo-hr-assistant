# Cost Centre Hierarchy 2026

Finance's cost centre structure for the 2026 financial year, issued by [[beatrix-lowe]] on 2026-01-16.

## Summary

About 310 cost centres in four levels: division, region of hubs or depot group, site, team. Old codes mapped to new in a second tab; 27 old codes retired with no successor.

## Why It Matters

The cleansing could not finish cost centres until this existed: 604 Staffora records had missing or invalid codes ([[hris-data-cleansing]]). Talvento accepts only codes on this list. Mapping errors still caused 11 July parallel run differences ([[parallel-run-review-2026-08-06]]).

# Hiring Manager Guide Hireloom

The six-page guide for hiring managers that I rewrote for the self-scheduling launch, published on 2025-05-09.

## Summary

Covers the manager's four jobs in Hireloom: approve the vacancy, shortlist, publish interview slots each week, and record the outcome with notes in Hireloom.

## Key Details

- Publish at least six slots a week while a vacancy is open; candidates book themselves.
- Interview notes go in Hireloom only, never in email or notebooks ([[candidate-data-retention-schedule]]).
- Use the template outcome emails; do not reject candidates from your own mailbox.
- Use the scoring sheet for each question ([[structured-interviewing]]).
- Who to call: Callum for Road Freight and Forwarding, me for Warehousing and Head Office.

## Why It Matters

Most scheduling problems after go-live were managers who had not read it; it is linked from every new vacancy notification.

Written before Talvento; the section on starter records still mentions Staffora and needs updating.

# Helpdesk Baseline Report 2025-03

The baseline I pulled from [[deskwell]] for March 2025 to start the [[helpdesk-triage-categories]] project.

## Summary

- 1,090 tickets in March 2025 across 31 categories.
- 38% in "General enquiry"; nine categories with fewer than five tickets a month; three overlapping leave categories.
- Median first response 4.6 working days; 48% within two working days.
- Satisfaction 71%.

## Why It Matters

The "before" picture against which the category change and the knowledge base rebuild are measured.

## Caveats

First response measured from ticket creation, including weekends in the old configuration; recalculated in working days by me.

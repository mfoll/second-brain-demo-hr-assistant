# Recruitment Week In Peak

How my week runs from September to mid-November, written down so Callum or Carys can cover if I am off.

| Day | Fixed points |
|---|---|
| Monday | 08:30 hub induction (one hub), 15:00 update [[peak-hiring-tracker-2026]], 17:00 tracker done |
| Tuesday | 09:30 peak review, then offers and Hireloom outcomes |
| Wednesday | 10:00 group assessment sessions (two hubs), 14:00 Talvento check with Aoife for next week's starters |
| Thursday | 12:00 weekly payroll cut-off; morning for chasing missing starters; afternoon Head Office vacancies |
| Friday | 10:00 group assessment sessions (other two hubs), 15:00 Carys check-in, retention and admin |

Rules of thumb:

- Nothing goes past Thursday 12:00 without a Talvento record.
- Never book more than 40 into a session.
- Head Office and helpdesk work gets mornings only if the tracker is done.

# Probation (workplace)

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes workplace probation as a status for new employees and trainees during which a supervisor evaluates their progress and skills, decides appropriate assignments and observes reliability and working relationships. Notes that terms and length vary by employer and country.

## Why It Matters

Read when setting up the [[probation-reminder-schedule]]. My Concept note [[probation]] records how it works at Pellmore: 26 weeks for permanent roles, 8 for seasonal contracts, reviews owned by managers.

## Caveats

General; our probation terms come from the contracts and the HR Advisors, not from this article.

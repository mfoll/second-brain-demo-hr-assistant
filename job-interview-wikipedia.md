# Job interview

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes the job interview as a conversation between an applicant and an employer's representative to decide whether to hire, and one of the most common selection methods. Interviews range from unstructured conversations to structured interviews with fixed questions in a set order; the article reports that research generally finds structured interviews better at predicting who will do well.

## Why It Matters

Read at the start of the [[interview-scheduling-overhaul]]. The scheduling project was only about logistics, but the article helped me argue for the scoring sheets in the [[hiring-manager-guide-hireloom]] and is the background for [[structured-interviewing]].

## Caveats

General and mostly about professional roles; our warehouse first interviews are short group sessions.

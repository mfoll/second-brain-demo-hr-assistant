# Hybrid Policy Consultation

Consulting the Pellmore Employee Forum and hybrid-eligible staff on changes to the hybrid arrangements before the Executive Committee decided. Completed on 2026-02-13 when the comment summary went into the Executive Committee paper.

## Outcome

Employee Forum consulted in three sessions; 412 free-text survey comments summarised for the Executive Committee.

## Current State

> [!success]
> Done. Three Forum sessions held (2025-11-26, 2025-12-17, 2026-01-21) and 412 comments coded and summarised in [[hybrid-consultation-comment-summary]], which went into the [[exec-committee-hybrid-paper-2026-02]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Consultation only; the decision belonged to the Executive Committee. The free-text comments came from an open question in a short Pulsewright survey to the 1,150 hybrid-eligible staff. I coded them into themes; the raw comments stayed in Pulsewright and nobody's comment was quoted with a team or site that could identify them.

## Working Context

[[priyanka-desai]] wrote the [[employee-forum-hybrid-consultation-paper]], which floated three options: two flexible office days; three fixed anchor days; three days chosen by teams. [[gabriel-santoro]] chaired the Forum sessions.

The comment themes, by count (some comments had more than one): desk availability at Head Office (131), wanting team choice of days (118), commuting cost and time (97), caring responsibilities and school runs (74), fairness for site-based staff (52), support for more office time for new starters and learning (49). The case for fixed days came mostly from managers in the last theme.

At the third session Gabriel asked that site-based staff get something too; that became [[should-site-based-roles-get-a-flexibility-offer]], parked.

What I would do differently: code the comments with a second person. I did all 412 alone in nine evenings, and [[jun-takahashi]] later found my "team choice" and "flexibility" codes overlapped on about 30 comments.

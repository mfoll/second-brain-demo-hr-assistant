# The Chatbot Failed on Content, Not Technology

## Stance

The Deskwell Assist trial failed because our knowledge base was wrong, contradictory and incomplete, not because the chatbot was bad at reading it. Fix the content and the same tool would probably pass; skip that and no tool will.

## Scope

Chatbots that answer from our own articles. Not a view on chatbots in general or on any other use of AI in HR ([[ai-in-recruitment]] is a different question).

## Why I Think This

- 86 of the 88 failed answers in the [[chatbot-test-run-2025-10-22]] traced to outdated, contradictory or missing articles; 2 to the tool misreading a correct one.
- The [[knowledge-base-content-audit-2025]] then found 61 outdated and 23 contradictory articles out of 212.
- The confident wrong pay answers repeated what our own articles said.

## What Would Change My Mind

Running the same 150 questions against the rebuilt knowledge base. If the score stays well under 70% with accurate articles, the tool is the problem. That test is cheap and I would like it done before anyone buys a licence.

## Implications

- A retry depends on the rebuild finishing and on one full review cycle ([[should-we-retry-a-helpdesk-chatbot]]).
- The test set is worth keeping current ([[chatbot-test-question-set-2025]]).

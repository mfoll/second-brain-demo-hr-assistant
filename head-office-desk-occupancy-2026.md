# Head Office Desk Occupancy 2026

Monthly desk booking summary for Head Office from Facilities ([[nadia-haddou]]): 520 bookable desks for 690 hybrid-eligible staff based there. Figures are peak-day bookings as a share of desks, building totals only.

## Summary

Under fixed anchor days (April to August), Tuesday to Thursday demand exceeded the number of desks: 104% in April and May, with the overflow working from meeting rooms and the canteen. Since v2 (September), Tuesday stays near full and the other days have room, but Wednesday is filling up.

## Why It Matters

The single strongest piece of evidence in the [[hybrid-policy-review]]: it made the problem physical rather than a matter of preference. It is also the test for [[does-team-choice-fix-the-desk-shortage]].

## Key Details

Average bookings as a share of 520 desks:

| Month | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| March (before anchor days) | 41% | 63% | 66% | 58% | 24% |
| April | 27% | 104% | 104% | 101% | 18% |
| May | 26% | 104% | 103% | 99% | 17% |
| June | 29% | 101% | 99% | 96% | 19% |
| July | 30% | 98% | 96% | 92% | 21% |
| September | 52% | 96% | 74% | 63% | 33% |

- Over 100% means bookings exceeded desks: the tool allowed "overflow" bookings to meeting rooms from 2026-04-07.
- No-shows run at about 9% of bookings, so attendance is lower than bookings.
- By floor (asked for by [[ingrid-salvesen]]), floors 3 and 4 (Finance and Forwarding) were the tightest, at up to 112% on anchor days in April.

## Caveats

- Bookings are not attendance. Nadia states this on every report.
- July and August are holiday months; August is left out because of summer leave.
- Building totals only; no individual booking data leaves Facilities.

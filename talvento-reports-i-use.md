# Talvento Reports I Use

The Talvento reports I run, how often and for what. All aggregate unless the report is for a named system task.

- **Starters this week** (daily during peak): pending new starters from Hireloom not yet complete. Checked against the Thursday cut-off ([[enter-new-starters-before-weekly-cutoff]]).
- **Absence approved after cut-off** (monthly): counts by division. For [[absence-data-reconciliation]].
- **Data quality exceptions** (first Monday): missing cost centres, inactive managers, duplicates ([[hr-data-quality-scorecard]]).
- **Headcount as at a date** (on request): effective-dated, so it answers "how many on 1 April" properly for the first time ([[effective-dating]]).
- **Leavers keyed after last day** (weekly): count only, for the provisioning check.
- **Managers without self-service training** (fortnightly): division counts for the [[manager-self-service-training-tracker]].

[[jun-takahashi]] builds anything more complicated. Reports from Staffora that still have no equivalent are on Rashid's list.

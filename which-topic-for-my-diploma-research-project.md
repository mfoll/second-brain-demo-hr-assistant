# Which Topic For My Diploma Research Project

From June to early September 2026 I had three candidate topics for Unit 7.

## Candidates

1. Whether hub induction days improve first-month retention of peak starters. Rejected: retention by start cohort gets close to identifying people at small sites, and peak starts in August, too late to analyse.
2. What managers do with survey results below and above the reporting threshold. Rejected: I am too close to the Steering Group and it would mean interviewing colleagues about a live disagreement.
3. Whether the rebuilt knowledge base reduces repeat queries after a policy change. Chosen.

## Current Answer

Topic 3, approved on 2026-09-22 ([[research-project-scope-approval-2026-09-22]]). It uses data that already exists in aggregate, it matters to the helpdesk, and [[leon-adjei]] wanted the answer anyway. [[amina-yusuf]]'s advice helped: pick the topic where someone at work will use the result.

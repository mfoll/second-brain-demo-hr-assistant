# Ticket Triage

Deciding, for each incoming ticket, what it is about, how urgent it is and who should answer it, before anyone tries to answer it.

## In our helpdesk

- **Category**: one of the 14 ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]). Category drives the queue through the [[deskwell-routing-rules]].
- **Urgency**: pay in payroll week, a new starter not set up before their first day, anything about someone's safety or wellbeing at work (straight to the HR Business Partner the same day).
- **Tier**: can the helpdesk answer it, or does it go to an advisor or HR Business Partner ([[tiered-hr-service-model]])?
- **Sensitivity**: anything that looks like employee relations or health goes to the restricted queue or the HR Business Partner without being discussed in the shared queue.

## Why it matters

Before 2025, 38% of tickets were "General enquiry" and nobody triaged; whoever opened a ticket answered it. Triage is what made the two-day first response achievable ([[first-response-time]]).

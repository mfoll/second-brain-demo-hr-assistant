# What First-Response Target Should the Helpdesk Use?

## Current Answer

Two working days for every category, met for at least 90% of tickets. Decided by [[leon-adjei]] in May 2025 and in force since 2025-06-16.

## Options considered

- One working day for everything: not achievable with three part-time triagers; we were at 4.6 days median.
- Different targets per category: I proposed one day for pay in payroll week. Leon preferred one target for simplicity; pay in payroll week is routed to the top of the queue instead ([[deskwell-routing-rules]]).
- Calling it an SLA: dropped; there is no agreement with employees, so it is a target ([[service-level-agreement-wikipedia]]).

## Since

91% in September 2026. Leon has asked whether to tighten to one day in 2027; my view is that repeat queries are the better next measure ([[repeat-queries-matter-more-than-first-response]]).

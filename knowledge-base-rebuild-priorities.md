# Knowledge Base Rebuild Priorities

The order of work agreed with [[leon-adjei]] on 2025-12-08 for the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]], and how it changed.

## Agreed order

1. **Pay and leave** (January to February 2026): highest ticket volume and the chatbot's worst errors.
2. **Onboarding and leavers** (March to April): steady volume, simple facts.
3. **Policies and ER process** (May to June).
4. **Learning and HR systems** (July to August): timed for Talvento go-live.
5. **Long tail** (autumn).

## What changed

- The Executive Committee decision on 2026-02-24 forced **hybrid** to the front in March; all 12 hybrid articles were written twice, for v1 and v2.
- Talvento go-live moved from April to 2026-06-29 ([[uat-defect-review-2026-03-11]]), so systems articles slipped to July.
- Payroll articles are on hold for the cutover decision on 2026-11-04 ([[when-should-payroll-cut-over-to-talvento]]).

## Rule we kept

Article list by ticket themes, not by policy index. If nobody asks about a policy, it gets a link in the policy library, not an article.

# Payroll

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

General article on payroll: the list of employees entitled to pay, the records of what they were paid and withheld, and the function that manages it, whether in-house or outsourced. Covers pay periods and the basic processing cycle.

## Why It Matters

Background I read in my first months when cut-offs were new to me. Pellmore runs two payroll cycles in-house under Finance, monthly and weekly ([[payroll-cutoff-calendar]]); the article helped me see why Group Payroll guards the cut-off so hard.

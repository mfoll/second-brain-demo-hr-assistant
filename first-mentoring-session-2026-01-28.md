# First Mentoring Session 2026-01-28

First one-hour online session with [[amina-yusuf]] through the [[guild-mentoring-scheme-guide|Guild mentoring scheme]].

## What we agreed

- Every six weeks, an hour, I set the agenda.
- Goals for the year: get through the diploma without burning out; learn to disagree well at work; decide what role to aim for after the diploma.
- Ground rule from both of us: no colleagues, no cases, no Pellmore documents.

## What stuck

Amina asked what I do when I disagree with a decision. Honest answer: I say nothing in the meeting and write it in my notes. She did not say that was wrong, but asked what the right channel would be for each kind of decision. Running theme since: [[mentoring-themes-with-amina]].

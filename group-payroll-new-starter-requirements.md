# Group Payroll New Starter Requirements

Gemma's list of what payroll needs for a new starter to be paid in their first run, updated for Talvento on 2026-08-24.

## Summary

Mandatory before cut-off: legal name, start date, contract type and hours, working pattern, pay rate reference, cost centre, bank details, tax information form, right to work checked flag. Weekly starters also need their depot or hub and shift pattern. Agency-to-direct transfers need the agency end date.

## Why It Matters

Missing bank details was the second biggest cause of late changes in 2025 ([[payroll-late-changes-log]]). During peak this list is the checklist for every Thursday ([[enter-new-starters-before-weekly-cutoff]]). Data comes partly from [[hireloom]] through the handoff, partly from the starter in employee self-service.

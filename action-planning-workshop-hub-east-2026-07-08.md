# Action Planning Workshop Hub East 2026-07-08

A 90-minute session at Hub East, hosted by [[maeve-donnelly]], to test the roll-up version of the [[department-action-plan-template]] with team leaders from three small depots that fall below the reporting threshold.

## What happened

- Eleven team leaders and managers attended; [[sanjay-mehrotra]] co-ran it with me.
- We read the site-level results together and asked "which of these sound like us?" Each depot claimed two or three findings and rejected at least one.
- All three depots left with a draft plan; two were agreed within a week.

## What I took from it

- Site results are usable if a team gets to argue with them. A pack posted by email does not do that.
- Team leaders wanted to know what other depots had chosen, which is fine at the level of themes.
- This is the main evidence for my answer so far to [[how-can-small-depots-act-on-site-level-results]], and Sanjay agreed it worked better than he expected, while still wanting 5.

# Executive Committee Hybrid Decision 2026-02-24

The Executive Committee decided that hybrid-eligible staff, about 1,150 roles, work three fixed anchor days in the office, Tuesday to Thursday, from 2026-04-01. I was not in the room; this is from the minute extract [[priyanka-desai]] shared on 2026-02-25 and her debrief.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Decision: three fixed anchor days, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, for all hybrid-eligible roles from 2026-04-01, with a review after three months. Sponsor: Rosamund Oyelaran (COO). Option chosen over two flexible days and three team-chosen days.

## What was decided

- Three office days a week, all fixed: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. Monday and Friday may be worked from home.
- Applies to the roles listed as hybrid-eligible by job family; site-based roles unchanged.
- Start 2026-04-01; People Services to publish policy and guidance by then ([[hybrid-policy-guidance]]).
- Review after three months, reported to the People Committee ([[hybrid-policy-review]]).

## Options in the paper

From the [[exec-committee-hybrid-paper-2026-02]], drafted by Priyanka with my consultation summary attached:

1. Two flexible office days a week, chosen by the employee. Closest to the 2024 arrangement; rejected as doing nothing for collaboration.
2. Three fixed anchor days, Tuesday to Thursday. Sponsored by [[rosamund-oyelaran]]: everyone in on the same days for meetings, learning and new starters.
3. Three days chosen by each team. Most popular in the consultation (118 comments asked for team choice); rejected as harder to manage and to report on.

## Concerns raised and how they were handled

- Desk capacity: the paper said Head Office had 520 desks for 690 hybrid-eligible staff and that about 70% would be in on any anchor day, about 480 people. Facilities flagged that the margin was thin; the committee accepted it and asked for monitoring. It reached 104% ([[head-office-desk-occupancy-2026]]).
- Caring responsibilities: left to the exceptions route through HR Business Partners.
- Forum view: the paper reported the consultation themes fairly, including the preference for team choice ([[hybrid-consultation-comment-summary]]).

## What it meant for me

Five weeks to write the FAQ, update the knowledge base and get the helpdesk ready. My own view, written the same week, is in [[fixed-anchor-days-were-the-wrong-tool]].

# Knowledge management

Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Knowledge management covers the processes by which an organisation becomes aware of, shares, uses and develops what it knows in support of its goals, spanning learning, collaboration and innovation.

## Why It Matters

Read in the first week of the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]] while drafting the plan. Mostly too broad for us, but it pushed me to treat the knowledge base as a process with owners and review rather than a set of pages, which became the owner and review-date rule ([[knowledge-article-lifecycle]]).

## Caveats

Very general; the article is about organisational knowledge in the widest sense, not self-service help content.

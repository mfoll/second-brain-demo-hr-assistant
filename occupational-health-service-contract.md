# Occupational Health Service Contract

My summary of the service schedule in Pellmore's contract with Calderline Occupational Health, made for the knowledge base articles on referrals. Contract owner: [[deborah-okwuosa]]; account lead: [[sunita-ramaswamy]].

## Summary

- Term 2024-04-01 to 2027-03-31, renewable once for two years.
- Services: management referrals, pre-placement health questionnaires for safety-critical roles (drivers, forklift operatives), health surveillance for workshop staff, and a telephone advice line for managers.
- Referrals made by managers or HR Business Partners through Calderline's portal, never by email.
- Service levels: first appointment offered within 5 working days of a complete referral; report to the referrer within 2 working days of the appointment.
- Quarterly service report with aggregate figures only.

## Why It Matters

The process facts for the referral articles and the helpdesk's answers to "how does occupational health work?". The service, never any individual referral, is what this vault records ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).

## Caveats

Summary of the service schedule only; pricing omitted.

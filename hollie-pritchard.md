# Hollie Pritchard

HR Business Partner for Pellmore Road Freight (about 1,450 people: drivers, transport planners, workshop).

## Role

Advises Road Freight management on organisation, workforce planning and people issues, and handles escalated cases for the division. Takes tier 3 tickets that the helpdesk and advisors route up ([[tiered-hr-service-model]]).

## How We Work Together

- She sends me patterns, never cases: "drivers at three depots cannot open the payslip article on their phones" became a style guide rule ([[write-knowledge-base-articles-for-site-staff-first]]).
- Fewer than 40 Road Freight roles are hybrid-eligible (transport planners at some depots), so the hybrid policy reached her mainly through the fairness question raised by the Forum ([[should-site-based-roles-get-a-flexibility-offer]]); she supports a shift-swap offer for drivers rather than anything called hybrid.
- Quick on email, slow on documents; I send her the two paragraphs that matter.

## Current Threads

- Knowledge base articles on driver hours and overtime pay, to be checked against Talvento once payroll cuts over.
- Ticket volume from depots during peak.

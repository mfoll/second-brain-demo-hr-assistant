# How Many Ticket Categories Do We Need?

Asked at the start of [[helpdesk-triage-categories]] in April 2025, when [[deskwell]] had 31 categories.

## Current Answer

Fourteen, one per owning team or distinct route, agreed at the [[helpdesk-category-workshop-2025-04-23]] and live since 2025-06-16 ([[helpdesk-ticket-categories]]).

## Reasoning

- Fewer than about 12 merged things that go to different people (pay and pensions; contracts and ER).
- More than about 16 brought back categories with a handful of tickets a month, which triagers forget.
- A category earns its place if it has its own owner, its own route, or a reporting need (hybrid, for the policy review).

## Revisit if

A new route appears (for example, if payroll queries move to a Talvento self-service form) or a category stays under 20 tickets a month for a year. Family leave is close to that line (29 in September 2026), but it has a distinct owner.

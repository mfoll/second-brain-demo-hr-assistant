# Saturday Induction Trainer Availability

What I found out after the [[peak-hiring-weekly-review-2026-10-20|peak review on 20 October]], where a Saturday induction on 2026-11-14 was suggested for the last peak intake ([[should-induction-run-on-saturdays-during-peak]]).

## Trainers

- I asked the trainers who cover weekly inductions. Three would work a Saturday if it is paid as overtime and agreed by Friday 30 October; the others said no or have not answered.
- Two trainers are enough for one session of up to 40 starters (two safety walk-round groups of 20), so one Saturday session is possible at one hub, not at two.
- Overtime is a cost for Warehousing, not People Services. [[conrad-baptiste]] has to agree it, probably with [[theo-varkey]] looking at the number.

## Site

- Hub East is the only hub with a site manager on shift every Saturday. [[maeve-donnelly]] said yes in principle.
- Hub West's room is booked for the peak briefing on 9 November, which is a separate problem.

## Payroll

A starter inducted on Saturday 14 November begins work on Monday 16 November, the last start date. The weekly cut-off for that week is Thursday 19 November at 12:00, so they need to be complete in [[talvento]] by then ([[enter-new-starters-before-weekly-cutoff]]).

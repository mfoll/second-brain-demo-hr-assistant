# Manager Self-Service Quick Guide

Two-page guide for line managers, written by me and [[shireen-qadir]]'s team in July 2026, version 2 of 2026-08-10. Laminated copies at every hub and depot office; also a Deskwell knowledge base article.

## Summary

Page one: approve or decline absence, record sickness (first day and return), the difference between a part day and a full day, what happens after the payroll cut-off. Page two: report a leaver (last day first), change hours, change a team member's manager, who to call.

## Why It Matters

Version 2 added the part-day example and the "last day first" box after the [[leaver-access-incident-2026-07-09]]. "How do I approve" tickets fell from 58 in July to 19 in September ([[manager-self-service-rollout]]). It is published in [[deskwell]] with me as owner and a review date of 2027-01-31.

# The Trickle-Down Effects of Supervisor Regulatory Foci on Newcomer Task Performance.

Zhao J, Chao W, Zhang H, Zhao G, Wang M. Behavioral Sciences (Basel), 2025.

## Summary

A two-wave questionnaire study of 253 matched supervisor and new employee pairs. Supervisors oriented towards gains and growth ("promotion focus") gave more developmental feedback to newcomers; supervisors oriented towards avoiding mistakes ("prevention focus") gave less. Developmental feedback was in turn linked to better newcomer task performance.

## Why It Matters

Found while reading for the buddy scheme in February 2026. It is about managers, not buddies, but it supports a point I made to Deborah: what helps a newcomer most is someone giving them forward-looking feedback early, and a busy or cautious manager may not. A buddy fills part of that gap ([[onboarding-buddy-scheme]]). I also used it in my diploma unit on learning and development.

## Caveats

Survey data, associations rather than causes, in one national context and not in logistics. Read from the abstract. Says nothing about peer buddies directly.

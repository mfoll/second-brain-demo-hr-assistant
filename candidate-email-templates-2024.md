# Candidate Email Templates 2024

> [!warning]
> Retired on 2025-09-29 and replaced by [[candidate-email-templates-2025]]. Kept for comparison and for the rewrite rationale.

The 71 Hireloom templates in use when I joined, accumulated since 2022. 23 were near-duplicates created by individual managers; 9 still referred to the pre-2023 company name of the Forwarding division; several rejections opened with three sentences of thanks before saying no. The oldest acknowledgement template promised "a response within 48 hours", which we met for about half of applications. What we changed and why is in [[candidate-email-tone-rewrite-notes]].

# Aggregate Data Is Enough For My Research

## Stance

For my diploma project, monthly counts by category answer most of the question that ticket texts would have answered, at a fraction of the risk. Starting from counts and asking what text would add is better than starting from text.

## Scope

My Unit 7 project and similar workplace research by someone inside HR. Not research by an independent researcher with consent from participants, where reading text may be justified.

## Why I Think This

- [[kasia-wrobel]] was right that redacted hybrid tickets still identify people through caring arrangements, health and team details ([[research-project-proposal-v1]]).
- The question is about volumes and repeats, which counts measure directly ([[repeat-query]]).
- I can talk about the project at work freely, which I could not with ticket texts.
- It is the same principle as [[data-minimisation]] and our [[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data|case rule]].

## What Would Change My Mind

If the counts show something I cannot interpret at all without knowing what people asked, for example a category spike with no policy change behind it. Even then the first step would be Leon checking inside Deskwell, not me reading tickets.

## Implications

I lose the "why" behind repeats, and the report will say so.

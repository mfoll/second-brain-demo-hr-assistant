# Onboarding

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Describes onboarding, also called organisational socialisation or, outside American English, induction, as the process by which new employees gain the knowledge, skills and behaviours to become effective members of an organisation. It covers newcomer characteristics, the tactics organisations use, and the usual outcomes studied: role clarity, self-efficacy, social acceptance and knowledge of the culture.

## Why It Matters

Read in my first week on the [[onboarding-checklist-redesign]]. The idea that onboarding is more than paperwork, and that newcomers need role clarity and social acceptance as well as forms, is why the stage checklists have a first-90-days list and why we later started the [[onboarding-buddy-scheme]].

## Caveats

General and US-leaning; the article says nothing about warehouse or shift work, where most of our starters are.

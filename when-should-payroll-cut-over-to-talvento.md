# When Should Payroll Cut Over To Talvento?

When does Pellmore's payroll stop being fed from Staffora and start being fed from Talvento: the November 2026 monthly payroll, or January 2027?

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Not decided. The HRIS Steering Group decides on 2026-11-04. The rule set on 2026-06-17 is two consecutive clean parallel runs. July had 37 unexplained differences, August 9, September 0; the October run closes on 2026-10-28. If October is clean, Fiona McBride wants to cut over for the November monthly payroll (paid 2026-11-26). Elspeth Harcourt prefers January 2027.

## Why It Matters

Until cutover every change to an employee's record is keyed twice, once in [[talvento]] and once in [[staffora]]. Double keying is the main cause of the remaining differences (backdated changes keyed in one system only), it costs People Services about a day a week, and Staffora's licence ends on 2027-03-31. On the other side, a payroll failure pays 4,100 people wrongly, and December is the busiest month for the weekly payroll.

## The two positions

**November (Fiona McBride, programme manager).** Two clean runs is the agreed rule; moving the goalposts after meeting it undermines the rule. Every month of double keying adds risk of the very differences we have been chasing. November leaves December and January to fix anything before Staffora's licence ends, and the implementation partner's support runs to cutover plus one month ([[talvento-statement-of-work]]).

**January 2027 (Elspeth Harcourt, Group Payroll Manager).** Year-end processing and the December weekly peak (about 2,600 hourly staff plus up to 260 seasonal operatives, with bank holiday cut-offs moved) should run on one system from start to finish. A November cutover means the first Talvento-fed weekly payrolls land in peak. She also points out that only the monthly payroll has had a full parallel run; the weekly payroll has had samples.

## What would answer it

1. The October run result (2026-10-28 plus tracing, by 2026-10-30).
2. Whether Group Payroll can run a full weekly parallel run for three weeks in November, which would answer Elspeth's weekly point.
3. The Steering Group decision on 2026-11-04.

## My view

I lean towards January, with medium confidence, for the weekly payroll reason: [[payroll-cutover-should-wait-for-january]]. It is not my decision and I have not argued it in the Steering Group; my job is to make sure the evidence in the paper is complete for both options.

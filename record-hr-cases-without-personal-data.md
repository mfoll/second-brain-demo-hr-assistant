# Record HR Cases Without Personal Data

How I write about anything involving an individual employee or candidate in this vault, in team documents and in knowledge base drafts. Agreed with [[kasia-wrobel]] (DPO) on 2025-03-19 after a helpdesk note with a colleague's sickness details was pasted into a shared team document ([[dpo-case-notes-agreement-2025-03-19]]). It applies to every area of my work, and to anyone or any agent writing in this vault.

## Rule

> [!danger]
> Never record a named or identifiable employee's or candidate's personal, medical, disciplinary, grievance or pay details. Describe the case by category and count, and point to the system that holds it.

## Steps

1. **Ask whether the note needs the case at all.** Most of the time what I need is the pattern ("three tickets this week about carry-over for compressed hours"), not the case.
2. **Describe by category.** Use the Deskwell ticket category, the process stage or the type of issue: "a right-to-work query for an agency worker at Hub West", not who.
3. **Count, do not list.** Write "4 starters" rather than four descriptions. If a count is below 10 and the group is small enough to identify someone (one depot, one team), roll it up or leave it out, following the survey threshold ([[survey-reporting-threshold-decision-2026-06-10]]).
4. **Point to the system.** If I need to find the case again, write the system and a placeholder reference: "Talvento case HR-2026-xxxx", "Staffora case reference", "Deskwell ticket". The real reference stays in the system, which has access control and retention; my notes do not.
5. **Never write names, health information, pay, ratings, disciplinary or grievance details, dates of birth or addresses**, even in a draft, even "temporarily".
6. **Colleagues in their work role are fine.** Person notes record role, ownership and how we work together. No health, family or appraisal content.
7. **Check before saving or sharing.** Read the note once for anything that would let a colleague guess who it is about (a rare role plus a site plus a month can be enough).

## Examples

- Good: "Two queries in September about first pay for peak starters at Hub North; both resolved by the Wednesday Talvento check. See Deskwell, category Pay and Payslips."
- Not allowed: anything naming the starter, their pay rate or why they were absent.
- Good: "Employee relations workload: 14 open cases across the group, owned by Tomás" (count only, no case content).

## When a slip happens

1. Remove the detail at once from the note or document.
2. In a Git-tracked vault, tell Kasia; she decides whether the history needs rewriting.
3. Record only that a correction happened, without repeating the detail.

## Verification

At each monthly vault review, search new notes for patterns that suggest a slip: words like "sick", "disciplinary", "salary", "grievance", "diagnosis", and personal names next to them. None found since March 2025.

# Chatbot Trial Kick-Off 2025-06-02

Start of the six-month Deskwell Assist trial. Call with the Deskwell account team, [[leon-adjei]], me and [[kasia-wrobel]] for the data protection points.

## Agreed

- Internal only: the bot is visible to People Services staff, not to employees, until it passes a test ([[deskwell-assist-trial-terms]]).
- It answers only from published knowledge base articles (then 212) and cites the article it used.
- No ticket history, no employee data. Kasia's condition, and the vendor confirmed the trial configuration cannot read tickets ([[could-the-chatbot-use-ticket-history]]).
- Success bar set by Leon: at least 70% of a test set answered correctly and completely, and no confidently wrong answers on pay.

## My actions

- Build a test set of about 150 questions from ticket themes, with answers signed off by article owners ([[chatbot-test-question-set-2025]]).
- Weekly log of what staff try and what goes wrong.

# People Services Team Charter

One-page charter agreed by People Services at the away day on 2026-01-15 and published by [[deborah-okwuosa]] on 2026-01-20.

## Summary

- One owner per piece of work, written down; "who has this?" answered in the Monday huddle.
- Handovers in writing in the ticket or tracker, never only in chat.
- No personal details of colleagues or candidates in shared documents, chats or notes; point to the system reference ([[record-hr-cases-without-personal-data]]).
- Helpdesk rota is protected time; swaps agreed with [[leon-adjei]] a day ahead.
- Meeting-free Friday mornings for focused work.
- Reply to colleagues within one working day, even if only to say when.

## Why It Matters

The team's own rules. My reading of how they work in practice is in [[people-services-ways-of-working]].

## Caveats

Reviewed yearly; next review at the January 2027 away day.

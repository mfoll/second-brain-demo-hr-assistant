# DPIA Screening Questions

The screening questions [[kasia-wrobel]] uses to decide whether a new process needs a full data protection impact assessment, with her answers for the Hireloom AI ranking module, as sent to Marek and me on 2026-07-09.

## Summary

Nine screening questions. Any "yes" to profiling or evaluating people, systematic monitoring, or new technology on a large scale triggers a full DPIA. The ranking module answered "yes" to three, so a full DPIA is required.

## Key Details

Triggers that applied:

- Evaluation or scoring of individuals: yes.
- New technology used on a large scale: yes (about 6,000 applications a year for operative roles).
- Decisions with significant effect on individuals (access to employment): yes, even with a human reviewing.

Information Kasia asked us to get from the vendor before the DPIA starts:

1. What data the model was trained on, and whether our data would train their models.
2. How "top factors" are produced and whether they can be shown to a candidate.
3. The bias report by gender, age band and disability, for our role family.
4. Whether ranking can be switched off for an individual candidate who objects.
5. Where the data is processed and for how long scores are kept.

## Why It Matters

The checklist for the next step of [[ai-cv-screening-pilot]]. Bram owes answers to the five vendor questions.

## Caveats

Kasia's answers are a screening view, not the DPIA itself.

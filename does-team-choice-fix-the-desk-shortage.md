# Does Team Choice Fix the Desk Shortage?

v2 assumed that letting teams choose two of their three days would spread demand across the week and bring Head Office under its 520 desks.

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Partly, after one month. September: Tuesday 96%, Wednesday 74%, Thursday 63%, Monday 52%, Friday 33%. No day over 100%, but Wednesday is the most popular team choice (52 of 78 teams) and two floors were over 95% on Wednesdays.

## What to watch

- October and November occupancy from [[nadia-haddou]]; peak season brings more Head Office project staff in.
- Whether floor-level caps hold: teams are supposed to pick days that keep each floor under 95%.
- Whether the six-monthly review of team days (March 2027) moves teams off Wednesday.

- [[hot-desking-wikipedia]]: shared desks only work with spread attendance.

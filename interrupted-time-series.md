# Interrupted time series

## Summary

Interrupted time series analysis tracks a measure over a long period before and after an intervention, and judges the intervention's effect by changes in the level and the slope of the series. It is a quasi-experimental design, used when a randomised comparison is not possible.

## Why It Matters

The closest named method to what my [[diploma-research-project]] does with monthly ticket counts around the two hybrid policy changes. It gave me the two things to look for, a jump and a change in trend, and the vocabulary for the method section of the [[research-project-proposal]]. [[jun-takahashi]] warned that 25 monthly points and two interruptions close together are thin for formal modelling; I will present it as descriptive with a segmented trend.

## Caveats

The design assumes nothing else changed at the same time, which is exactly what I cannot assume ([[can-aggregate-ticket-counts-show-a-knowledge-base-effect]]).

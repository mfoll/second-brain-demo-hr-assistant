# Data governance

English Wikipedia article.

## Summary

Explains that data governance is used at two levels: internationally, as part of internet governance, and within organisations, as the management of data through roles, policies and decision rights over the data life cycle.

## Why It Matters

The organisational sense is what is missing at Pellmore after the Talvento programme ends: nobody has decision rights over HR data quality ([[who-owns-hr-data-quality-after-go-live]]). One option is turning the HRIS Steering Group into a data governance group.

# Diploma Research Project

Unit 7 of the [[level-5-diploma]]: a workplace research report asking whether the rebuilt Deskwell knowledge base reduces repeat queries after a policy change, using the hybrid policy as the case.

## Outcome

Report of up to 6,000 words submitted to the Guild by 2027-02-26, built only on monthly ticket counts by Deskwell category.

## Current State

- Scope approved on 2026-09-22 by tutor [[harriet-osei]] and [[kasia-wrobel]] (DPO) ([[research-project-scope-approval-2026-09-22]]). [[deborah-okwuosa]] and [[leon-adjei]] agreed to the data extract.
- Data: monthly ticket counts by the 14 categories in [[helpdesk-ticket-categories]], January 2025 to January 2027. No ticket text, no names, no ticket-level rows ([[research-dataset-specification]]).
- The case has two natural break points: hybrid v1 on 2026-04-01 (hybrid tickets 4% before, 23% in April) and v2 on 2026-09-01 (9% in September). Hybrid articles were live for both changes, but the v1 set was drafted in five weeks from consultation themes, before anyone had asked a question; the v2 set was rewritten in August from the April ticket themes ([[knowledge-base-rebuild]]).
- The first proposal wanted to code 200 ticket texts; Kasia said no, and she was right ([[research-project-proposal-v1]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Aggregate counts only, for the whole group. No category-month cell under 10 is published in the report; small cells are merged with the next month or category. Nothing from Deskwell leaves Pellmore except the final aggregate tables in the report. The Guild ethics form is filed ([[research-ethics-form-2026-09]]).

## Working Context

- Method: compare the hybrid category's share of tickets before and after each policy change, and ask whether the second spike was smaller and shorter. A simple version of an [[interrupted-time-series]] with only 25 monthly points, so the report will be careful about what it can claim.
- The main confounder: v2 was a smaller change than v1, so a smaller spike would happen even without better articles. I will say so, and use the pulse check and the FAQ views in Deskwell as supporting context, not proof.
- What counts as a repeat query: [[repeat-query]].

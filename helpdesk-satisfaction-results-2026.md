# Helpdesk Satisfaction Results 2026

Monthly results of the one-question satisfaction survey sent by [[deskwell]] when a ticket closes ("How satisfied are you with how your question was handled?", five-point scale), January to September 2026.

## Summary

Satisfied or very satisfied: 74% in January, 66% in April (hybrid spike), back to 78% in September, against 71% in March 2025 before the category change. Response rate steady at about a third of closed tickets.

## Why It Matters

Satisfaction dipped with the April hybrid spike and recovered with FAQ fixes, which supports the case that clear content matters more than speed ([[repeat-queries-matter-more-than-first-response]]).

## Caveats

Self-selected respondents. Only reported for the whole helpdesk and by category, never by site where a group is under 10 respondents. Free-text comments are not kept in reports.

## Source Location

Deskwell satisfaction report, exported monthly to "Helpdesk reports/Satisfaction".

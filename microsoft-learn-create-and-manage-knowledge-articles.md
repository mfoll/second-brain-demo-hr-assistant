# Create and manage knowledge articles

Microsoft Learn documentation page for knowledge articles in Dynamics 365 Customer Service.

## Summary

Explains how service teams author, edit, search, publish and translate knowledge articles, which can hold solutions to common issues, product documentation, FAQ answers and similar. Each article has a title, keywords for search and a short description shown in search results. A business process flow guides an article through stages named New Process, Author, Review and Publish.

## Why It Matters

Not our tool; [[deskwell]] works differently. I read it while planning the [[knowledge-base-rebuild]] to see how a mature knowledge product separates authoring, review and publishing. We borrowed the idea of explicit stages and a search description for every article ([[publish-or-review-a-knowledge-base-article]]).

## Caveats

Vendor documentation for a different product, describing its own features. Read on 2025-11-24; the page has since been updated.
